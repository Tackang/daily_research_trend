📡 [2026-05-21] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ MetaEarth-MM: Unified Multimodal Remote Sensing Image Generation with Scene-centered Joint Modeling `arXiv:2605.20090`
   - Proposes a foundation model unifying cross-modal generation across 5 RS modalities via a decoupled scene-centered latent representation; introduces EarthMM dataset with 2.8M globally distributed aligned image pairs.
   - Why it matters: First all-to-any RS generation foundation model—directly relevant to multi-sensor fusion and representation learning for Landsat/Sentinel.

2. LESSViT: Robust Hyperspectral Representation Learning under Spectral Configuration Shift `arXiv:2605.18541`
   - Presents a sensor-flexible ViT with LESS attention (low-rank spatial-spectral factorization) and HyperMAE pretraining, enabling robust transfer across hyperspectral sensors with varying band configurations.
   - Why it matters: Sensor-agnostic pretraining tackles the core challenge of building RS foundation models transferable across heterogeneous satellite sensors.

3. SkyNative: A Native Multimodal Framework for Remote Sensing Visual Evidence Reasoning `arXiv:2605.17949`
   - Introduces an encoder-free VLM for RS that feeds raw satellite image patches directly into language model token space, reducing spatial over-compression and improving grounded visual reasoning.
   - Why it matters: Introduces a diagnostic benchmark (progressive visual degradation + misleading prompts) to measure genuine image-grounded reasoning in RS VLMs.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

4. How Far Are We From True Auto-Research? `arXiv:2605.19156`
   - Benchmarks Claude Code, Codex, and Kimi Code on 13 CS research tasks via ResearchArena; finds experimental rigor (fabricated results, underpowered experiments) to be the key bottleneck—none of 117 generated papers met top-venue standards.
   - Why it matters: First artifact-aware evaluation of autonomous research agents reveals LLMs can write papers but cannot yet do real science.

5. Toto 2.0: Time Series Forecasting Enters the Scaling Era `arXiv:2605.20119`
   - Demonstrates consistent scaling laws (4M→2.5B params) for time series foundation models with five open-weights models achieving SoTA on BOOM, GIFT-Eval, and TIME benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: Scaling laws now proven for TS forecasting—paves the way for foundation-model-level temporal modeling of RS and climate data.

💡 One-line comment: Multi-modal EO foundation models are converging on scene-centered architectures—MetaEarth-MM signals the shift from single-modality pretraining to universal cross-modal generation.
