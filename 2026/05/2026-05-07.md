📡 [2026-05-07] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ DINO Soars: DINOv3 for Open-Vocabulary Semantic Segmentation of Remote Sensing Imagery `arXiv:2605.03175`
   - Applies the DINOv3 vision foundation model to RS semantic segmentation without domain-specific pretraining, using open-vocabulary queries across diverse satellite scenes.
   - Shows strong zero-shot transfer from natural-image FMs to RS, questioning whether domain-specific RS pretraining is always necessary.
   - Why it matters: Directly challenges the premise of building RS-specific foundation models (e.g., Landsat FM); transfer from general FMs may be more powerful than assumed.

2. Sentinel2Cap: A Human-Annotated Benchmark for Multimodal RS Image Captioning `arXiv:2605.03189`
   - Introduces a human-validated dataset pairing Sentinel-2 imagery with natural language captions across multiple spectral modalities.
   - Enables rigorous evaluation of vision-language models on satellite imagery description at scale.
   - Why it matters: First large-scale human-verified captioning benchmark for Sentinel-2; fills a critical gap for multimodal RS model evaluation.

3. deSEO: Physics-Aware Dataset Creation for High-Resolution Satellite Image Shadow Removal `arXiv:2605.03610`
   - Generates geometry-consistent paired shadow/shadow-free satellite images via physics-based radiometric normalization.
   - Provides the first dedicated high-resolution benchmark for satellite shadow removal with rigorous physical constraints.
   - Why it matters: Shadow artifacts degrade LULC and biomass estimation; physically-grounded training data can improve downstream RS tasks.

4. SoDa2: Single-Stage Open-Set Domain Adaptation for Cross-Scene Hyperspectral Classification `arXiv:2605.03371`
   - Decouples spectral and spatial feature alignment in a single-stage framework for cross-scene hyperspectral classification without target labels.
   - Achieves robust generalization to unknown scenes by disentangling spectral appearance from semantic content.
   - Why it matters: Cross-scene generalization is critical for operational RS workflows where training and deployment regions differ.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

5. GeoDecider: Coarse-to-Fine Agentic Workflow for Lithology Classification via LLMs `arXiv:2605.03383`
   - Proposes a coarse-to-fine agentic pipeline where an LLM orchestrates geological data retrieval, multi-step reasoning, and rock-type classification.
   - Outperforms standard ML baselines on lithology mapping without task-specific training data.
   - Why it matters: Demonstrates LLM agents replacing specialized classifiers in geoscience — a template directly applicable to RS land-cover classification.

6. Proteo-R1: Reasoning-Guided Protein Design via Multimodal LLMs and Diffusion `arXiv:2605.02937`
   - Combines chain-of-thought reasoning in a multimodal LLM with diffusion-based geometric generation for de novo protein design.
   - Produces structurally valid proteins with improved functional properties over diffusion-only baselines.
   - Why it matters: Exemplifies the LLM-reasoning + generative model pipeline for scientific discovery, extending well beyond NLP tasks.

7. Agentic-imodels: Autoresearch Loop for Data-Science Tool Optimization `arXiv:2605.03808`
   - Self-improving agent loop that iteratively proposes, evaluates, and refines data-science models for both predictive performance and interpretability.
   - Achieves competitive accuracy with simpler, more interpretable models through fully autonomous experimental cycles.
   - Why it matters: Autonomous scientific discovery loop that could accelerate RS algorithm development without manual tuning.

8. Programmatic Context Augmentation for LLM-Based Symbolic Regression `arXiv:2605.03101`
   - Enables LLMs to analyze data statistics and structure during evolutionary symbolic regression search, grounding equation recovery in data-derived context.
   - Outperforms prior methods on physical law discovery benchmarks by integrating programmatic data analysis at each search step.
   - Why it matters: Opens a path for LLMs to discover physical relationships in geophysical datasets, complementing simulation-based RS models.

💡 One-line comment: Today's papers collectively stress-test whether domain-specific RS pretraining still pays off — general FMs are closing the gap faster than expected.
