🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ FLORO: A Multimodal Foundation Model for Ecological Remote Sensing `arXiv:2605.28174`
   - Masked autoencoding on Sentinel-1, Sentinel-2, SkySAT, UAV, and elevation data; availability-aware inputs handle heterogeneous sensor combinations without retraining.
   - Sets strong results on PANGAEA benchmark for biomass and canopy-height prediction using less pretraining data than prior models.
   - Why it matters: Explicitly targets ecological monitoring across scales with the same masked-autoencoding paradigm as Landsat FM work — direct architectural cousin.

2. AGFlow: Asynchronous RS Time-Series Fusion for Cloud Removal and Anytime Reconstruction `arXiv:2605.27726`
   - Fuses Sentinel-1 SAR and Sentinel-2 optical via spatiotemporal flow matching, enabling reconstruction at any user-specified timestamp without manual temporal alignment.
   - Achieves 16–19% error reduction over baselines on the RESTORE-DiT protocol under persistent cloud cover.
   - Why it matters: Directly solves the cloud-gap bottleneck for continuous optical satellite vegetation and land cover monitoring.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

3. Why LLMs Fail at Causal Discovery and How Interventional Agents Escape `arXiv:2605.27567`
   - Proves a kernel obstruction theorem showing supervised fine-tuning cannot confer causal discovery; proposes A-CBO — a frozen LLM acting as interventional oracle with an external Bayesian loop.
   - Outperforms fine-tuning on 24-variable Extended Corr2Cause, with the gap widening as complexity grows.
   - Why it matters: First formal proof of an LLM reasoning ceiling, plus a provably convergent agentic bypass in logarithmic rounds.

4. Discovery Agents for Real-Time Analytics: Toward Proactive Insight Systems `arXiv:2605.27571`
   - Autonomous multi-agent pipeline (hypothesis → analytics → validation → visualization) over Kafka/Flink data streams using contract-driven typed artifacts for safe dynamic execution.
   - Demonstrates proactive insight discovery across retail, finance, and public-sector data without any user queries.
   - Why it matters: Scalable blueprint for agentic scientific discovery over live observational streams.

5. MolLingo: Molecule-Native Representations for LLM-Powered Scientific Agents `arXiv:2605.27853`
   - Coordinates Literature, Chemist, and Orchestrator agents using BRICS fragment decomposition for chemically meaningful molecule representations anchored to protein docking geometry.
   - 4× docking score improvement over GPT-5.4 and state-of-the-art on TOMG-Bench using the same underlying model.
   - Why it matters: Domain-native representations unlock agent-level scientific design — a generalizable paradigm beyond chemistry.

6. Faster Thermal Profiling of a Lunar Rover with ML-Adapted Finite Difference Model `arXiv:2605.27651`
   - Transfer neural network dynamically determines 3D finite-difference nodalization; 50% accuracy gain over coarse physics models and 3× speedup over high-fidelity simulation.
   - Maintains physical consistency under extreme lunar temperature gradients and radiative heat transfer conditions.
   - Why it matters: Physics-informed ML for fast surrogate simulation — pattern broadly applicable to Earth system and climate modeling.
