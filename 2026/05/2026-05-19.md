📡 [2026-05-19] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ ChronoEarth-492K: A Large Scale and Long Horizon Spatiotemporal Hyperspectral Earth Observation Dataset and Benchmark `arXiv:2605.15666`
   - 492K hyperspectral patches from NASA EO-1 Hyperion (2001–2017), with 28K multi-temporal sequences; unified benchmark covers land cover, crop type, forest dynamics, and soil properties across global sites.
   - Why it matters: First large-scale temporally calibrated hyperspectral SSL dataset — critical pretraining infrastructure for multitemporal EO foundation models like Landsat-based ones.

2. ELDOR: A Dataset and Benchmark for Illegal Gold Mining in the Amazon Rainforest `arXiv:2605.15397`
   - UAV orthomosaic dataset covering 2,500+ hectares with pixel-level semantic labels; benchmarks segmentation and vision-language models on fine-scale environmental disturbance detection.
   - Why it matters: Reveals resolution limits of satellite monitoring for land degradation — motivates multi-scale EO approaches combining UAV and satellite data.

3. Text-RSIR: A Text-Guided Framework for Efficient Remote Sensing Image Transmission and Reconstruction `arXiv:2605.15558`
   - Compresses satellite imagery to ~2% of original bandwidth using low-res images plus compact text descriptions; a text-conditioned model reconstructs spatial detail via cross-modal learning.
   - Why it matters: Demonstrates text as a semantic bottleneck for EO imagery, with implications for efficient on-orbit data downlink.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
4. NOVA: Fundamental Limits of Knowledge Discovery Through AI `arXiv:2605.15219`
   - Proves cumulative AI discovery cost scales as R_cum(D) = Θ(D^α), with a "contamination trap" where false-positive verification rates grow as genuine discoveries become scarce.
   - Why it matters: Theoretical ceiling for autonomous scientific AI — sets realistic scope for research automation agents.

5. SMCEvolve: Principled Scientific Discovery via Sequential Monte Carlo Evolution `arXiv:2605.15308`
   - Reformulates program search as sampling from a reward-tilted distribution via Sequential Monte Carlo; outperforms evolutionary baselines on math, algorithm, symbolic regression, and ML research benchmarks with fewer LLM calls.
   - Why it matters: More efficient, theoretically grounded alternative to random LLM-based search for AI-driven discovery.

6. Agentic Discovery of Neural Architectures: AIRA-Compose and AIRA-Design `arXiv:2605.15871`
   - 11–20 LLM agents autonomously discover Transformer-Mamba hybrid architectures (AIRAhybrids) that outperform Llama 3.2 by 2.4–3.8% at 1B scale within a 24-hour search budget.
   - Why it matters: Concrete demonstration that AI agents can design better foundation model architectures — a step toward recursive self-improvement.

💡 One-line comment: Multitemporal hyperspectral datasets and agentic architecture search define today's twin frontiers — EO foundation models get better data, AI foundation models get self-designed architectures.
