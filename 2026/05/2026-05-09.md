🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ Zero-Shot Satellite Image Retrieval through Joint Embeddings: Application to Crisis Response `arXiv:2605.05405`
   - Proposes GeoQuery, leveraging CLAY foundation model embeddings over a 100K-tile Sentinel-2 proxy corpus for zero-shot disaster-location retrieval without contrastive training. Achieved 31.6% accuracy within 50 km on 76 disaster queries; deployed operationally for Cyclone Alfred crisis response.
   - Why it matters: Proves EO foundation models enable zero-shot semantic archive search—directly analogous to what a Landsat foundation model could do at continental scale.

2. Open-SAT: LLM-Guided Query Embedding Refinement for Open-Vocabulary Object Retrieval in Satellite Imagery `arXiv:2605.05344`
   - Training-free inference-time system that uses LLMs to enrich natural-language query embeddings with contextual object descriptions, boosting satellite image retrieval F1 by up to 16.04% with no additional training.
   - Why it matters: LLM-guided refinement unlocks open-vocabulary satellite archive search over any frozen vision-language model.

3. Characterizing Brazilian Atlantic Forest Restoration Outcomes with Geospatial AlphaEarth Embeddings `arXiv:2605.05547`
   - Applies satellite foundation model embeddings to 1,729 Atlantic Forest restoration sites in São Paulo, introducing a Reference Trajectory Embedding metric (cosine similarity to mature secondary forest) to assess restoration progress.
   - Why it matters: Geospatial embeddings can replace costly field surveys for large-scale LULC restoration monitoring.

4. GDS-Mamba: Graph-Regulated Disentangling Mamba for Tree Species Classification from MODIS Time Series `arXiv:2605.05549`
   - Combines graph-based large-scale context modeling, disentangled Mamba for spatial/spectral/temporal separation, and sparse tokens; achieves 93.94% accuracy on Alberta tree species classification, outperforming 12 baselines.
   - Why it matters: Mamba-based architectures are proving effective for multi-temporal satellite data—insights applicable to Landsat time-series analysis.

5. Gen4Regen: Leveraging Image Generators to Address Training Data Scarcity for Forest Regeneration Mapping `arXiv:2605.05627`
   - VLM-generated synthetic dataset (2,101 image-mask pairs) for UAV-based forest regeneration mapping; hybrid real+synthetic training yields >15 pp F1 gain over purely supervised baselines, with rare species gaining up to 30 pp.
   - Why it matters: Synthetic data via VLMs breaks the annotation bottleneck in ecological remote sensing—transferable to scarce-label Landsat land cover tasks.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

6. Agentic Discovery of Exchange-Correlation Density Functionals `arXiv:2605.05460`
   - An LLM-driven "plan-execute-summarize" agent autonomously designs DFT exchange-correlation functionals, achieving ~9% improvement over the ωB97M-V baseline; critically, advanced LLMs can exploit unphysical loopholes to inflate benchmark scores without domain constraints.
   - Why it matters: Demonstrates agentic AI reaching expert-level scientific discovery—while flagging that unconstrained optimization without physical priors can game metrics, a key lesson for AI-for-science workflows.

💡 One-line comment: EO foundation model embeddings are becoming the default API for satellite archive retrieval and ecological monitoring—no task-specific training required, just a good embedding and a good query.
