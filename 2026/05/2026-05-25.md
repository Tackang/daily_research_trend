🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ A Non-Reference Diffusion-Based Restoration Framework for Landsat 7 ETM+ SLC-off Imagery in Antarctica `arXiv:2605.21371`
   - DiffGF restores ~22% missing pixels from Landsat 7's 2003 SLC failure via a two-stage latent+pixel diffusion model, requiring no external reference imagery. Trained on SLCANT, a newly built Antarctic dataset, with validated downstream crevasse detection.
   - Why it matters: Unlocks the full historical Landsat 7 archive for Antarctic EO — critical for long-term multi-temporal analysis and foundation model pretraining on polar regions.

2. Vision Transformers and CNNs for Land Use Scene Classification `arXiv:2605.21268`
   - Systematic benchmark of ViTs vs. CNNs on UC Merced and EuroSAT: CNNs outperform on small datasets with strong local texture, while ViTs win on complex scenes when training data is sufficient.
   - Why it matters: Empirical architecture-selection guide for LULC tasks, especially under real-world data constraints.

3. STAR-IOD: Scale-decoupled Topology Alignment for Remote Sensing Incremental Object Detection `arXiv:2605.20738`
   - Combines subspace-decoupled topology distillation with cluster-driven pseudo-label generation to combat catastrophic forgetting in continual RS detection, gaining +1.7–2.1% mAP on new DIOR-IOD / DOTA-IOD benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: Enables updating RS detection models with new categories without retraining from scratch.

4. Foundation AI Models for Aerosol Optical Depth Estimation from PACE Satellite Data `arXiv:2605.00678`
   - ViTCG, a channel-wise-grouped Vision Transformer, retrieves AOD from PACE hyperspectral TOA radiance, cutting MSE by 62% vs. Prithvi while producing spatially coherent aerosol fields.
   - Why it matters: First foundation model benchmark for PACE atmospheric retrieval — signals geospatial FMs expanding from surface to atmospheric EO tasks.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

5. Ratchet: A Minimal Hygiene Recipe for Self-Evolving LLM Agents `arXiv:2605.22148`
   - Frozen LLMs self-manage their skill libraries via four lightweight mechanisms (outcome-driven retirement, bounded cap, meta-skill guidance, pattern canonicalisation), lifting MBPP+ pass rate from 0.258 to 0.584 with Claude Opus.
   - Why it matters: Skill library hygiene — not model scale — is the key lever for autonomous agent self-improvement.

6. IdleSpec: Exploiting Idle Time via Speculative Planning for LLM Agents `arXiv:2605.22154`
   - During tool-execution waits, agents speculatively draft multiple plan branches and adaptively select among them; improves GAIA+FRAMES accuracy by 5.1% and MLE-Bench medal rate by 9.1% with no extra model calls.
   - Why it matters: Turns unavoidable I/O latency into a free performance boost — directly applicable to long-horizon scientific agentic workflows.

7. TO-Agents: A Multi-Agent AI Pipeline for Preference-Guided Topology Optimization `arXiv:2605.21622`
   - Specialist agents translate natural-language design goals into iterative topology optimization loops using VLM-based evaluation, achieving 60% preference-aligned outcomes on cantilever/product design tasks.
   - Why it matters: Shows multi-agent LLM orchestration can close the loop on physics-based scientific design — a template for AI-for-science automation.

8. Planning in the LLM Era: Building for Reliability and Efficiency `arXiv:2605.21902`
   - Argues LLM-generated symbolic solvers (rather than single-shot plans) yield sound and efficient agentic planning; surveys the field and maps open research directions.
   - Why it matters: Provides conceptual scaffolding for building trustworthy AI planners in verifiable scientific domains.
