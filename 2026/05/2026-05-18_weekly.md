📚 [2026-05-18] Weekly Journal Digest (past 7 days Early Access)

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ Generating an Annual 30 m Rice Cover Product for Monsoon Asia (2018–2023) Using Harmonized Landsat and Sentinel-2 Data and the NASA-IBM Geospatial Foundation Model
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Vol. 335, 2026)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115256
   - Applies the NASA-IBM Prithvi geospatial foundation model — pre-trained on Harmonized Landsat and Sentinel-2 (HLS) data — to generate annual 30 m rice maps across Monsoon Asia from 2018–2023, outperforming conventional ML baselines in cross-region generalization.
   - Validated over 12 countries with F1 scores consistently above 0.85; annual product enables interannual paddy area change tracking at national to continental scale.
   - Why it matters: First operational demonstration of an HLS-based foundation model for annual large-area crop mapping — directly validates Landsat FM transferability to downstream agriculture tasks.

2. ⭐ AgriFM: A Multi-Source Temporal Remote Sensing Foundation Model for Agriculture Mapping
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Vol. 334, 2026)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115234
   - Pre-trains a Video Swin Transformer backbone on 25M+ globally sampled images from MODIS, Landsat-8/9, and Sentinel-2, using a synchronized spatiotemporal downsampling strategy to handle variable-length satellite time series.
   - Outperforms general-purpose RSFMs on five crop mapping tasks (field delineation, fine-grained LULC, paddy mapping, winter wheat mapping) across three continents.
   - Why it matters: Multi-source temporal pre-training integrating Landsat sets a new RSFM benchmark for agriculture — the multi-resolution fusion strategy is directly transferable to Landsat FM pre-training pipelines.

3. ⭐ Spectral and Spatial Harmonization of Landsat ETM+, OLI, and Sentinel-2 MSI Using Time-Series Harmonic Analysis and Blur Kernel Estimation
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Vol. 339, 2026)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115384
   - Proposes a two-stage harmonization framework combining harmonic time-series analysis for spectral correction and blind kernel estimation for spatial blur removal, enabling consistent multi-sensor reflectance across Landsat generations and Sentinel-2.
   - Demonstrates reduced inter-sensor RMSE by 40–60% over direct compositing approaches in diverse land cover types.
   - Why it matters: Robust Landsat/Sentinel-2 harmonization is a prerequisite for large-scale multi-sensor pre-training datasets for Landsat foundation models.

4. A Framework for Integrating Spatiotemporal Deep Learning Methods with Landsat for Annual Land Cover and Impervious Surface Mapping (LCAMS)
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Vol. 338, 2026)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115347
   - Introduces LCAMS, a two-stage deep learning system that decouples per-year spatial feature extraction (Landsat) from inter-annual temporal consistency modeling, serving as the engine behind the new Annual National Land Cover Database (NLCD).
   - Achieves 91.3% overall accuracy at national scale (CONUS) with annual updates, replacing decade-interval NLCD releases.
   - Why it matters: Landsat-based deep learning is now production-ready for operational national-scale annual LULC — a strong use-case argument for Landsat foundation models in mapping pipelines.

5. SpecAware: A Spectral-Content Aware Foundation Model for Unifying Multi-Sensor Learning in Hyperspectral Remote Sensing Mapping
   - Journal: ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (Vol. 234, April 2026)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2026.02.024
   - Proposes a hypernetwork-driven unified embedding architecture that dynamically conditions channel-wise encoding on sensor metadata and image content, pre-trained on Hyper-400K (400K+ AVIRIS hyperspectral patches); achieves SOTA on segmentation, change detection, and scene classification across seven datasets.
   - Enables zero-shot transfer to unseen sensors by treating spectral configuration as a learnable input rather than a fixed architecture assumption.
   - Why it matters: Sensor-agnostic foundation model design via hypernetworks — a scalable strategy applicable beyond HSI to any multi-spectral sensor family including Landsat/Sentinel combinations.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

6. On the Foundations of Earth Foundation Models
   - Journal: Communications Earth & Environment (Vol. 7, Article 103, 2026)
   - DOI: 10.1038/s43247-025-03127-x
   - Zhu et al. define 11 desirable features for Earth foundation models — spanning geoscience versatility, energy efficiency, adversarial robustness, uncertainty quantification, and interpretability — and evaluate current EO FMs against these criteria.
   - Identifies critical gaps: most current EO FMs lack principled uncertainty estimates and cross-domain geoscience transferability beyond remote sensing imagery.
   - Why it matters: Community-defining framework paper that sets the evaluation standard for next-generation Earth observation foundation models — essential reference for Landsat FM design and benchmarking.

7. A Semantic-Enhanced Multi-Modal Remote Sensing Foundation Model for Earth Observation (SkySense++)
   - Journal: Nature Machine Intelligence (2026)
   - DOI: 10.1038/s42256-025-01078-8
   - Pre-trains on 27M multi-modal RS images using a factorized multi-modal architecture with semantic enhancement via text-image alignment; achieves SOTA across 12 EO downstream tasks spanning 7 domains (agriculture, forestry, oceanography, atmosphere, biology, land surveying, disaster).
   - Single pre-trained model generalizes to both optical and SAR sensors without sensor-specific retraining heads.
   - Why it matters: Largest multi-modal RS foundation model to date — demonstrates that multi-modal pre-training with semantic alignment yields strong universal EO representations, setting the bar for future Landsat multi-sensor FMs.

📊 This week's journal trend: Landsat-centric foundation models and multi-sensor harmonization converge toward operational annual LULC pipelines, while hyperspectral and multi-modal FMs push sensor-agnostic pre-training to the forefront.
