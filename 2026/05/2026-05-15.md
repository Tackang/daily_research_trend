🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ No One Knows the State of the Art in Geospatial Foundation Models `arXiv:2605.12678`
   - Audits 152 GFM papers revealing 46 cross-paper benchmark disagreements >10 pts, with 39% not releasing model weights.
   - Proposes six community standards: named-license weight release, shared core evaluations, variance reporting, and controlled ablations.
   - Why it matters: Exposes the reproducibility crisis in geospatial FM benchmarking — directly relevant to validating Landsat foundation models.

2. RS-Claw: Progressive Active Tool Exploration via Hierarchical Skill Trees for Remote Sensing Agents `arXiv:2605.13391`
   - Repositions LLM agents as "active explorers" within RS tool ecosystems using hierarchical skill trees for tool organization.
   - Achieves 86% input token compression while outperforming flat and RAG baselines on Earth-Bench complex reasoning tasks.
   - Why it matters: Structured tool exploration dramatically boosts RS agent efficiency for complex geospatial queries.

3. Earth Science Foundation Models: From Perception to Reasoning and Discovery `arXiv:2605.12542`
   - Surveys 200+ datasets across all Earth science domains tracing FM evolution from perception to agentic scientific workflows.
   - Identifies multimodal data heterogeneity and scientific reliability as key open challenges.
   - Why it matters: Broadest current overview of Earth science FM landscape — essential reference for positioning Landsat FM research.

4. GHGbench: A Unified Multi-Entity, Multi-Task Benchmark for Carbon Emission Prediction `arXiv:2605.13743`
   - Benchmarks GHG prediction using 491K+ building-year records with multimodal RS embeddings from 13 open sources.
   - Foundation models with RS embeddings improve predictions, but cross-city transferability remains poor.
   - Why it matters: First RS-based benchmark targeting carbon stock monitoring scalability across urban and regional contexts.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
5. Agentic AI for Remote Sensing: Technical Challenges and Research Directions `arXiv:2604.24919`
   - Argues generic agentic AI applied to EO faces cascading errors from reprojection, resampling, and compositing operations.
   - Proposes EO-native agent design: geospatial state management, tool-aware reasoning, and verification-guided execution.
   - Why it matters: First principled framework for reliable EO agents beyond simply porting generic AI to geospatial domains.

6. PROMETHEUS: Automating Deep Causal Research Integrating Text, Data and Models `arXiv:2605.12835`
   - Transforms literature, agent traces, and simulations into "causal atlases" using sheaf-like mathematical structures.
   - Validated on ocean temperature impacts, hydrology modeling, microplastics forcing, and protein signaling research.
   - Why it matters: Demonstrates AI-driven causal discovery across Earth and life sciences — a step toward autonomous scientific research.

7. Position: Agentic AI System Is a Foreseeable Pathway to AGI `arXiv:2605.12966`
   - Proves agentic systems exhibit exponentially superior generalization over monolithic models across DAG topologies.
   - ICML'26 position paper with theoretical analysis of optimization constraints between agentic and single-model architectures.
   - Why it matters: Provides theoretical grounding for why distributed scientific AI agents can outperform scaling single models.
