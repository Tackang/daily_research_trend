🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. Benchmarking Composed Image Retrieval for Applied Earth Observation `arXiv:2605.24442`
   - Introduces xView2-CIR, a disaster-change dataset, and benchmarks 6 vision-language backbones for query-guided satellite image search; training-free composition proves a strong baseline.
   - Establishes that change-centric retrieval in EO fundamentally differs from attribute-based approaches, with scene identity preservation being key.
   - Why it matters: Language-guided archive search enables faster retrieval of pre/post-disaster satellite scenes.

2. Overcoming 'Physics Shock' in Earth Observation `arXiv:2605.24106`
   - Identifies "Physics Shock"—gradient instability when rigid physical laws meet noisy SAR flood data—and proposes a heteroscedastic uncertainty PINN that adaptively relaxes constraints in high-noise areas.
   - Achieves +25% relative IoU over deterministic baselines on the Sen1Floods11 benchmark.
   - Why it matters: PINN-based flood mapping becomes operationally viable for real-world noisy SAR imagery.

3. Plume Segmentation from MethaneSAT with Cross-Sensor Transfer Learning `arXiv:2605.24273`
   - Applies Mask R-CNN (ResNet-50) for methane plume detection from MethaneSAT satellite data, using transfer learning from MethaneAIR airborne imagery to overcome satellite data scarcity.
   - Physics-informed postprocessing delivers 0.92 precision / 0.70 recall in high-confidence operational mode.
   - Why it matters: Automated, scalable GHG emission monitoring from satellite is now feasible at the source level.

4. ⭐ Remote Sensing Data Imputation via Deep Learning for Multispectral Imagery `arXiv:2605.24003`
   - Benchmarks CNN and CNN-LSTM models for reconstructing cloud-obscured spectral bands in PlanetScope SuperDove time series; standard CNN outperforms linear interpolation and more complex architectures.
   - Reconstructed imagery preserves validity for algal bloom detection indices across four lake test sites.
   - Why it matters: Robust cloud-gap filling is foundational for Landsat and multispectral satellite time-series analysis.

   - Pairs LLM hypothesis generation with hypothesis-conditioned active experiment selection; introduces ActiveSciBench for enzyme kinetics and gene regulatory network discovery tasks.
   - Achieves 2–5× sample efficiency gain over competing methods; 67.6% symbolic accuracy on NewtonBench.
   - Why it matters: Closed-loop LLM experimentation slashes data requirements for scientific discovery pipelines.

6. EvoSci: Bio-Inspired Multi-Agent Framework for the Evolution of Scientific Discovery `arXiv:2605.24018`
   - Mentor, researcher, and reviewer LLM agents co-evolve research ideas through evolutionary feedback and knowledge graph modeling in a collaborative multi-agent loop.
   - Achieves peer-review score 4.90 and top-10 ranking among 54 proposals evaluated on real research topics.
   - Why it matters: Multi-agent LLM evolution shows promise for automated, competitive research ideation at scale.
