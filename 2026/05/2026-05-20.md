📡 [2026-05-20] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Synthetic Aperture Radar Image Change Detection Based on Global Dynamic Context-Aware Network `arXiv:2605.16764`
   - Proposes GDNet with a global dynamic convolution module that adapts kernel weights based on global semantic context, combined with a two-stage Mixup augmentation strategy for improved generalization.
   - Evaluated on three SAR datasets, outperforming existing change detection methods.
   - Why it matters: Dynamic context-aware kernels offer a principled way to bridge local detail and global semantics in SAR change detection.

2. Axial-Relation Guided Fusion State Space Model for Optical-Elevation Sensing Image Segmentation `arXiv:2605.16768`
   - ARG-Mamba integrates optical and DSM elevation data via axial-relation guided cross-modal fusion within a Mamba-based state space model.
   - Achieves competitive segmentation accuracy on ISPRS Vaihingen and Potsdam benchmarks with favorable computational cost.
   - Why it matters: Efficient multi-modal fusion of optical + elevation data is a key enabler for large-scale urban LULC mapping.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
3. From Prompts to Protocols: An AI Agent for Laboratory Automation `arXiv:2605.16552`
   - LLM-based agent integrated with EOS generates, validates, and corrects experimental lab protocols from natural language with a visual graph editor.
   - Achieves 97% first-attempt protocol generation success across simulated chemistry, biology, and materials science environments.
   - Why it matters: Bridges natural language intent and automated lab execution — a concrete step toward AI-driven scientific discovery.

4. NeuroMAS: Multi-Agent Systems as Neural Networks with Joint Reinforcement Learning `arXiv:2605.16757`
   - Frames multi-agent LLM systems as trainable neural-network architectures where role-free agent nodes learn coordination strategies via joint RL rather than fixed workflows.
   - Progressive scaling from pre-trained smaller systems outperforms both workflow-engineered and trained MAS baselines.
   - Why it matters: Shifts MAS design from manual orchestration to principled architecture scaling — opens new paths for capable agentic systems.

5. ArtifactLinker: Linking Scientific Artifacts for Automatic State-of-the-Art Discovery `arXiv:2605.16902`
   - Uses GNNs and LLM-based coding agents to predict and verify missing model–dataset links on HuggingFace's artifact graph (ArtifactBench: 14K artifacts, 51K relations).
   - Demonstrates automated discovery of state-of-the-art results without manual benchmarking.
   - Why it matters: Automates the SOTA tracking bottleneck — accelerates the research cycle in rapidly evolving ML domains.

6. AgentWall: A Runtime Safety Layer for Local AI Agents `arXiv:2605.16265`
   - Policy-enforcing MCP proxy that intercepts every agent action, evaluates it against declarative policies, and logs a full execution trail; works across Claude Code, Cursor, and Windsurf.
   - Achieves 92.9% policy enforcement accuracy with sub-millisecond overhead on 14 benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: As agents take real system actions, runtime guardrails become as critical as model-level alignment.

7. Skim: Speculative Execution for Fast and Efficient Web Agents `arXiv:2605.16565`
   - Profiles websites offline to generate URL/answer templates; routes routine steps to lightweight models and escalates only failures to frontier agents.
   - Reduces per-task cost 1.9× and latency 33% with no accuracy loss across three backbone agents.
   - Why it matters: Practical efficiency gains could democratize large-scale agentic deployment in web-heavy scientific workflows.
