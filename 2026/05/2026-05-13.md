🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ WATCH: Wide-Area Archaeological Site Tracking for Change Detection `arXiv:2605.08160`
   - Benchmarks SatMAE, Prithvi-EO-2.0, GeoRSCLIP, and Satlas-Pretrain on ~2,000 heritage sites via PlanetScope mosaics (2017–2024); unsupervised temporal embedding comparison outperforms weakly-supervised baselines with month-level precision.
   - Why it matters: First head-to-head FM embedding benchmark for scalable satellite change detection — directly informs Landsat FM evaluation methodology.

2. Geospatial-Temporal Sensemaking of Remote Sensing Activity Detections with Multimodal LLM `arXiv:2605.10739`
   - Introduces SMART-HC-VQA, a Sentinel-2 VQA dataset for construction activity; adapts MLLM to spatiotemporal reasoning over multi-date satellite time series.
   - Why it matters: MLLMs applied to EO time series for event understanding beyond pixel classification — new paradigm for RS + LLM integration.

3. HyGAS: Open, Sensor-Agnostic Multi-Satellite Methane Plume Retrieval Platform `arXiv:2605.09049`
   - Unified L1-radiance-to-emission-rate pipeline covering PRISMA, EnMAP, Tanager-1, EMIT, and GHGSat with full uncertainty propagation.
   - Why it matters: Cross-sensor harmonization for GHG monitoring mirrors the multi-mission consistency challenge central to Landsat/Sentinel workflows.

4. Do Foundation Model Embeddings Improve Cross-Country Crop Yield Generalisation? `arXiv:2605.08113`
   - Tests geospatial FMs (Prithvi, SatMAE) on Sub-Saharan Africa maize yield prediction; FMs show no advantage over conventional spectral features in leave-one-country-out evaluation.
   - Why it matters: Exposes EO FM domain-generalization limits — a critical design constraint for Landsat foundation model development.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

5. LLMs with In-Context Learning for Algorithmic Theoretical Physics `arXiv:2605.08212`
   - Claude + Maple CAS automates cosmological perturbation calculations in modified gravity; frontier LLM solves most test problems from in-context worked examples alone.
   - Why it matters: LLM + domain tool integration is a scalable, replicable AI-for-Science pattern.

6. MemQ: Integrating Q-Learning into Self-Evolving Memory Agents over Provenance DAGs `arXiv:2605.08374`
   - Q-learning propagates credit through provenance DAGs, teaching agents to evolve memory access strategies; outperforms static RAG on six multi-hop benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: Self-evolving memory is a foundational capability for autonomous research agents.

7. Universal Gene Regulatory Network Inference via Single-Cell Foundation Models `arXiv:2605.08128`
   - Virtual Value Perturbation and Gradient Trajectory probe scFM internals to extract generalizable inter-gene features; large cross-dataset GRN inference improvement.
   - Why it matters: FM probing for scientific discovery — a generalizable pattern beyond biology.
