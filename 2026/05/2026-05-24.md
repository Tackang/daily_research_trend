🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ Flow Matching for Sentinel-2 Super-Resolution: Implementation, Application, and Implications `arXiv:2605.00367`
   - Flow matching model performs 4× super-resolution of Sentinel-2 imagery, outperforming diffusion and Real-ESRGAN baselines in pixel-wise accuracy. Validated on a continental-scale 2.5m land cover product over Chesapeake Bay (89.11% OA).
   - Why it matters: Directly relevant to high-res Landsat/Sentinel data preparation for foundation model pretraining.

2. ⭐ Rethinking Electro-Optical Vision Foundation Models for Remote Sensing Retrieval: A Controlled Comparison with Generalist VFM `arXiv:2605.02283`
   - Controlled evaluation shows strong generalist VFMs match or outperform domain-specific EO foundation models for remote sensing image retrieval, with superior cross-scene generalization stability.
   - Why it matters: Challenges the premise of EO-specific pretraining — foundational design decisions for Landsat-scale models must be revisited.

3. Multi-Sensor Methane Mapping in a Unified Framework: Tanager-1 Integration and Comparison to EnMAP and PRISMA `arXiv:2605.09048`
   - Planet's Tanager-1 satellite integrated into a unified hyperspectral methane detection pipeline with CWCMF to suppress sensor artifacts. Demonstrated over a Buenos Aires landfill.
   - Why it matters: Multi-satellite interoperability enables global-scale greenhouse gas monitoring from orbit.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
4. I-SAFE: Wasserstein Coherence Metrics for Structural Auditing of Scientific AI Models `arXiv:2605.21731`
   - Proposes distributional coherence metrics to audit whether scientific AI models learn domain-meaningful patterns rather than shortcuts. Applied to drug-target interaction, revealing three equally accurate models have vastly different internal representations.
   - Why it matters: Performance metrics alone cannot validate AI models for science — structural auditing is essential.

5. ExComm: Exploration-Stage Communication for Error-Resilient Agentic Test-Time Scaling `arXiv:2605.22102`
   - Multi-agent protocol intercepts conflicting belief states during exploration, verifies and corrects errors across agents. Achieves 5–6% improvement over state-of-the-art test-time scaling baselines.
   - Why it matters: Error propagation remains a critical bottleneck in multi-agent reasoning — coordinated correction unlocks reliable autonomous workflows.

6. SR²AM: Efficient Agentic Reasoning Through Self-Regulated Simulative Planning `arXiv:2605.22138`
   - Decomposes agent reasoning into simulative planning, self-regulation, and reactive execution. Reduces token usage by 25–95% vs. comparable LLM agents while achieving competitive task performance.
   - Why it matters: Lean agentic architectures with learned self-regulation are key to scaling AI automation in scientific domains.

💡 One-line comment: Generalist VFMs are eating EO specialists' lunch — while agentic AI learns to plan smarter and burn fewer tokens.
