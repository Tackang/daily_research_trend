🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. RemoteVLPG: How to Obtain a Specific VLM for Language-Guided Path Generation on Remote Sensing Images? `arXiv:2611.25458`
   - Builds a task-specific VLM that generates paths between start and end points on remote sensing images. Compares three architectures and releases a pre-training dataset and three benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: Shows that a small task-specific VLM can beat some commercial VLMs zero-shot on RS spatial reasoning.

2. Fusing Multimodal CNN Features with Structurally-Masked Transformers for Hierarchical Sub-Pixel Tree Species Mapping `arXiv:2611.25824`
   - Fuses PRISMA hyperspectral, panchromatic and DEM data in a multi-branch CNN with a structurally-masked Transformer. The Transformer enforces taxonomic consistency from forest type down to species.
   - Why it matters: Taxonomy-aware sub-pixel mapping is useful for forest carbon and biomass work.

3. Does It Depend on the Split? Frozen Satellite Features vs. Fine-Tuned CNNs for Tree Species Mapping `arXiv:2611.25902`
   - Compares a frozen DINOv3 satellite model (linear probe) with a fine-tuned ResNet-50 on TreeSatAI. 28.8% of test patches overlap train/val; on a spatially held-out split the gap vanishes (F1 0.648 vs 0.640).
   - Why it matters: Spatial leakage can inflate foundation-model benchmark claims, so use spatially disjoint splits.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
4. TwinLab-Bench: Evaluating AI Scientists in Agentic Twins of Real Labs `arXiv:2611.26248`
   - 52 tasks across physical science, life science and engineering. Agents design experiments, and "twin" agents run them using real lab records.
   - Why it matters: The best model scores only 45.0, so end-to-end AI scientists are still far from reliable.

5. Steering Automated Scientific Discovery via Audited Epistemic Exploration `arXiv:2611.25214`
   - EpiCompass keeps an epistemic tree linking hypotheses, evidence, beliefs and revisions. It beats baselines on three discovery benchmarks and three LLM backbones.
   - Why it matters: Tracking evidence and belief revision reduces unsupported claims in automated discovery.

6. Pro² Agent: Scientific Problem Probing for Autonomous AI R&D `arXiv:2611.25357`
   - Uses an explicit "Problem State" of blocking problems and candidate fixes as the agent's main state, instead of the codebase. Reaches a 70.7% MLE-Bench medal rate at 24h and 76.0% at 48h.
   - Why it matters: Problem-centric state is a promising design for long-horizon research agents.

7. Closing the Analyze-to-Design Gap: An Authorization-Bounded Agent for Self-Driving Laboratories `arXiv:2611.25043`
   - An agent for the Analyze-Design step of the design-make-test-analyze loop, with authorized, validated and auditable actions. On an LC-MS screening campaign its rankings matched scripted peak integration.
   - Why it matters: Auditable action limits are a prerequisite for safe lab automation.
