📡 [2026-11-14] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. In-orbit demonstration of object-based change detection applied to the maritime domain `arXiv:2611.14857`
   - Validates an onboard satellite pipeline that detects vessels and matches them across consecutive images on the CogniSAT-6 mission.
   - Why it matters: Onboard change detection means downlinking alerts instead of raw imagery.

2. RSLiveBench-UAV: A Continually Evolving Vision-Language Benchmark for Aerial Spatial Intelligence `arXiv:2611.14953`
   - A regularly refreshed benchmark built from UAV imagery. Embodied models outperform the remote-sensing models tested.
   - Why it matters: Refreshing the data limits benchmark contamination, and it shows that RS-specific models lag on spatial reasoning.

3. CODE-PIF: Convex/Deep Multiview Pseudoinvariant Features for Multitemporal Multispectral Surface Reflectance Imagery `arXiv:2611.16802`
   - Uses an unsupervised convex optimization step to refine coarse pseudoinvariant features from DeepMGCCA. This improves agreement of reflectance and spectral values across acquisition dates.
   - Why it matters: Multi-date radiometric consistency underpins reliable change detection and time-series pretraining (relevant to Landsat work, but not Landsat-specific, so no ⭐).

4. SkillEarth `arXiv:2611.15481`
   - Extracts reusable remote-sensing tool-use skills from agent trajectories without retraining the model.
   - Why it matters: It points to a low-cost way to build EO analysis agents.

5. SyntheIce: A Synthetic Radargram Annotation Benchmark for Englacial Layer Reconstruction `arXiv:2611.14728`
   - Provides a synthetic radar dataset of subsurface ice and a linking algorithm that joins fragmented ice-layer detections into continuous horizons.
   - Why it matters: Synthetic labels ease the annotation bottleneck in geophysical radar.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
6. RSI-PDEbench `arXiv:2611.14949`
   - A benchmark and self-improving agent harness for discovering and solving PDEs from data.
   - Why it matters: It offers a testbed for agent-driven scientific discovery in physical modeling.

7. Science Prediction Universe `arXiv:2611.15049`
   - Benchmarks agents on ranking experimental outcomes in chemistry, materials, and biology. The best configuration reaches a Spearman correlation of only 0.478.
   - Why it matters: Agents are still far from reliably predicting experimental results.

8. AutoAssumption `arXiv:2611.14844`
   - Searches over explicit assumptions in Lean-checked algorithm proofs. It reports improved KV-cache eviction and pruning methods.
   - Why it matters: It couples formal verification with automated discovery.
