🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. geo-eval-harness for Geospatial Foundation Models `arXiv:2611.33990`
   - Compares 100 models on 6 classification and 14 segmentation datasets.
   - Why it matters: General backbones compete on RGB, but geospatial models do better on multispectral data.

2. PANDA: Cross-Disaster Building Damage Mapping `arXiv:2611.33790`
   - Positive-unlabeled domain adaptation needs only a few damaged-building labels in the target domain.
   - Why it matters: Could cut labeling needs after new disasters.

3. TerraMind Embeddings for Vessel Detection `arXiv:2611.34899`
   - Sentinel-1 embeddings reach 0.95 detection ROC-AUC; ~300 m median localization error for vessels over 100 m.
   - Why it matters: Tests what compressed foundation model embeddings retain.

4. Early Rice Blast Forecasting `arXiv:2611.33237`
   - Fuses weather and remote sensing data to forecast risk 5–10 days before symptoms; 95.79% F1 in validation.
   - Why it matters: Points to earlier, targeted crop intervention.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
5. Partial Routing for Agentic Workflows `arXiv:2611.33402`
   - A lead model delegates recurring subtasks to a cheaper worker, without training either.
   - Why it matters: On ViBench, score rises 70.2→78.5 at 19% lower cost.

6. Learning to Reason in Real-Time `arXiv:2611.33513`
   - Trains agents for environments that change while they reason, tested on Frogger and Snake.
   - Why it matters: Reward gains of 33% in-distribution and 55% out-of-distribution over the best baseline.

7. Semantic Density and Agent-Memory Collapse `arXiv:2611.33184`
   - None of 14 memory-store statistics reliably predicts early accuracy decline.
   - Why it matters: Long-running agents need better health checks.
