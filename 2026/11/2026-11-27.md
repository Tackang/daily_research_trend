1. AlphaEarth Embeddings for Agricultural Mapping `arXiv:2611.34019`

2. Mangrove Mapping with Sentinel-1/2 `arXiv:2611.33369`

3. MOVR-SAR: Multi-resolution SAR Registration Dataset `arXiv:2611.33673`
   - 450K tile pairs from four SAR sources; fine-tuning lifts FlexGlue AUC@5 on Capella from 3% to 90%.
   - Why it matters: Off-the-shelf matchers can fail badly on SAR.

4. Conformal Risk Control for Sentinel-1 Flood Maps `arXiv:2611.33235`
   - Under event shift, 8 of 10 held-out folds pick thresholds that label every valid pixel as flooded.
   - Why it matters: Report mapped area alongside miss-risk guarantees.

5. Speckle Effects via Neuron Interventions `arXiv:2611.33632`
   - In the SARMAE foundation model, restoring speckle-selective neurons doesn't improve reconstruction; another 64-neuron set recovers 44% of correlation loss.
   - Why it matters: Strong neuron response isn't functional importance.
