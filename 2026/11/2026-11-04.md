📡 [2026-11-04] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ Auditing GeoFM Evaluation for Field-Extent Segmentation `arXiv:2611.00055`
   - Compares geospatial foundation models (Prithvi, TerraMind) with a U-Net on single-date Sentinel-2 field-extent segmentation.
   - Label proxies and baselines can change benchmark conclusions, and frozen features sometimes match fine-tuning.
   - Why it matters: It shows how to evaluate GeoFMs fairly, which applies directly to benchmarking a Landsat foundation model.

2. Adaptive Land Units from Earth Embeddings `arXiv:2611.01050`
   - Clusters AlphaEarth embeddings without supervision into variable-size land units over Denmark.
   - Replaces about 402M pixels with about 1.27M units.
   - Why it matters: Object-level units built from embeddings could make large-area LULC analysis much cheaper.

3. Circumpolar Wall-to-Wall Detection of Retrogressive Thaw Slumps with Scarce Labels and Extreme Class Imbalance `arXiv:2611.00046`
   - Maps Arctic permafrost thaw slumps with deep learning on 4.8 m PlanetScope imagery.
   - Proposes an evaluation protocol for rare targets.
   - Why it matters: It is a template for continental-scale change detection when labels are scarce.

4. InSARFlow: Riemannian Flow Matching for Interferometric SAR `arXiv:2611.00003`
   - A flow-matching generative model that denoises and generates SAR interferograms.
   - It respects the periodic nature of the phase.
   - Why it matters: Geometry-aware generative models could improve deformation monitoring from InSAR.

5. Shared-Pattern Collaborative Network for Multi-Source Remote Sensing Data Classification `arXiv:2611.03069`
   - Jointly classifies hyperspectral data with SAR or LiDAR.
   - Tested on Houston2013, Augsburg, and Houston2018.
   - Why it matters: It targets multi-sensor fusion, a recurring need for land cover mapping.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
6. Governing Equations of Human Sleep Macro-Structure `arXiv:2611.00550`
   - Applies symbolic discovery to 1,106 nights of sleep data.
   - Derives closed-form laws for sleep cycles.
   - Why it matters: It is a concrete example of automated discovery of interpretable equations.

7. Evolving Scientific Intelligence `arXiv:2611.00580`
   - A position paper proposing a shared scientific memory and distributed experimental loops.
   - Evidence from one lab could trigger hypotheses in another.
   - Why it matters: It sketches an architecture for collaborative AI-driven science.

8. Decentralized Multi-Agent Coordination in Shared Codebases (ParallACT) `arXiv:2611.00314`
   - Benchmarks parallel coding agents editing one repository.
   - Performance drops sharply on conflicting tasks.
   - Why it matters: Coordination is a bottleneck for scaling multi-agent systems.

💡 One-line comment: GeoFM work is shifting from building bigger models to auditing how they are evaluated, while AI-for-science moves toward autonomous discovery.
