📡 [2026-11-17] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ MaskSCD: Bitemporal Mask Classification for Semantic Change Detection `arXiv:2611.18350`
   - Treats semantic change detection as mask classification over bitemporal label pairs, keeping change and land-cover maps consistent.
   - Reports best results on SECOND, Landsat-SCD and Hi-UCD.
   - Why it matters: A Landsat LULC-change benchmark result that could serve as a downstream test for a Landsat foundation model.

2. Lens: Training-Free Semantic Search in Satellite Imagery `arXiv:2611.18251`
   - Searches large satellite mosaics using frozen DINOv3 features, debiased for positional bias.
   - Matches queries by cosine similarity, with no per-class detector.
   - Why it matters: Shows that general-purpose frozen backbones can be reused for EO retrieval.

3. DySENSE: Satellite Imagery + Building Energy Emulation `arXiv:2611.17878`
   - Jointly generates satellite-style urban imagery and aligned monthly building energy maps.
   - Tested in New York, Washington, Norfolk and Busan, with lower error than prior methods.
   - Why it matters: Links imagery generation to physical, time-varying variables.

4. FOSSeL: Few-Shot Open-Set Hyperspectral Classification `arXiv:2611.17974`
   - Learns features from unlabeled hyperspectral data by self-supervision, then propagates labels from a few seed pixels across superpixels.
   - Flags unseen classes and is competitive with fully supervised methods on three benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: Cuts labeling needs and handles unknown classes.

5. ChlCast: AI Chlorophyll-a Forecasting `arXiv:2611.17704`
   - Forecasts surface chlorophyll-a at about 4 km resolution over 10 days.
   - Has lower error than the operational 0.25° Copernicus system against satellite and in situ data.
   - Why it matters: A data-driven model beating an operational system on an ocean-color variable.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
6. Verifiable pXRD: LLM Agents for Structure Determination `arXiv:2611.17539`
   - Frames crystal-structure determination from powder X-ray diffraction as a search problem that agents can verify.
   - Introduces a 200-problem benchmark.
   - Why it matters: A concrete example of verifiable scientific-discovery automation.

7. Certified Thomson-Problem Improvements via LLM-Agent Search `arXiv:2611.17776`
   - Reports new configurations for three large-N instances that beat the Cambridge Cluster Database values, certified with exact arithmetic.
   - Documents failed attempts and verification practices.
   - Why it matters: Shows how to check agent-made discoveries.

8. More Agents, More Samples, or More Thinking? `arXiv:2611.17610`
   - Pools 516 multi-agent configurations and finds lower average accuracy than compute-matched baselines.
   - Gains need partitioned evidence, external verification or model diversity.
   - Why it matters: A caution against scaling agent count without a reason.

9. FlexCast: Flexible Context Time Series Foundation Model `arXiv:2611.17862`
   - Casts forecasting as masked prediction over variable-length context and target patches.
   - One pretrained model supports one-step, iterative and autoregressive-style forecasting.
   - Why it matters: A flexible design that could apply to satellite time series.

💡 One-line comment: Landsat benchmarks and frozen foundation features are showing up in EO work, and agent papers are moving toward verifiable discovery.
