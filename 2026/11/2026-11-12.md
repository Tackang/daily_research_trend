📡 [2026-11-12] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ Satellite Pretraining Is Not One Thing: Matched-Pair Study of Generic vs. EO Foundation Models `arXiv:2611.13849`
   - Compares five matched EO and generic encoders on few-label scene classification.
   - Satellite pretraining helps some models and hurts others.
   - Why it matters: Don't assume EO pretraining wins; this is a baseline check for any Landsat foundation model.

2. Relaxing Cross-Modal Correspondence for Multimodal Remote Sensing Pretraining `arXiv:2611.13807`
   - Uses class-consistent pairs instead of exact instance-level pairs during pretraining.
   - Transfer performance is comparable to exact pairing.
   - Why it matters: Multimodal pretraining may not need costly co-registered data.

3. SpatialSR: Resolution-Centric Arbitrary-Scale Remote Sensing Super-Resolution `arXiv:2611.13046`
   - Estimates input resolution to set the scale factor, with diffusion texture guidance distilled into one step.
   - Reports gains on super-resolution and on downstream classification and segmentation.
   - Why it matters: Resolution-aware SR could help harmonize multi-sensor imagery.

4. DisasterAgent: Evidence-Driven Agent for Verifiable Disaster Reports from RS Imagery `arXiv:2611.13776`
   - Builds a spatiotemporal graph from bitemporal optical imagery and cites evidence inline.
   - Beats general VLMs on the DisasterM3 and RSCC benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: Verifiable citations make EO agents more trustworthy.

5. Distribution Shift Detection in Remote Sensing Imagery using ML Models `arXiv:2611.13510`
   - Evaluates Alibi-Detect, NannyML and PyTorch-OOD on cross-dataset UAV imagery.
   - Finds trade-offs between detection accuracy and latency.
   - Why it matters: Shift monitoring is needed when models move across regions and sensors.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
6. EarthRecord: Benchmark for Multimodal Agents on Raw Satellite Archives `arXiv:2611.12934`
   - Poses 238 land-change questions over four decades of data at 56 sites.
   - Agents finish most analyses but struggle to map from imagery, and about half of answers follow altered data.
   - Why it matters: It exposes the reliability gap in EO agents.

7. Auditable Human-Agent Teams for Autonomous X-ray Experimentation `arXiv:2611.12933`
   - Coding agents run synchrotron experiments under evolving human goals.
   - Full transcripts let the authors reconstruct decision provenance.
   - Why it matters: Provenance tracking is key to trustworthy autonomous science.

8. Dependable Long-Horizon LLM Agents `arXiv:2611.13254`
   - Annotates 95 failed runs from six multi-agent systems.
   - Evidence against the error often existed beforehand but was rarely acted on.
   - Why it matters: It points to error-detection fixes for long agent workflows.

💡 One-line comment: Evaluation and verifiability are the theme today, covering EO foundation models and science agents.
