   - Reports 57.3% mean mIoU across eight clean benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: Open-vocabulary LULC mapping without retraining is a cheap way to reuse foundation models.

   - Why it matters: It shows that agents need physics-aware verification before they are trusted on climate code.

   - Agent harness for multi-week materials research, applied to Cu-Zn surface segregation with DFT and ML potentials.
   - Why it matters: A concrete case of autonomous scientific discovery running over long horizons.

   - Ablates context and skill management in autoresearch agents on MLE-Bench and KernelBench.

   - A verification-first monitor improves correct recovery.
   - Why it matters: Silent failures are a real risk for any automated research pipeline.

7. Gradient-Free Few-Shot Adaptation of Vision Models via Agentic Coding `arXiv:2611.28105`
   - A coding agent writes a program that sets class weights in a frozen detector head, with no gradient steps.
   - Why it matters: It offers a low-cost route to adapting frozen vision backbones in few-shot settings.
