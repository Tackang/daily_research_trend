📡 [2026-11-18] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ Machine Learning for Near Real-Time Identification of Wildfire Burned Areas `arXiv:2611.20036`
   - A pix2pix model maps a preprocessed index from Landsat 8/9 imagery to an approximation of the USGS burn probability.
   - Evaluated by RMSE.
   - Why it matters: Landsat-based generative mapping that could be reused as a downstream task for a Landsat foundation model.
2. Puzzle: One Pass Is Not Enough for Open-Vocabulary Remote Sensing Segmentation `arXiv:2611.19146`
   - A plug-and-play iterative re-inference method that refines patch-level predictions.
   - Targets segmentation of unseen classes in remote sensing imagery.
   - Why it matters: improves open-vocabulary segmentation without retraining.
3. SAR-TopoGuard: Training-Free Topological Verification for SAR-to-Optical Translation `arXiv:2611.19316`
   - Ranks candidate SAR-to-optical outputs by their consistency with SAR edges.
   - Reduces invented detail without any training.
   - Why it matters: hallucination control for cloud-free optical synthesis from SAR.
4. XLSAR-Bench: Extremely Large SAR Imagery Understanding of MLLMs `arXiv:2611.19364`
   - 405 large-scene SAR images (about 7,153 × 7,153 px on average).
   - Tests multimodal LLMs on QA, visual grounding and captioning.
   - Why it matters: exposes how MLLMs handle full-scene SAR rather than small crops.
5. From very fine Vertical Resolution to Range Super-Resolution on BIOMASS P-BAND SAR Data `arXiv:2611.21358`
   - Uses SAR tomography and multi-acquisition sub-band combining on BIOMASS P-band data.
   - Sharpens range resolution.
   - Why it matters: relevant to the BIOMASS mission's forest biomass and carbon-stock mapping.
6. TyphoonWorld: Residual Token World Model for Typhoon Imagery Forecasting `arXiv:2611.18740`
   - A discrete-token generative model that samples residuals on top of a regression forecast.
   - Produces ensemble satellite forecasts, evaluated on Digital Typhoon data.
   - Why it matters: probabilistic forecasts give uncertainty, not just a single track.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
7. Earth World Model: Physics-constrained latent dynamics for environmental scenario analysis `arXiv:2611.18654`
   - A latent-state forecasting model that takes environmental drivers (e.g. irradiance) as replaceable inputs.
   - Tested on photovoltaic and wind power sites.
   - Why it matters: scenario analysis with swappable drivers is a step toward Earth-system foundation models.
8. Guiding AI Agents to Generate Conjectures by Encoding How Theorems Build on One Another `arXiv:2611.19108`
   - Builds a graph of theorem relations to steer LLM agents toward worthwhile conjectures.
   - Why it matters: structured knowledge makes agentic scientific discovery more directed.
9. Agents Cannot Hand Over What They Have Not Yet Understood `arXiv:2611.18642`
   - Shows that short written handoff notes between successive agents lose much of what the earlier agent learned.
   - Why it matters: a limit on long-horizon multi-agent research pipelines.

💡 One-line comment: SAR and world-model work is gaining ground, while Landsat-specific papers are rare.
