📡 [2026-11-03] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. SAR-TS-BERT: Physics-Guided Self-Supervised Pre-Training With Polarimetric Features for SAR Time-Series Crop Classification `arXiv:2610.41728`
   - BERT-style pretraining on multitemporal SAR with a physics-guided span masking strategy (SpanPhysics) and a dual-branch encoder that fuses backscatter with polarimetric features.
   - Reaches 96.78% overall accuracy on EuroCropsML (Estonia), and pretraining adds +2.58 OA with only 1% labels.
   - Why it matters: Physics-aware self-supervised pretraining for satellite time series cuts the labeled data needed, which is the same idea behind a Landsat time-series foundation model.

2. SATFRONT: Detecting Atmospheric Fronts with Satellite Data using Machine Learning `arXiv:2610.42111`
   - Detects cold, warm and occluded fronts over Europe from geostationary satellite data, with optional ERA5 input.
   - Satellite-only CSI is 0.428 against 0.133 for an operational rule-based detector, and ERA5 raises it to 0.591.
   - Why it matters: Learned detection from satellite data alone can beat rule-based operational baselines.

3. Spectral Haar Binarizer: Compact Boolean Contrast Representations for Hyperspectral Classification `arXiv:2610.41478`
   - Turns hyperspectral bands into compact Boolean features by comparing spectral intervals, with AdaBoost-style selection.
   - Beats per-band binarization on 5 datasets.
   - Why it matters: Compact spectral features suit lightweight, interpretable hyperspectral classifiers.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
4. FrontierMat: Benchmarking Language Models at the Frontier of Materials R&D `arXiv:2610.41162`
   - A 330-problem benchmark from frontier materials research, where models predict withheld outcomes.
   - The best general model scores 53.7%, and domain adaptation lifts an open-weight model from 36.7% to 55.4%.
   - Why it matters: It shows how far LLMs still are from real scientific R&D, and that domain adaptation can close part of the gap.

5. Researcher-in-the-Loop Evolutionary Discovery of Entanglement-Assisted Quantum Code Families `arXiv:2610.41183`
   - Pairs LLM-guided program evolution with exact evaluation and a Lean-checked proof.
   - Yields a proved code family that closes 54 gaps in archived EAQECC tables.
   - Why it matters: It is a concrete example of LLM-driven discovery with formal verification.

6. Frozen in Time: How a Physics Foundation Model Forgets Under Sim-to-Real Adaptation `arXiv:2610.40857`
   - Studies forgetting when the fluid-dynamics foundation model Walrus-1.3B is adapted from simulation to real data.
   - Replaying about 1% of simulated data recovers most of the lost performance.
   - Why it matters: Cheap replay can reduce forgetting when adapting scientific foundation models, which applies to EO fine-tuning too.

💡 One-line comment: Few foundation-model papers on Earth observation appeared in this listing (none on Landsat), while AI-for-science work is moving toward verifiable, benchmarked discovery.
