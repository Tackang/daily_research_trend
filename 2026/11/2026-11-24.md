🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. FM4SAR-Bench 1.0: Towards A Fair Benchmark for Evaluating Foundation Models for SAR Image Interpretation `arXiv:2611.27158`
   - Proposes a taxonomy and hierarchical evaluation framework for SAR foundation models, covering 76 vision and 40 vision-language models.
   - SAR-native VLMs do not clearly beat remote-sensing VLMs trained on mixed data, largely because public annotated SAR data is scarce.
   - Why it matters: A common yardstick for EO foundation models, and a reminder that data scarcity limits SAR more than model design does.

2. PIU-CR: Physics-Informed Deep Unfolding with SAR-Optical Image Fusion for Cloud Removal `arXiv:2611.27093`
   - Fuses optical and SAR images in an unfolded multi-stage network. The data term embeds an atmospheric scattering model, with SAR-guided structure and spectral regularizers.
   - The authors report better results than state-of-the-art cloud-removal methods.
   - Why it matters: Cloud-free optical time series are a bottleneck for Landsat/Sentinel-2 analysis, and physics-informed priors make the gap-filling more trustworthy.

3. WINSO: A Large-Size Very-High-Resolution Remote Sensing Dataset for Wind Turbine Detection and Solar Panel Segmentation `arXiv:2611.27241`
   - A benchmark of 10,962 very-high-resolution images with 166,473 annotated wind turbine and solar panel instances, with baselines and cross-dataset evaluations.
   - Why it matters: It gives clean-energy infrastructure mapping a standardized test set.

4. RemoteEvolver: Expanding Earth Observation Capabilities through Agent Harness Evolution `arXiv:2611.27110`
   - An external harness for remote-sensing agents that acquires missing tools on demand and distills successful runs into reusable skills, with the base model kept fixed.
   - Reports average accuracy gains of 8.18 points on Earth-Bench and 23.86 points on OpenEarthAgent-Bench over the initial state.
   - Why it matters: Capability gains come from the harness rather than retraining, which suits EO workflows that rely on many specialized tools.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
5. How Does SAM-3 Adapt? Capacity, Update Geometry, and Domain Transfer `arXiv:2611.27142`
   - Compares LoRA, HiRA, KRAdapter, QuanTA and full fine-tuning of SAM-3 on five segmentation datasets.
   - Parameter-efficient methods often match full fine-tuning, but through different update directions rather than a shared low-rank approximation.
   - Why it matters: It is practical guidance for choosing how to adapt a large vision foundation model to a new domain.

6. ChatAtom: A Multi-Agent LLM System for Molecular Dynamics Simulation and Visualization `arXiv:2611.26782`
   - Turns a natural-language request into a full molecular dynamics workflow, using LAMMPS, OVITO, HPC and analysis agents.
   - Loadable skills support autonomous hypothesis generation.
   - Why it matters: It is a concrete example of multi-agent automation of a full simulation pipeline in science.
