📡 [2026-11-06] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ChangeJEPA: Predict Before You Compare for Remote Sensing Change Detection `arXiv:2611.05570`
   - Recasts bitemporal change detection as predicting the post-change latent representation.
   - Beats a Siamese baseline on three benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: Predictive latent objectives could carry over to LULC change monitoring on Landsat time series.

2. XuanJian-DINOSAR: A SAR Foundation Model Based on Global and Local Semantic Consistency `arXiv:2611.06105`
   - Adapts an optically pretrained DINOv2 to SAR using 360K+ public chips, with a local-consistency branch for speckle.
   - Reports strong classification and detection results.
   - Why it matters: It is a recipe for moving optical foundation models to other sensors.

3. CAPE-JEPA: Cross-modal Adversarial Probabilistic Embedding JEPA `arXiv:2611.06247`
   - Probabilistic self-supervised embedding that aligns SAR and multispectral imagery for retrieval.
   - Gains over X-JEPA on BigEarthNet-14K and fMoW.
   - Why it matters: Shows cross-sensor alignment, which is useful for fusing Landsat and Sentinel representations.

4. TOP: Transmission-Oriented Remote Sensing Pansharpening `arXiv:2611.06430`
   - Fuses satellite data onboard into a compact binary latent code, and only that code is downlinked.
   - Reaches 31.4 dB PSNR at 50x downlink compression on GaoFen-2.
   - Why it matters: Onboard compression could change what raw data reaches the ground.

5. GeoDIVE: Geospatial Descent Informed by Value Estimation `arXiv:2611.05697`
   - Best-first search over a multiresolution satellite image gallery.
   - Matches exhaustive search at about 10% of the encoding cost.
   - Why it matters: It makes planet-scale imagery search affordable.

6. Generative AI for urban planning: synthesizing land-use maps with satellite imagery via DualControlNet `arXiv:2611.05859`
   - Generates paired land-use maps and satellite images from text prompts.
   - Trained on 40K+ tiles from Orlando and Chicago.
   - Why it matters: Synthetic LULC pairs could augment scarce labels.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
7. Autonomous agents for electricity-system planning `arXiv:2611.05911`
   - LLM agents propose and revise regional grid investment plans.
   - Numerical optimization and fixed acceptance criteria check the plans.
   - Why it matters: Agents paired with a verifier are a template for reliable scientific workflows.

8. Can Large Language Models Review the Scientific Literature? A Test Against a 102-Article Benchmark `arXiv:2611.05459`
   - Tests six AI tools on finding, interpreting and synthesizing research.
   - Finds they assist but cannot replace expert judgment.
   - Why it matters: Gives a reality check on automated literature review.

9. Self-Supervised Learning of Universal Latent Spaces for Operator Learning `arXiv:2611.05735`
   - A functional autoencoder is pretrained without labels on analytic functions.
   - The frozen latent space is reused across PDE and physics tasks.
   - Why it matters: Points toward foundation-model-style pretraining for scientific simulation.

💡 One-line comment: JEPA-style predictive pretraining and cross-sensor adaptation are spreading through remote sensing, while AI-for-science agents increasingly need built-in verification.
