📡 [2026-11-19] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Sub-Meter Canopy Height Synthesis from Sentinel-2 via Anchored Residual Flow `arXiv:2611.22636`
   - Super-resolves 10 m Sentinel-2 to 0.5 m canopy height, trained on 197k LiDAR-paired tiles. A regression anchor gives 2.83 m MAE; a flow-matching residual improves perceptual fidelity.
   - Why it matters: Free imagery becomes a route to fine-scale vegetation structure and biomass, though individual-tree detection is still weak.

2. Reliability-Driven Feature Correction for Hyperspectral and LiDAR Classification `arXiv:2611.22508`
   - ReDF weights HSI and LiDAR features by class-level reliability for land-cover classification. It beats compared methods on three public datasets.
   - Why it matters: Reliability-aware fusion is a practical way to improve LULC maps from mixed sensors.

3. SARVIA: Fast-Adapting the Strong SAR Foundation Models That Are Already in Your MLLMs `arXiv:2611.22273`
   - Adapts multimodal LLMs to SAR with 600 steps (about 2 GPU-hours). It outperforms existing foundation models on six SAR detection and segmentation benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: Cheap specialization of general models may beat costly EO-specific pretraining.

4. Rethinking Perceptual Similarity for Remote Sensing (RSPD) `arXiv:2611.21816`
   - Proposes a human-aligned metric built on RemoteCLIP features for generated remote sensing imagery. It is tested on super-resolution, SAR-to-optical translation and time series.
   - Why it matters: Natural-image metrics transfer poorly, so EO generative models need their own evaluation.

5. Generation of Sample Complex Wishart Distributed Matrices and Change Detection in Polarimetric SAR Data `arXiv:2611.22943`
   - Gives a test statistic for detecting differences between two complex Wishart covariance matrices. It is checked on generated matrices.
   - Why it matters: Statistical change detection remains a lightweight baseline for PolSAR monitoring.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
6. Inventa: Decentralized Self-Improving Agents for Autonomous Scientific Discovery `arXiv:2611.21847`
   - LLM research units generate and verify ideas against a shared knowledge store. It scores 73.6% on EurekaBench versus 56.2% for CORAL, using 82% fewer model calls.
   - Why it matters: Decentralized agents may make automated discovery cheaper and more scalable.

7. DiscoveryAgent: A Framework for Scientist-centric Agentic AI `arXiv:2611.21950`
   - Combines autonomous investigation with scientist-steered hypotheses and audit agents. Case studies span physical, social and life sciences.
   - Why it matters: Keeping humans in the loop with auditing is key to trusting AI-driven research.

8. An AI-Based OSSE Framework to Evaluate Learned Physical Consistency `arXiv:2611.22024`
   - Compares FourCastNet V2, GraphCast, Aurora and Prithvi WxC in a 60-day experiment inserting ERA5 temperature data. The foundation models (Prithvi WxC, Aurora) respond most strongly, and GraphCast shows an instability.
   - Why it matters: Shows whether Earth-system foundation models learn physics rather than just fit data.
