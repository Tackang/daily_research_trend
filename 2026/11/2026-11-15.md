📡 [2026-11-15] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Deep Unrolling of Lanczos-based Laplacian Eigensolver for Crop Classification in Satellite Images `arXiv:2611.14709`
   - Lightweight, interpretable network for per-pixel crop classification, unrolling a Lanczos eigensolver over a learned signed graph.
   - Matches state-of-the-art deep models on Harmonized Landsat-Sentinel imagery with up to 100x fewer parameters.
   - Why it matters: A parameter-efficient baseline on HLS data, relevant for Landsat-based modeling.
2. Winter wheat improves soil carbon sequestration with reduced gains in warmer climates `arXiv:2611.15232`
   - Causal machine learning on five years of Lithuanian field data estimates how winter wheat rotations affect soil organic carbon across climates.
   - Satellite data use: unconfirmed.
   - Why it matters: Climate-dependent carbon estimates help constrain carbon stock modeling.
3. Fine-Grained Generative Hallucination Detection and Correction for Aerial Image Captioning `arXiv:2611.15809`
   - Reference-free, lightweight method to detect and fix errors in captions of UAV aerial imagery.
   - Why it matters: Hallucination control is needed before vision-language models can be trusted on geospatial imagery.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
4. PhysXAI: A Theoretical Framework for Diagnosing Physical Hallucinations in Weather Foundation Models `arXiv:2611.15053`
   - Detects and attributes physics-law violations in global forecasts, evaluated on ERA5, WeatherBench 2 and MERRA-2 with GraphCast, Pangu-Weather and NeuralGCM.
   - Why it matters: Physical consistency checks are a template for validating Earth-system foundation models.
5. A Transferable Pretrained 3D Model for Impedance Inversion with Sparse-Well Prior Conditioning `arXiv:2611.14922`
   - Masked autoencoder pretrained on unlabeled seismic data, then reused for 3D impedance inversion on new surveys.
   - Beats a target-trained U-Net on a field survey without retraining.
   - Why it matters: Shows the pretrain-then-transfer recipe working for geoscience data.
6. Where Autonomy Belongs: A Separation of Powers for Agentic Program Evolution `arXiv:2611.15309`
   - Separates governance, proposal and judgment among distinct owners in LLM-driven program evolution.
   - Reports state-of-the-art results on math, systems and algorithm-design benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: Role separation could make automated scientific discovery loops more reliable.
7. A frontier machine intelligence for human neuroimaging `arXiv:2611.15094`
   - Neuro-VL, a vision-language model trained on nearly one million neuroimaging studies, reads full exams and reports findings in conversation.
   - Why it matters: Domain-scale pretraining on large archives is the same path geospatial foundation models follow.

💡 One-line comment: Foundation-model work is shifting from scaling to validation, with physical-consistency checks and agent governance as the new focus.
