📡 [2026-11-11] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. National-Scale Crop Type Mapping in Morocco Using Google AlphaEarth Foundations Embeddings and the Fields of the World Field Boundary Map `arXiv:2611.12681`
   - Lightweight classifiers on pretrained AlphaEarth embeddings, constrained to field boundaries, map three crop categories across Morocco.
   - Reaches about 87% accuracy.
   - Why it matters: Frozen EO foundation-model embeddings plus simple heads can give national-scale maps, a useful baseline for Landsat-based embeddings.
2. Polarization Aware Deep Learning Framework for SAR Despeckling `arXiv:2611.10091`
   - A single CNN despeckles single-, dual-, and full-polarization SAR using a shared nine-channel covariance representation.
   - Polarization-aware masking handles the different input modes.
   - Why it matters: One model replaces separate per-mode despeckling pipelines.
3. EarthJev `arXiv:2611.10005`
   - Ultra-high-resolution remote sensing VQA, where a lightweight verifier selects image regions and the answering model stays frozen.
   - Reports higher accuracy and lower latency than ZoomSearch.
   - Why it matters: Large EO imagery becomes practical for VLMs without retraining.
4. GeoScope: Training-Free Program Execution beyond the Counting Horizon for Ultra-High-Resolution Remote Sensing Question Answering `arXiv:2611.12699`
   - Splits large images by a measured VLM counting limit, with a text-only LLM planning the spatial queries.
   - Needs no training.
   - Why it matters: It targets the object-counting failures of VLMs on large scenes.
5. Detecting space objects with Earth-observation radar satellites `arXiv:2611.12158`
   - Uses open Sentinel-1 radar data to detect and validate space objects, including debris, at ranges of tens to hundreds of kilometres.
   - Why it matters: Open EO radar data gains a second use in space situational awareness.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
6. Sage-Taiyi: A Metacognitive Architecture for Scientific Discovery `arXiv:2611.12762`
   - Uses accumulated scientific understanding to decide what to investigate next and how.
   - Reports top ideation scores and lab-validated chemistry yield gains.
   - Why it matters: It is one of the few AI-scientist papers with wet-lab validation.
7. SciMARS: Multi-Agent Recursive Self-Improvement for Closed-Loop Scientific Discovery `arXiv:2611.12552`
   - Adds a shared evidence graph and separate editors so multi-agent teams adapt their roles and memory.
   - Why it matters: Agent teams get self-adapting structure rather than fixed roles.
8. Blind Variation, Selective Retention: Improving Creative Idea Generation in AI Scientists `arXiv:2611.12184`
   - Limits task information during idea generation to boost originality, then selects candidates using past proposal outcomes.
   - Why it matters: A concrete way to address the lack of novelty in AI-generated research ideas.

💡 One-line comment: Frozen foundation-model embeddings and training-free VLM pipelines are spreading in EO, while AI-scientist work now includes lab validation.
