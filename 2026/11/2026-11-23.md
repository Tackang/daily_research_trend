📡 [2026-11-23] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Cloude/van Zyl Decomposition for Full/Quad-Pol SAR `arXiv:2611.26195`
   - Extends the reflection-symmetric Cloude/van Zyl polarimetric decomposition to full/quad-pol data, demonstrated on X-band F-SAR data over Vejers, Denmark.
   - Gives lower entropy and a more pronounced dominant scattering mechanism for bright man-made targets.
   - Why it matters: A cleaner physical SAR scattering interpretation that can feed SAR feature extraction and pretraining. No Landsat link, so no ⭐.

2. WHU-Road3D: Dataset and Benchmark for Fine-Grained Road Surface Mapping `arXiv:2611.25873`
   - Reviews road-element datasets and extraction methods, then introduces a benchmark with richer semantics and more data modalities.
   - Covers overhead-to-perspective views and coarse-to-fine detail.
   - Why it matters: A benchmark for mapping from remote-sensing and ground views, relevant to geospatial evaluation.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
3. WeatherCraft: Reinforcement-Aligned Probabilistic Weather Forecasting with Discrete Tokens `arXiv:2611.25570`
   - Represents atmospheric states as a coarse-to-fine token hierarchy, modeled by a Transformer.
   - Combines probabilistic ensemble generation with RL alignment, bringing the GPT-style pretrain/post-train recipe to global forecasting.
   - Why it matters: Shows that tokenized pretraining plus RL alignment can transfer to Earth-system models.

4. Auditing Learned Dynamics in Weather Models `arXiv:2611.25119`
   - Uses sparse-autoencoder features from GraphCast to test whether causal relevance requires physical semantics.
   - Also tests steering physically associated latent directions to improve extreme-event forecasts.
   - Why it matters: Interpretability tools for geoscience foundation models, useful for trusting EO models.

5. A Verifier from the Past: Test-Time Compute for Time Series Foundation Models `arXiv:2611.25171`
   - Proposes Backtest-Gated Augmentation Averaging, which builds a causal verifier from a series' own rolling-origin backtests.
   - Brings test-time compute to forecasting without using future information.
   - Why it matters: Applies to forecasting pixel- or biomass-level satellite time series with foundation models.

6. DeepQuest: From Knowledge Gaps to Scientific Discovery in Alzheimer's Disease `arXiv:2611.26343`
   - An AI-scientist framework that turns unresolved questions from domain reviews into testable hypotheses.
   - Prioritizes them with data-feasibility screening and Elo ranking, and audits both experimental reliability and knowledge contribution.
   - Why it matters: It targets scientific discovery rather than just automating experiments.

7. When AI Analysts Disagree: Agentic Multiverse Analysis `arXiv:2611.25198`
   - Deploys 150 autonomous coding agents on one dataset and finds they disagree through the same defensible analytic choices humans make.
   - Proposes a framework that makes conclusions' sensitivity to analytic choices visible at scale.
   - Why it matters: A robustness check for AI-driven science and agent-generated analyses.

8. Beyond Weight Updates: Evolving Agent Harnesses for Domain Specialization `arXiv:2611.25380`
   - Evolves the system prompt, tool schemas and skill library of a frozen-policy agent with the GEPA optimizer.
   - Needs no GPU training and composes with weight-based RL.
   - Why it matters: A cheap way to specialize science agents per domain.
