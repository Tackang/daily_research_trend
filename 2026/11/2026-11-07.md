📡 [2026-11-07] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. When GeoFMs Meet VHR: Benchmarking Foundation Models on Very High Resolution EO Imagery `arXiv:2611.08003`
   - Benchmarks 12 pretrained encoders on 10 datasets at ≤1.5 m resolution. Short continued pretraining of DINOv3 ViT-L on VHR tiles improves results.
   - Why it matters: Shows how well natural-image and EO-specific pretraining transfer across resolutions.

2. TerraMesh-Masks: Open-Vocabulary Segmentation for Earth Observation `arXiv:2611.08081`
   - Releases 16M OpenStreetMap-derived binary masks. An open-vocabulary TerraMind reaches 35.18% IoU.
   - Why it matters: Brings text-prompted segmentation to EO foundation models.

3. National-scale Tree Species Mapping in Switzerland from Aerial Imagery and Multimodal Data `arXiv:2611.08095`
   - Maps basal-area shares of 10 species at 30 m by combining aerial RGB, airborne laser scanning, satellite embeddings and climate data.
   - Why it matters: Multimodal fusion captures minor species that dominant-class maps miss, which matters for forest and carbon work.

4. Detecting Weather Fronts from Satellite Observations: What Does Reanalysis Add? `arXiv:2611.08148`
   - Compares a geostationary-IR-only model with one that adds 28 ERA5 fields. CSI@150 rises from 0.402 to 0.575.
   - Why it matters: Quantifies how much satellite-only models lose without physical context.

5. Georeferenced Cross-View 3D Reconstruction from Ground and Satellite Images `arXiv:2611.07575`
   - X-VGGT is a feed-forward model that takes ground photos plus a georeferenced satellite image. It outputs camera poses, dense 3D geometry and geo-alignment.
   - Why it matters: Links ground and satellite views for scalable geo-registration.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
6. PharmAgents2: Towards Autonomous End-to-End Drug Discovery `arXiv:2611.07653`
   - Parallel agents explore targets and hypotheses with knowledge-guided decisions. Its proposed hypotheses and compounds were supported by later human-run lab experiments.
   - Why it matters: Reports lab validation of agent-generated hypotheses, which most agent papers lack.

7. RIVER: Evidence-guided Multi-agent Retrieval for Enzyme–Reaction Discovery `arXiv:2611.07757`
   - Agents gather chemical, sequence and database evidence to link enzymes and reactions in both directions.
   - Why it matters: Reports the best retrieval results on public benchmarks and on E. coli tasks built from recent literature.

8. Formalizing Inverse Problems in the Age of AI `arXiv:2611.06962`
   - AI agents autoformalized classical linear regularization theory in Lean as the AutoIP library.
   - Why it matters: Suggests the human role shifts to checking that formal statements match their intent.

9. A Survey of Nonlinear Filtering Methods for Data Assimilation and Generative AI-Enabled Score Filters `arXiv:2611.07160`
   - Reviews the Ensemble Score Filter and 14 extensions for chaotic systems such as weather and wildfire.
   - Why it matters: Connects generative AI to data assimilation, which underlies EO-driven Earth-system forecasting.
