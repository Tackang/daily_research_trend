🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Nowcasting of Tropical Cyclone Wind Fields and Intensity from Geostationary Imagery `arXiv:2611.08255`
   - Simple neural nets predict 2D surface wind fields and storm intensity metrics from geostationary cloud-top imagery.
   - Jointly predicting fields and scalar metrics improves radius-of-maximum-wind estimates during rapid intensification.
   - Why it matters: Near-real-time wind fields without sparse SAR passes, a template for EO-to-physical-variable regression.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
2. Scientist World: Grow a Research World for AI-Scientists `arXiv:2611.08478`
   - Turns papers into executable environments, with 1,000 verified environments released and ~6,400 hours of research traces.
   - Reported: frontier AI scientists beat human baselines in about 74% of cases.
   - Why it matters: A scalable way to benchmark and train AI scientists on real research tasks.

3. SwarmEvolve: Latent Agent Swarms for Evolutionary Discovery and Optimization `arXiv:2611.08483`
   - Cheap agents reason in latent space and share KV caches, with a novelty filter on hidden states.
   - Best average score on 21 math and scientific-computing problems, reaching in 3-8 minutes what others need ~60 minutes for.
   - Why it matters: Shows that discovery search can be cheap and fast with small open models.

4. A Generative Foundation Model for Multimodal Tokamak Tasks `arXiv:2611.08153`
   - GenToka uses a shared conditional diffusion Transformer to handle 20 MAST tasks, including reconstruction and disruption prediction.
   - Cuts normalized RMSE by 4.7-17.7% and raises disruption F1 by 8.4% over TokaMind.
   - Why it matters: Another case of one pretrained model serving heterogeneous scientific signals, which is relevant to multi-sensor EO models.

5. ChemE-as-Code: An Open-Source Platform for Agentic AI in Chemical Engineering `arXiv:2611.07947`
   - Agents use external calculation tools, with Git-versioned files and automated consistency checks.
   - An ammonia case study produced a preliminary engineering package for about $224 in API costs.
   - Why it matters: Tool-grounded agents with automated checks are a practical pattern for scientific workflows.

6. MCPHallu: Benchmarking Reasoning, Execution, and Memory Hallucinations in MCP Agents `arXiv:2611.07689`
   - 358 tasks on 36 MCP servers, evaluating 14 frontier LLM agents.
   - Agents often keep calling tools or claim success when the goal is unreachable.
   - Why it matters: Science agents need to know when to stop.
