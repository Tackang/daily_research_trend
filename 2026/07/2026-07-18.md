🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. Explainable Geospatial AI for Satellite Ground Station Siting Using LiDAR-Derived Terrain Intelligence `arXiv:2607.14127`
   - LightGBM trained on LiDAR-derived clutter heights combined with global land-cover, terrain, and optical remote sensing products achieves MAE 1.79m (R²=0.765), substantially outperforming ITU baseline standards.
   - SHAP analysis identifies tree canopy coverage and spectral reflectance as top predictors, showing open geospatial data can scale interpretable clutter modeling globally.
   - Why it matters: A reusable template—open EO products + explainable ML—that replaces static classification tables in geospatial prediction tasks.

2. Multi-Scale ViT Inference with Habitat-Fit Priors and kNN Retrieval for Multi-Species Plant Identification `arXiv:2607.14509`
   - Fine-tuned DINOv2 ViT-L/14 over multi-scale quadrat image tiles with FAISS kNN retrieval; habitat-fit demotion via geographic and altitude constraints boosts precision over standard classifiers.
   - Temporal fusion across repeated site visits achieves macro-F1 0.44 (3rd place, PlantCLEF 2026); geographic and altitude priors are the single largest contributor.
   - Why it matters: Geospatial habitat priors improve biodiversity monitoring vision models—directly applicable to LULC and ecological change detection.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

3. ReasFlow: Assisting Reasoning-Centric Scientific Discovery in Applied Mathematics via a Knowledge-Based Multi-Agent System `arXiv:2607.14178`
   - Multi-agent framework with automated verification and knowledge retrieval collaborates with human experts to perform complex mathematical derivations, autonomously producing 5 complete research papers from minimal prompts.
   - Validated on the ReasLab platform; the verification agent audits logical coherence before any human review step.
   - Why it matters: AI research automation extends beyond code to formal mathematical theory—AI as first author is arriving.

4. RetroAgent: Harnessing LLMs to Search Over Structured Memory for Agentic Retrosynthesis Planning `arXiv:2607.14512`
   - LLM-based agent with structured memory tracks the full multi-step synthesis route search state—explored routes, alternatives, intermediate properties—rather than scoring candidates in isolation.
   - Outperforms classical tree-search methods on both in-distribution and out-of-distribution chemical synthesis benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: Global-context combinatorial search via LLM agents is now competitive in chemistry; the pattern transfers to any multi-hop scientific planning task.

5. VLT: A Vision-Language-Time Series Multimodal Foundation Model for Industrial Intelligence `arXiv:2607.14510`
   - Bridges temporal signals, frequency spectrograms, and text via a Time-aware Mixture-of-Experts module and Frequency-Text Augmented Learner with gradient alignment for cross-modal conflict resolution.
   - Achieves strong few-shot and noisy-data performance on industrial time-series benchmarks across heterogeneous datasets.
   - Why it matters: The trimodal signal-spectrogram-text fusion architecture is directly transferable to EO time-series interpretation and multispectral satellite data.

6. MemoHarness: Agent Harnesses That Learn from Experience `arXiv:2607.14159`
   - Decomposes LLM agent harnesses into six editable control dimensions and maintains a dual-layer experience bank for test-case-specific adaptation without labels or search overhead.
   - Consistently outperforms static harness configurations across multiple agent benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: Self-tuning agent scaffolding is emerging as a key capability for deploying AI in complex scientific and geospatial workflows.

💡 One-line comment: AI agents are becoming first authors—from formal mathematics to molecular synthesis, autonomous scientific discovery pipelines are shipping in 2026.
