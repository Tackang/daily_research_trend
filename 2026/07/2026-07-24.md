🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. Physics-Aware Complex-Valued State Space Model with Scattering-Prior Feature Modulation for PolSAR Image Classification `arXiv:2607.19787`
   - CV-SSMNet embeds seven polarimetric scattering priors as adaptive modulation signals into a complex-valued SSM, bridging radar physics with long-range spatial modeling for PolSAR classification.
   - Why it matters: Physics-informed inductive bias improves SAR scene understanding without requiring additional labeled data.

2. Delineate Anything v2: A Global Foundation Model for Field Delineation `arXiv:2607.19069`
   - Introduces FBIS-73M (73M field instances, 61 countries) and a foundation model achieving +103% relative mAP; maps all of Ukraine's agricultural fields in 5.4 hours on a consumer workstation.
   - Why it matters: Scalable field boundary delineation directly enables global LULC mapping and carbon accounting at country scale.

3. Multimodal Large Language Models for Remote Sensing: Domain-Specific or General-Purpose? `arXiv:2607.20284`
   - Systematic benchmark shows general-purpose MLLMs frequently match or exceed specialized RS models; spatial reasoning and fine-grained understanding remain key unresolved gaps.
   - Why it matters: Reshapes the RS community's assumption that domain-specific fine-tuning is always the winning strategy.

4. RS-RIE-Bench: Benchmarking Reasoning-Guided Remote Sensing Image Editing `arXiv:2607.20197`
   - First benchmark for language-guided satellite image editing across temporal, causal, and spatial reasoning tasks; best models achieve only ~24% accuracy under strict criteria.
   - Why it matters: Opens a rigorous evaluation frontier for RS generative models and simulation-based data augmentation.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

5. NMR Elucidation as an Agentic Search Problem, Not a Modeling Problem `arXiv:2607.19406`
   - A frozen LLM with domain chemistry tools achieves 71–80% top-1 accuracy on NMR structure elucidation, matching graduate-student chemist performance with zero task-specific training.
   - Why it matters: Tool-augmented agentic search beats specialized end-to-end models at expert scientific tasks — a widely transferable AI-for-science pattern.

6. Reading and Steering Representations of Materials-Science Mechanisms in an Open-Weight LLM `arXiv:2607.20058`
   - Jacobian probes and causal interventions on Gemma-4-E4B-it reveal that physical relationships are encoded more clearly in state transitions than absolute hidden states, confirming genuine physics reasoning.
   - Why it matters: Mechanistic interpretability of science LLMs enables controllable, auditable AI-driven materials discovery.

7. MOF-Sleuth: Tool-Grounded Reward Alignment for Explainable Fine-Grained MOF CIF Auditing `arXiv:2607.19935`
   - RL-trained LLM combined with deterministic chemical analysis detects and explains errors in metal-organic framework crystallographic data, outperforming baselines by coupling evidence to explanation quality.
   - Why it matters: Trustworthy AI auditing of structural databases is foundational infrastructure for any materials discovery pipeline.

💡 One-line comment: Tool-augmented agentic AI is overtaking specialized fine-tuning — from NMR elucidation to RS image reasoning, "search over actions with domain tools" is the emerging paradigm across both science and geospatial AI.
