🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ Predicting Viticulture Potential through an Ensemble of U-Net and a Geospatial Foundation Model `arXiv:2607.08449`
   - Combines U-Net segmentation with Prithvi-2.0 (HLS Landsat/Sentinel-pretrained geospatial FM) for wine-growing suitability prediction in Southern France; ranked 2nd among 7 teams at ImageCLEF AI4Agri 2026 with ±1 accuracy of 68.32.
   - Demonstrates that geospatial FMs pretrained on Landsat+Sentinel data transfer competitively to fine-grained agricultural land assessment with minimal adaptation.
   - Why it matters: Direct evidence that HLS-pretrained FMs generalize to agricultural suitability mapping — a near-identical transfer scenario to LULC and biomass tasks.

2. 3D Reconstruction of Deciduous Trees via Low-Cost UAV Photogrammetry for Shoot Elongation Monitoring `arXiv:2607.07905`
   - Consumer-grade UAVs (<250g) achieve 5–6 mm point-cloud accuracy with 92–98% canopy completeness over a full growing season, enabling primary shoot elongation measurement at tree-canopy scale.
   - Introduces a skeletonization-based pipeline validated via 3D-printed ground-truth branches for structural shoot extraction.
   - Why it matters: Scalable, low-cost UAV phenology monitoring closes the gap between ground inventory and satellite-based vegetation dynamics models.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

3. AI-Integrated Models for Assessing Agricultural Resilience `arXiv:2607.07759`
   - Bridges GTAP (global economic) and APSIM (crop biophysical) simulation models through an AI natural-language interface, enabling non-expert stakeholders to probe cross-domain food supply chain disruptions.
   - Framework captures cascading shocks across biological and economic subsystems via a unified conversational query layer.
   - Why it matters: AI as a cross-disciplinary bridge between Earth system models is a template applicable to carbon–climate–land-use coupled modeling.

4. Context Graphs for Proactive Enterprise Agents `arXiv:2607.07721`
   - Introduces a live relational Context Graph (Delta Detection Engine + Proactivity Scorer + LLM Surfacing Layer via Claude API) enabling agents to surface insights without user queries.
   - Achieves 0.83 Precision@5 and reduces notification latency from 47 min to <30s across three enterprise scenarios.
   - Why it matters: Proactive push-based agent architectures are the natural evolution for monitoring-heavy scientific workflows like satellite data pipelines.

5. Infinity-Parser2 Technical Report `arXiv:2607.07836`
   - Pairs a scalable synthetic data engine (Infinity-Doc2-5M: 5M bilingual samples) with multi-task RL across 8 document tasks (tables, formulas, charts, layout, QA); achieves SOTA 87.6% olmOCR-Bench.
   - Flash variant delivers 3.68× throughput over Pro while maintaining competitive accuracy across all tasks.
   - Why it matters: Unified RL over heterogeneous perception tasks is a generalizable foundation model training recipe beyond the document domain.

6. From Prompts to Contracts: Harness Engineering for Auditable Enterprise LLM Agents `arXiv:2607.08028`
   - Relocates LLM agent behavior guarantees from prompts into versioned code manifests, schemas, and validation artifacts; code-owned harness achieves 120/120 vs. 88/120 for prompt-only guardrails across three LLMs.
   - Provides a reusable engineering pattern for building production-ready, source-grounded, traceable agent systems on Korean corporate data.
   - Why it matters: Auditable agent contracts are a required primitive as AI-driven workflows enter regulated science and government sectors.

💡 One-line comment: Geospatial foundation models are proving their transfer value across agricultural domains, while agent engineering matures from prompt intuition to formal, code-owned behavioral contracts.
