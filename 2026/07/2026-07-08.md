🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ A Harmonised Dataset for Earth System Foundation Models `arXiv:2607.03298`
   - WorldTensor unifies hundreds of remote sensing, climate reanalysis, land-use, emissions, and socioeconomic variables into a standardized spatial-temporal grid for training Earth system foundation models.
   - Why it matters: Provides the multimodal pretraining scaffold that Landsat/EO foundation models have been missing — climate + land + society in one machine-learning-ready resource.

2. MambaRefine-CD: MambaVision with Region-Boundary Temporal Refinement `arXiv:2607.04403`
   - Dual-stream MambaVision encoder for binary change detection in remote sensing, with separate decoders for region localization and boundary refinement via adaptive feature pyramids.
   - Why it matters: Mamba-based architecture rivals transformers for satellite change detection while avoiding quadratic attention cost.

3. Aircraft Detection in Satellite Imagery using Deep Learning Object Detectors `arXiv:2607.02699`
   - Combines Gabor filtering for noise reduction with YOLOv11 for aircraft detection in satellite imagery, achieving mAP 95%, precision 97%, and F1-score 91%.
   - Why it matters: Demonstrates that signal pre-processing + modern detection heads can bridge satellite image quality limitations for high-accuracy operational use.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

4. SwarmResearch: Orchestrating Coding Agents for Open-Ended Discovery `arXiv:2607.02807`
   - A Shepherd Agent steers a swarm of Search Agents operating on independent code branches, promoting exploratory diversity over single-path convergence in optimization tasks.
   - Why it matters: Practical multi-agent blueprint for automated scientific discovery that outperforms existing LLM-guided and single-agent search approaches.

5. Language Models Guide Symbolic Equation Discovery by Controlling Search `arXiv:2607.04156`
   - LLM-PySR delegates variable/operator selection to LLMs and fitting/enumeration to symbolic regression (PySR), validated on 74 AI-Feynman physics equations with strong accuracy-complexity tradeoffs.
   - Why it matters: Combines LLM intuition with symbolic regression rigor — a promising path to interpretable physical law discovery from Earth observation data.

6. Folding, Reasoning, and Scaling with Open-source Drug Discovery Engine `arXiv:2607.03787`
   - OpenDDE uses co-folding as a shared structural reasoning layer for sequence-structure-function modeling, open-sourcing training code, inference tools, and benchmarks for biomolecular AI.
   - Why it matters: Open-source foundation model ecosystem for science — the infrastructure philosophy is directly analogous to what the EO community needs for large-scale pretraining.
