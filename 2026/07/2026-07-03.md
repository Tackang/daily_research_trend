🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ Uncertainty-Aware Tree Height Change Regression `arXiv:2607.00638`
   - Introduces a canopy height change dataset (>10,500 km², PlanetScope imagery, Spain) with spatially-resolved uncertainty labels, framing continuous height change regression as a new task for Geospatial Foundation Models (GFMs).
   - Benchmarks state-of-the-art GFMs on fine-tuning for this problem, revealing remaining gaps in uncertainty-aware biophysical variable retrieval.
   - Why it matters: Directly advances GFM evaluation for carbon-critical forest monitoring — core methodology for Landsat-scale foundation model downstream tasks.

2. GeoSearcher: Anchor-Guided Progressive Reasoning for RS Visual Grounding `arXiv:2607.01050`
   - Reformulates remote sensing visual grounding as anchor-centric progressive reasoning combining location, relational, and attribute cues; process-aware RL refines localization of small targets in cluttered high-resolution satellite scenes.
   - Why it matters: Structured chain-of-thought reasoning in VLMs substantially outperforms direct-answer models on fine-grained geospatial referring tasks.

3. Polarimetric SAR Soil Moisture Retrieval over Heterogeneous Mine Terrain `arXiv:2607.00294`
   - Enhanced TU Wien SSM index adapted for ALOS-2 PALSAR-2 quad-pol data over a Finnish limestone quarry; best semi-empirical model achieves R²=0.67, RMSE=5.65% with sediment-specific temporal features.
   - Why it matters: Physics-based SAR retrieval remains competitive in data-scarce, complex terrain settings where supervised deep learning lacks sufficient training samples.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

4. DiscoPER: Autonomous Scientific Discovery via Iterative Meta-Reflection `arXiv:2607.01131`
   - LLM-based agent (DiscoPER) performs hypothesis-free, open-ended dataset exploration with statistical validation; a second-order meta-reflection layer synthesizes patterns across accumulated findings to guide deeper inquiry.
   - Recovered 8/9 known ecological patterns on the iNatDisco benchmark (72.7% hypothesis support) without predefined research questions.
   - Why it matters: First system to demonstrate open-ended scientific inquiry with genuine meta-level reasoning — a step toward AI co-scientists.

5. Agentic Verifiable Rules for Self-Expanding Reaction Classification `arXiv:2607.01061`
   - Multi-agent LLM pipeline autonomously expands chemical reaction taxonomy from 68 to 14,073 classes across 665K patent reactions, achieving 97.7% accuracy while matching a leading proprietary classifier.
   - Why it matters: Agentic rule generation + symbolic validation is a generalizable recipe for AI-driven, self-expanding knowledge formalization in any scientific domain.

6. Graph-PRefLexOR: Graph-Native RL for Traceable Scientific Hypothesis Generation `arXiv:2607.00924`
   - Combines reinforcement learning with graph-structured reasoning phases for materials science hypothesis generation; achieves 40–65% performance gains over base models with ~2–3× greater semantic diversity.
   - Why it matters: Adds interpretability and auditability to AI hypothesis generation — a prerequisite for trustworthy AI-for-science pipelines.
