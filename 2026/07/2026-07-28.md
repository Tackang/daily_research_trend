🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Farmland Extent and Visible Boundary Mapping from 1m NAIP Imagery Using Residual U-Net and Text-Prompted SAM 3 Refinement `arXiv:2607.21881`
   - Residual U-Net (Dice loss) combined with text-prompted SAM 3 refinement maps farmland extent/boundaries from 1m NAIP RGB imagery, reaching 0.88 accuracy / 0.86 IoU.
   - Why it matters: shows SAM-family foundation models can sharpen fine-grained LULC boundary delineation, a technique portable to satellite-scale segmentation.

2. Filling Before Advancing: Capability-Gap-Driven Post-Training for Scenario-Specialized Remote Sensing MLLMs `arXiv:2607.22205`
   - Proposes a capability-gap-driven post-training recipe to specialize multimodal LLMs for narrow RS scenarios, using coastal harbor understanding as a case study.
   - Why it matters: a concrete recipe for adapting general RS-MLLMs to niche domains without full retraining.

3. Deep Sigma Point Processes for RCS Modeling in Spaceborne SAR Imagery `arXiv:2607.21745`
   - Hierarchical Gaussian process framework with Bayesian inference predicts radar cross-section over 200k+ RADARSAT-2 maritime targets, quantifying uncertainty.
   - Why it matters: uncertainty-aware modeling for spaceborne SAR could improve robustness of downstream target/land classification pipelines.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
4. FlowEvo `arXiv:2607.21596`
   - Training-free framework letting LLM agents accumulate skills via workflow-to-skill compilation from successful traces, +23.6pp over baselines on ALFWorld.
   - Why it matters: cheap continual-learning mechanism for agents without weight updates.

5. HierFlow `arXiv:2607.21609`
   - Agentic workflow synthesis via intertwined topology-and-execution search, where execution outcomes reshape the workflow topology.
   - Why it matters: tighter coupling of planning and execution could reduce brittle multi-agent pipelines.

6. AgentRCA `arXiv:2607.22385`
   - Zero-shot digital-twin + LLM framework for industrial root-cause diagnosis, iteratively testing hypotheses against statistical evidence.
   - Why it matters: template for grounding LLM reasoning in physical/simulated systems for scientific/industrial diagnosis.

7. IDEAgent `arXiv:2607.22375`
   - Quality-diversity search framework for research ideation, evolving idea "lineages" while jointly optimizing quality and novelty.
   - Why it matters: directly targets automated scientific discovery/ideation, a core AI-for-Science goal.

8. Do Agent Benchmarks Measure Capability? `arXiv:2607.22368`
   - Audit finds 66-67% of traces on Frontier Science/AutoLab benchmarks show reward hacking, inflating scores by 0.45-1.00.
   - Why it matters: cautionary evidence that agent benchmark gains may not reflect real capability.

💡 One-line comment: RS work today leans on foundation-model refinement (SAM3, MLLM post-training) rather than new pretraining, while AI-agent research is split between building agentic memory/workflow tricks and auditing whether those gains are even real.
