🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ HLS-GPT: GPT for Landsat+Sentinel-2 Reflectance Reconstruction `arXiv:2606.18115`
   - Reconstructs all HLS spectral bands (incl. red-edge) for any date from 12-month pixel time series; outperforms NASA Prithvi with <0.026 RMSE at 50% masking, trained on 250k+ U.S. pixels.
   - Why it matters: GPT-style temporal gap-filling for HLS directly targets the cloud-contamination problem at the heart of Landsat foundation model pretraining.

2. TerraDiT-Ω: Unified Spatial Control for Satellite Image Synthesis with Any Geospatial Primitive `arXiv:2606.31029`
   - Geometry-Aware Local Attention enables satellite image generation conditioned on any vector input (polygon/polyline/bbox/point); improves land-cover segmentation, object detection, and scene classification via synthetic augmentation.
   - Why it matters: Scalable synthetic data pipeline for RS tasks across annotation budgets — useful for data-hungry pretraining.

3. Self-Supervised Tree-level Biomass Estimation in Urban Environments from LiDAR and Optical Data `arXiv:2606.26194`
   - Label-free deep learning framework maps 90,726 trees across 810 km² (Ontario), estimating ~850 Gg C total carbon; detects +39 Gg C net gain over 2018–2023 with a public bitemporal crown-level AGB database.
   - Why it matters: Demonstrates scalable self-supervised urban carbon monitoring at management-relevant resolution.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

4. Agentic-Ideation: Sample Efficient Agentic Trajectories Synthesis for Scientific Ideation Agents `arXiv:2606.31229`
   - Oracle-guided multi-agent synthesis achieves 11.91% quality gain and >10× data efficiency over workflow-based LLM ideation; workflow masking forces models to learn general decision logic.
   - Why it matters: Dramatically lowers training cost for agentic AI driving automated scientific hypothesis generation.

5. DDIAgents: Mechanism-Conditioned Context Flow for Drug-Drug Interaction Prediction `arXiv:2606.31085`
   - Mechanism-aware planner routes heterogeneous biomedical evidence to specialized expert agents then aggregates; outperforms graph-based and LLM baselines with interpretable, mechanism-conditioned outputs.
   - Why it matters: Transferable multi-agent knowledge-routing pattern for complex scientific prediction.

6. Joint Discovery of Governing PDEs from Multi-Source Datasets by Competitive Optimization `arXiv:2606.30699`
   - MCO-PDE trains per-dataset neural surrogates, applies soft-competitive weighting and genetic algorithms to identify shared governing equations from heterogeneous sparse real-world data.
   - Why it matters: Automated physical law discovery applicable to multi-sensor earth science observations.
