📡 [2026-07-09] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ Phase-Preserving Trimodal Transformer for Tropical Forest Biomass Estimation Using Optical and PolInSAR Data `arXiv:2607.03663`
   - TCCT fuses Landsat-5 optical with complex-valued P- and L-band PolInSAR via co-attention that dynamically de-weights cloud-contaminated optical pixels; achieves RMSE 3.78 m for canopy height in the Amazon basin.
   - Meets ESA BIOMASS mission <20% error requirement, proving Landsat+SAR multimodal fusion is ready for operational continent-scale carbon stock mapping.
   - Why it matters: Direct blueprint for extending a Landsat foundation model to biomass/carbon downstream tasks using PolInSAR as a complementary modality.

2. GeoSAM-Lite: A Lightweight Foundation Model for Onboard Remote Sensing Segmentation `arXiv:2607.03760`
   - Reduces SAM parameters by 92.8% via geospatial domain pre-training and feature-fusion layers, enabling onboard satellite deployment. Validated on cloud detection benchmarks at competitive accuracy.
   - Why it matters: RS foundation models moving to the edge—real-time onboard inference opens disaster-response use cases infeasible with ground-side processing.

3. ⭐ GlacierCastAI: Predicting Glacier Retreat from Multi-Modal Satellite Imagery and Climate Signals `arXiv:2607.04117`
   - ResNet50+ConvLSTM fuses Landsat time-series, ERA5 reanalysis, and terrain data across five climate zones to forecast glacier boundary retreat; ERA5 alone recovers 98% of imagery-only accuracy.
   - Why it matters: Demonstrates Landsat temporal stacks as a backbone for multi-modal climate forecasting—a compelling downstream task for Landsat foundation models.

4. Recovering Cloud Microstructures with Cascaded Diffusion Inversion `arXiv:2607.05637`
   - Two-stage diffusion SR pipeline delivers 4× resolution enhancement on multi-spectral geostationary satellite cloud imagery; stage-one uses paired real data, stage-two self-supervised learning.
   - Why it matters: Higher-fidelity cloud microstructure data improves both numerical weather model inputs and cloud-seeding operation accuracy.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

5. FirstResearch: Auditable Question Formation for LLM Scientific Discovery Agents `arXiv:2607.05682`
   - Structures LLM-generated research outputs into Research Question Certificates (definitions, assumptions, mechanisms, testable hypotheses), scored 4.86/5 vs. 4.38/5 for best baseline across 10 research topics.
   - Why it matters: Auditability is the missing link making AI-generated hypotheses trustworthy enough for domain scientists to act on.

6. Prompt-to-Paper: Agentic AI System for Bioinformatics `arXiv:2607.05456`
   - Three-agent pipeline: section-aware RAG over 60–100 verified papers + autonomous coding agent running real experiments + 8-dimensional iterative quality scorer. Mean +17.96 score gain; 7.0/10 human rating.
   - Why it matters: First end-to-end research agent grounded in real experiments, not synthetic outputs—raises the bar for reproducibility in automated science.

7. Danus: Orchestrating Mathematical Reasoning Agents with Fact-Graph Memory `arXiv:2607.06447`
   - Coordinator + parallel proof-search workers share a fact-graph of verified claims and logical connections, enabling incremental construction of long formal arguments. Validated on six research-level problems in algebraic geometry and combinatorics.
   - Why it matters: Fact-graph shared memory solves multi-agent state coordination at proof scale—a pattern directly extensible to complex scientific reasoning tasks.
