🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ A Framework for Integrating Spatiotemporal Deep Learning Methods with Landsat for Annual Land Cover and Impervious Surface Mapping
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115347
   - LCAMS uses a two-stage Landsat-based DL architecture — spatial models extract per-year spectral features, temporal models enforce inter-annual consistency — producing annual 30 m land cover and impervious surface maps across the US. Piloted by USGS as the backbone for the new Annual NLCD with region-specific fine-tuning to handle diverse landscapes.
   - Why it matters: First operational proof that Landsat annual time-series deep learning scales to national land cover production — sets the standard for continental FM benchmarking.

2. ⭐ Spectral and Spatial Harmonization of Landsat ETM+, OLI, and Sentinel-2 MSI Using Time-Series Harmonic Analysis and Blur Kernel Estimation
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115384
   - Corrects spectral and spatial mismatches among Landsat ETM+, OLI, and Sentinel-2 MSI via harmonic regression and blur kernel estimation, enabling seamless multi-sensor fusion for long-term time series. Validated across diverse biomes; yields substantially more consistent spectral indices than prior cross-sensor methods.
   - Why it matters: Reliable Landsat–Sentinel-2 harmonization is the critical preprocessing foundation for any multi-decadal LULC or biomass change analysis.

3. Forest Connectivity Boosts Carbon Recovery in Regenerating Atlantic Forests
   - Journal: Communications Earth & Environment (Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1038/s43247-026-03480-5
   - Landscape connectivity amplifies aboveground carbon accumulation by 43–69% in regenerating Brazilian Atlantic Forest; highly connected patches gain >3× more carbon than fragmented ones, based on remote sensing + space-for-time substitution. Full protection of 2020 secondary forests could add 132 Tg C (+35%) by 2030.
   - Why it matters: First RS-scale quantification of connectivity as a carbon multiplier — actionable evidence for REDD+ landscape design.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

4. ⭐ Generating an Annual 30 m Rice Cover Product for Monsoon Asia (2018–2023) Using Harmonized Landsat and Sentinel-2 Data and the NASA-IBM Geospatial Foundation Model
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115256
   - Fine-tunes NASA-IBM Prithvi on HLS (harmonized Landsat + Sentinel-2) with auto-generated regional rice labels for continental-scale rice mapping across Monsoon Asia; 83.04% OA across four climatic zones (2018–2023). Outperforms MODIS- and SAR-based benchmarks with strong annual temporal consistency.
   - Why it matters: Direct proof that HLS-pretrained Prithvi generalizes to large-scale crop monitoring — same pipeline transferable to Landsat foundation models.

5. ⭐ AgriFM: A Multi-source Temporal Remote Sensing Foundation Model for Agriculture Mapping
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115234
   - Pre-trains on 25M+ globally sampled images from MODIS (250/500 m), Landsat-8/9 (30 m), and Sentinel-2 (10/20 m) with global land cover supervision; achieves strong transfer to crop type mapping tasks with minimal labeled data. First FM jointly bridging coarse-to-fine satellite resolutions across MODIS, Landsat, and Sentinel-2.
   - Why it matters: Multi-scale joint pre-training across MODIS–Landsat–Sentinel-2 directly informs Landsat-centric FM architecture design.

6. Bridging Semantics and Geometry: A Decoupled LVLM–SAM Framework for Reasoning Segmentation in Optical Remote Sensing
   - Journal: ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2026.04.036
   - Trains an LVLM prompter via mask-only GRPO reinforcement learning to output structured geometric prompts that drive a frozen SAM backbone, enabling zero-shot text-guided delineation without SAM fine-tuning. Achieves competitive RS reasoning segmentation results while preserving SAM's general spatial capabilities.
   - Why it matters: Merging LLM semantic reasoning with SAM's spatial precision advances the path toward open-vocabulary RS segmentation agents.
