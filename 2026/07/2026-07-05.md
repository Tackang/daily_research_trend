🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. Spatial Support Matters: Geometry-Aware Graph Fusion for Rainfall Field Reconstruction `arXiv:2607.01621`
   - Geometry-aware GNN fuses point gauges, microwave links, and satellite/radar grid products via cross-support messaging; achieves 23.2% RMSE improvement over IDW on Singapore urban data.
   - Fusion gains are largest where gauge density is low relative to the rainfall field's spatial correlation length.
   - Why it matters: Multi-sensor EO data fusion for hydrological nowcasting—bridges spaceborne rainfall products with ground sensor networks.

2. SIMBA: Bidirectional Retrieval-Forward Simulation for FY-4A GIIRS Hyperspectral IR Radiances `arXiv:2606.19943`
   - Cycle-consistent deep learning jointly optimizes atmospheric profile retrieval and forward radiance simulation from the FY-4A/GIIRS satellite hyperspectral IR instrument.
   - Shared latent space enforces physical consistency across both tasks, improving NWP temperature and humidity vertical profiles.
   - Why it matters: Cycle-consistency is an underused but powerful design for satellite hyperspectral data assimilation in operational weather systems.

3. Storm Track Self-Reinforcement Through Cloud Radiative Effects `arXiv:2607.02213`
   - Satellite observations and aquaplanet simulations show cloud radiative heating independently sustains Southern Hemisphere storm tracks through summer when solar baroclinic forcing weakens.
   - Explains anomalously active storm seasons in satellite EO records that conventional models underpredict.
   - Why it matters: A space-detectable cloud-climate feedback with direct implications for EO-based seasonal forecast verification.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

4. Diverse Evidence, Better Forecasts: Multi-Agent Deliberation Under Information Asymmetry `arXiv:2607.01661`
   - InfoDelphi assigns private evidence subsets to each LLM agent; rationale-driven deliberation and confidence-weighted merging yield 12–18% Brier score improvement on 375 forecasting questions.
   - Information diversity among agents—not agent count—is the primary driver of multi-agent performance gains.
   - Why it matters: Core design principle for multi-agent EO hypothesis generation and scientific forecasting systems.

5. Autonomous Discovery of Traffic Laws with AI Traffic Scientists `arXiv:2607.01639`
   - TrafficSci's critic-judge agentic loop rediscovers 3 established traffic laws and uncovers 1 new temporal memory law, validated across 8 cities and 2 independent trajectory datasets.
   - Iterative evidence scoping and observational-interventional validation keep the discovery process auditable and reproducible.
   - Why it matters: First agentic system to autonomously discover a new physical law from real-world data—a direct blueprint for AI-driven EO scientific discovery.

6. Coding-Agents Can Replicate Scientific Machine Learning Papers `arXiv:2607.02134`
   - Agentic replication workflow reconstructs methods and verifies 158 computational claims across 4 ML papers with full match; separates genuine algorithmic gains from evaluation artifacts.
   - Exposes which claims fail on held-out benchmarks, systematizing automated reproducibility checking at scale.
   - Why it matters: Scalable automated reproducibility for AI4Science—critical for vetting the expanding literature on EO foundation models.
