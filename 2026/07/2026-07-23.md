🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ Now We Know? A Systematic Comparison of TerraMind and THOR `arXiv:2607.18504`
   - ESA's two geospatial foundation models compared across 10 tasks: THOR uses variable-patch tokenization with Sentinel-1/2/3 at native resolution; TerraMind employs multimodal generative dual-scale pretraining enabling any-to-any cross-modal inference.
   - Patch size and decoder type explain more performance variance than model identity—architectural choices dominate over scale.
   - Why it matters: First controlled head-to-head study of leading satellite FMs; direct design signal for Landsat foundation model architecture decisions.

2. STS-NET: Self-Supervised Crop Stress Detection via Satellite Time Series `arXiv:2607.18791`
   - Self-supervised 3D-CNN autoencoder on PlanetScope SITS (NDVI/GNDVI/RECI/NDRE) detects water stress (97.98% precision), nitrogen stress (85.08%), and combined stress in sugarcane without manual stress labels.
   - Temporal self-supervision over a full-year acquisition window replaces per-class annotation overhead.
   - Why it matters: Validates self-supervised SITS pretraining as a scalable path for fine-grained crop stress monitoring from satellite time series.

3. Incomplete Observations Boost Evolutionary Performance in Ocean Modeling `arXiv:2607.19147`
   - Generative state-space model (continuous hidden Markov + EM optimization) trained directly on sparse, noisy FY-3D satellite observations—no complete reanalysis fields required.
   - Sparse satellite inputs directly improve ocean-state dynamics representation beyond baselines that rely on dense reanalysis data.
   - Why it matters: Challenges the assumption that complete data is necessary; training on raw incomplete satellite observations can outperform reanalysis-dependent Earth system models.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

4. Engineering Trustworthy Agentic AI for Critical Systems `arXiv:2607.18548`
   - Comprehensive survey mapping trustworthiness dimensions (safety, robustness, transparency, accountability, security) to deployed AI agents in engineering domains via a layered failure-mode-to-mitigation framework.
   - Why it matters: Practical blueprint for deploying autonomous agents in high-stakes scientific and Earth observation pipelines.

5. PEARL: Solver-in-the-Loop Interactive Optimization Modeling `arXiv:2607.18256`
   - LLM agents iteratively formulate, execute, debug, and revise optimization problems through repeated solve-revise cycles with Python execution and solver feedback; substantially outperforms single-shot prompting.
   - Why it matters: AI-in-the-loop optimization is directly applicable to calibration and inverse problems in remote sensing (e.g., radiative transfer inversion, biomass retrieval).

💡 One-line comment: Patch size beats model identity for satellite FM performance—and sparse, incomplete observations may be sufficient (even beneficial) for training Earth system AI.
