📡 [2026-07-30] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. RRS-10K: A Multitask Vision-Language Model Benchmark for Rare Remote Sensing Image Interpretation `arXiv:2607.24810`
   - New benchmark of 10,738 rare/military-related RS images across 20 tasks; evaluates 52 VLMs.
   - Why it matters: exposes how badly current RS-VLMs fail on long-tail scenes, a real deployment gap.

2. RSMeM: Knowledge-Enhanced Memory Evolution for Remote Sensing Agents with Systematic Evaluation `arXiv:2607.24772`
   - Adds hierarchical knowledge grounding + failure-aware experience refinement to RS agents; +6% accuracy on DeepSeek-V3.2 on EarthBench.
   - Why it matters: shows domain-specific memory, not just bigger models, drives RS agent reliability.

3. OpenPVMapper: A Multi-source, Nationwide Database of Rooftop Photovoltaic Systems in France `arXiv:2607.25153`
   - Combines aerial-imagery deep learning with OSM to map 1.14M rooftop PV systems (~15GW) at 74-75% precision.
   - Why it matters: template for fusing multiple imperfect sources into a usable national-scale RS product.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
4. Matryoshka Agent: Unfolding Sub-Agents for Long-Horizon Machine Learning Engineering `arXiv:2607.25090`
   - Orchestrator + sub-agent hierarchy lets small models (Qwen3-4B) match o4-mini on long-horizon ML engineering tasks.
   - Why it matters: hierarchical decomposition is emerging as the fix for long-context agent degradation.

5. Towards an Agent Operating System - Lessons from Classical and Cloud OS `arXiv:2607.25076`
   - Argues agentic AI needs POSIX/Kubernetes-style standardized abstractions for planning, tools, memory, and collaboration.
   - Why it matters: signals the field moving from ad-hoc agent frameworks toward infrastructure consolidation.

6. Towards Robust Reinforcement Learning for Small-Scale Language Model Agents `arXiv:2607.25091`
   - Identifies PEFT/LoRA freezing, bfloat16 overflow, and reward-collapse failure modes in RL-tuned 70-500M agents; fixes stabilize training.
   - Why it matters: practical fixes for making cheap small-model agents trainable without large-model compute.

💡 One-line comment: today's crop is less about new benchmarks and more about making agents (RS and general) actually reliable — memory, hierarchy, and standardized infrastructure over raw scale.
