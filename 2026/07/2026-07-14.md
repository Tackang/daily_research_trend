🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ Is Sub-Metre Resolution Necessary for Cocoa Mapping? `arXiv:2607.08945`
   - Benchmarks 0.5m Pleiades VHR vs 10m Sentinel-2 and foundation model embeddings (TESSERA, AlphaEarth) on cocoa detection in Côte d'Ivoire across 2,821 reference points. VHR tops at F1=0.92; TESSERA embeddings reach F1=0.86 — competitive without costly high-res acquisition.
   - Why it matters: Geospatial FM embeddings can substitute expensive VHR imagery for large-area LULC mapping, directly validating the scalability case for satellite FM approaches.

2. Promptable Concept Segmentation from Above: Evaluating SAM 3 for Remote Sensing `arXiv:2607.09583`
   - Benchmarks SAM 3 on EO scene classification, object detection, and instance segmentation under strict zero-shot and one-shot constraints. Visual prompts outperform text prompts due to overhead semantic misalignment.
   - Why it matters: First systematic SAM 3 evaluation on remote sensing — sets the baseline for next-gen EO segmentation foundation models.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

3. Toward Auditable AI Scientists: A Hypothesis Evolution Protocol for LLM Agents `arXiv:2607.09195`
   - Introduces HEP, a framework that makes LLM-agent scientific reasoning auditable via explicit hypothesis-test-evidence-belief cycles. Demonstrated on materials science tasks with superior generalization over planning-only baselines.
   - Why it matters: Adds transparency and rigor to agentic discovery workflows — a key step toward reproducible AI-driven science.

4. Rethinking Scientific Discovery in the Agentic Era `arXiv:2607.03863`
   - Presents SCION, a multi-agent scientific OS that unifies tasks, tools, agents, and memory through a Research Execution Plan. Applied to materials analysis, molecular design, and protein screening.
   - Why it matters: Blueprint for full multi-agent research infrastructure — moves well beyond single-agent demo papers.

5. GATS: Graph-Augmented Tree Search with Layered World Models `arXiv:2607.08894`
   - Combines UCB1 tree search with a 3-layer world model (symbolic / statistical / LLM) achieving 100% task success and zero LLM calls at inference, versus LATS at 92% with 37 LLM calls per task.
   - Why it matters: Decouples planning intelligence from inference cost — critical for deploying autonomous agents at scale.

6. ARCANA: Reflective Multi-Agent Framework for ARC-AGI-2 `arXiv:2607.09059`
   - Four specialized agents (perception → hypothesis generation → symbolic execution → reflective refinement) collaborate to solve ARC-AGI-2 abstract reasoning tasks within strict compute budgets.
   - Why it matters: Structured multi-agent reflection beats monolithic LLM approaches on hard compositional reasoning — a pattern transferable to scientific problem-solving pipelines.

💡 One-line comment: Foundation model embeddings are closing the gap with costly VHR satellite data, while the AI-for-science field pivots from single-agent demos to full multi-agent research operating systems.
