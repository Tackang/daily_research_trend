📚 [2026-07-06] Weekly Journal Digest (past 7 days Early Access)

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ Prithvi-EO-2.0: A Versatile Multi-Temporal Foundation Model for Earth Observation Applications
   - Journal: IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing (Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1109/TGRS.2025.3642610
   - Multi-temporal vision transformer pretrained at scale on a decade of NASA Harmonized Landsat Sentinel-2 (HLS) data at 30 m, with spatial/temporal attention and location-time metadata encoding; fine-tuned across disaster response, land cover, crop, and ecosystem tasks.
   - Why it matters: The closest published analog to a dedicated Landsat foundation model — 30 m HLS pretraining at scale is the exact paradigm central to this research direction.

2. ⭐ AgriFM: A Multi-Source Temporal Remote Sensing Foundation Model for Agriculture Mapping
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Vol. 334, Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115234
   - Pretrained on 25M+ global samples from MODIS, Landsat-8/9, and Sentinel-2 time series; first multi-source temporal FM for agricultural LULC tasks; achieves SOTA on winter wheat, rice, and cropland segmentation across heterogeneous geographies.
   - Why it matters: Demonstrates that multi-mission Landsat/Sentinel temporal pretraining transfers powerfully to domain-specific LULC tasks — direct design validation for a Landsat FM.

3. ⭐ Generating an Annual 30m Rice Cover Product for Monsoon Asia (2018–2023) Using Harmonized Landsat and Sentinel-2 Data and the NASA-IBM Geospatial Foundation Model
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Vol. 335)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115256
   - First continental-scale application of the Prithvi-1.0 geospatial foundation model for rice monitoring using HLS data across Monsoon Asia; outperforms CNN/LSTM baselines in transferability over fragmented smallholder farmlands.
   - Why it matters: Empirically validates that 30 m Landsat-based FMs generalize to continental-scale crop mapping — a key benchmark for the Landsat FM agenda.

4. ⭐ Spectral and Spatial Harmonization of Landsat ETM+, OLI, and Sentinel-2 MSI Using Time-Series Harmonic Analysis and Blur Kernel Estimation
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Vol. 339)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115384
   - Combines harmonic time-series decomposition with blur kernel estimation to achieve spectral-radiometric consistency across Landsat ETM+, OLI, and Sentinel-2 MSI, enabling seamless multi-decadal analysis stacks.
   - Why it matters: Solves a core preprocessing bottleneck for training Landsat/Sentinel foundation models on multi-mission, multi-decade archives.

5. Natural Forest Expansion Is a Larger Carbon Sink than Secondary Forests in Moist Tropics
   - Journal: Nature Geoscience (Online: 4 June 2026)
   - DOI: 10.1038/s41561-026-01984-5
   - Satellite forest cover change maps combined with LiDAR biomass estimates reveal that natural forest expansion in moist tropics sequesters 795 ± 132 TgC — 5.4% more than secondary forest regrowth — identifying NFE as an overlooked pan-tropical mitigation pathway.
   - Why it matters: Reframes tropical carbon accounting and highlights the need for finer-scale multi-sensor monitoring of emerging forest carbon sinks.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

6. Multi-Agent AI Systems Need Transparency
   - Journal: Nature Machine Intelligence (2026)
   - DOI: 10.1038/s42256-026-01183-2
   - Perspective arguing that agentic AI frameworks in scientific workflows require explicit motivation, modular transparency, and reproducibility standards; without these, compounded errors, wasted compute, and irreproducible results are likely.
   - Why it matters: Establishes governance standards for AI agent pipelines in science — essential reading for anyone building or deploying multi-agent research systems.
