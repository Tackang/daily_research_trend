🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. LoRetta: A Foundation Model and Extensive Dataset for Global-Scale Remote Sensing Dense Image Matching `arXiv:2608.04106`
   - Reformulates dense image matching as localization-and-registration to handle acquisition time, viewpoint, and resolution gaps; ships LEVIR-GM, 800K+ pairs across six continents (2018-2022, 0.5-1024m).
   - Why it matters: A reusable global-scale correspondence backbone could underpin multi-sensor fusion pipelines feeding Landsat-style foundation models.

2. DefoEye: Python-Based Software for Facilitating Time-Series InSAR Analysis of Sentinel-1 Remote-Sensing Data `arXiv:2608.04915`
   - Open-source GMTSAR wrapper providing an end-to-end TS-InSAR workflow with parallel job execution and interferogram network pruning; validated 2020-2024 across four geologically distinct regions.
   - Why it matters: Lowers the barrier to ground-deformation monitoring, filling a gap left by commercial/restricted InSAR tools.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
3. EviGraph: Evidence-Guided Autonomous Research Agents `arXiv:2608.04738`
   - Represents the research process as a typed evidence graph (Problem→Hypothesis→Experiment→Finding→Claim) to catch unsupported claims and inconsistencies, then repairs the weakest node instead of full re-runs.
   - Why it matters: +40% Claim Support Rate over baselines is a concrete step toward trustworthy autonomous science agents.

4. Improving Auto-Design of Neural PDE Solvers with a Domain-Specific Language `arXiv:2608.04384`
   - Introduces ADSL-PDE, a structured search space between solver concepts and code so LLMs spend search budget on solver quality rather than fixing broken programs.
   - Why it matters: Practical recipe for LLM-driven scientific-computing automation, a core AI-for-Science building block.

5. SafeCommit: Certifying When Memory-Grounded Agents May Safely Act `arXiv:2608.04289`
   - Formalizes "safe commitment under memory uncertainty," adding a risk-controlled layer that checks whether an agent's memory is stale/conflicting before it acts.
   - Why it matters: Addresses a key reliability gap for long-horizon agents used in autonomous research and monitoring pipelines.
