1. OVEarth-Bench: Evaluating Category Breadth and Query Diversity for Open-Vocabulary Earth Observation `arXiv:2607.27278`
   - New benchmark for open-vocabulary EO localization, testing broad hierarchical categories and diverse query types (vocabulary/referring/reasoning) under a unified zero-shot protocol.
   - Why it matters: shows general MLLMs beat EO-specific models on open-vocab tasks — a signal that current EO foundation models still lag general-purpose ones.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
2. DeepResearch Agent System `arXiv:2607.27562`
   - Sparse MoE agent (30B params, 3B active) with dual reasoning modes (ReAct / IterResearch) for multi-step research and tool use; open-sourced.
   - Why it matters: strong scores on Humanity's Last Exam (87.3%) show sparse-activation agents closing the gap with dense frontier models at much lower inference cost.

3. Evaluating Agentic Bioinformatics through Function, Evidence, and Validation `arXiv:2607.27556`
   - Introduces the FEV framework, evaluating bio AI agents by workflow trajectory rather than final answers, surveying 109 agent systems across 6 bioinformatics domains.
   - Why it matters: highlights that scientific agents still lack replayability and provenance — a key gap for trustworthy AI-driven discovery.

4. MECA: A Mechanism-Centered Agent for Constructing Well-Specified and Valuable Mathematical Conjectures `arXiv:2607.27709`
   - Multi-agent framework where explorer agents build mechanisms behind conjectures while critic agents judge validity and research value.
   - Why it matters: concrete step toward AI systems that generate genuinely novel, prover-challenging scientific hypotheses, not just restate known results.

5. Baikal: Structured Search for Deep Research over Data Lakes `arXiv:2607.27726`
   - Frames deep research over large heterogeneous data lakes as a budgeted search problem, clustering evidence into semantic regions to explore efficiently.
   - Why it matters: 28-36% gains over prior methods suggest structured exploration strategies matter as much as model scale for research agents.
