📡 [2026-08-24] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. SED-FOD: Scattering-Aware Expert Decomposition for Few-Shot Cross-Sensor SAR Object Detection `arXiv:2608.18755`
   - Splits SAR features into shared and scattering-specific expert paths to transfer object structure across sensors with few labels.
   - Why it matters: Tackles the chronic SAR domain-shift problem without full retraining per sensor.

2. Bridging Gap Between Labeled and Unlabeled Data via Unified Flow with Feature Memory Bank `arXiv:2608.16681`
   - Semi-supervised RS segmentation combining a visual foundation model with an RS-domain teacher and a shared feature memory bank.
   - Why it matters: Cuts label dependence for large-scale LULC segmentation pipelines.

3. Cross-View Urban Sensing: Mapping Streetscape Perception via AlphaEarth Embeddings `arXiv:2608.16310`
   - Predicts street-level perception purely from satellite embeddings, hitting median R²=0.76 across Southeast Asian cities.
   - Why it matters: Shows satellite-only embeddings can substitute for expensive street-view data.

4. ⭐ AlignJEPA: Predictive Vision-Language Alignment for Remote Sensing Foundation Models `arXiv:2608.15456`
   - JEPA-style framework predicting RS text embeddings from masked visual foundation-model tokens for natural-language archive search.
   - Why it matters: Directly relevant to language-groundable Landsat foundation model work.

5. ⭐ Earth Observation Foundation Models for Terrestrial Ecohydrology `arXiv:2608.15282`
   - Synthesis finding EO foundation model pretraining is still dominated by optical/SAR, under-covering coupled water-energy-carbon signals.
   - Why it matters: Flags a pretraining data gap directly relevant to biomass/carbon-focused FM design.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
6. AI4AI-Bench: Benchmarking LLM Agents in Algorithmic Design for Recursive Self-Improvement `arXiv:2608.20318`
   - Tests whether agents can improve their own training algorithms; best config reaches only 0.250 on a normalized score.
   - Why it matters: Sobering benchmark on real limits of agentic self-improvement.

7. Harness Continual Learning: Continual Adaptation Beyond Model Parameters `arXiv:2608.19013`
   - Keeps the foundation model frozen and evolves the surrounding prompts/memory/tools "harness" instead.
   - Why it matters: A parameter-free alternative to continual fine-tuning worth watching for FM deployment.

8. OmniScientist: An Omni-Modal Omni-Discipline AI Scientist `arXiv:2608.13558`
   - End-to-end research system operating directly on raw images, signals, audio, video, and 3D structures across disciplines.
   - Why it matters: Pushes AI-scientist systems beyond text-only literature mining.

9. Intern-S2-Preview: Scientific Agentic Foundation Model `arXiv:2608.13505`
   - Multimodal scientific FM trained via SFT plus scalable multi-task and agentic RL.
   - Why it matters: A concrete open model bridging general FM training with science-agent capability.

💡 One-line comment: RS foundation-model papers are converging on language-alignment and multimodal pretraining gaps, while AI-for-Science work is quietly admitting agentic self-improvement still barely works.
