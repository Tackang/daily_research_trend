🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ Generating an annual 30 m rice cover product for monsoon Asia (2018–2023) using harmonized Landsat and Sentinel-2 data and the NASA-IBM geospatial foundation model
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115256
   - Fine-tunes the NASA-IBM HLS geospatial foundation model to map paddy rice at 30 m across monsoon Asia over six growing seasons.
   - Why it matters: Direct evidence that Landsat/Sentinel-2 foundation models generalize to operational crop mapping at continental scale.

2. ⭐ Spectral and spatial harmonization of Landsat ETM+, OLI, and Sentinel-2 MSI using time-series harmonic analysis and blur kernel estimation
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115384
   - Proposes a harmonic-analysis and blur-kernel approach to align spectral and spatial properties across three sensors into one consistent surface reflectance record.
   - Why it matters: Better cross-sensor harmonization directly improves the training data quality for Landsat-based foundation models.

   - Journal: ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2026.04.056
   - Introduces a flexible, prompt-adaptable foundation model architecture for LULC segmentation across heterogeneous sensor inputs.
   - Why it matters: Shows the field moving toward general-purpose, task-flexible LULC foundation models rather than single-sensor networks.

4. FlexiMo: A Flexible Remote Sensing Foundation Model
   - Journal: IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing (Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1109/tgrs.2026.3656362
   - Proposes a foundation model that adapts to varying spatial resolutions and spectral bands without retraining, evaluated across multiple RS benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: Cross-resolution flexibility is a key gap for deploying pretrained RS foundation models on heterogeneous satellite archives.

5. Large vision-language model knowledge guided multi-source urban land-use mapping: A case study of representative cities across six continents
   - Journal: ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2026.04.004
   - Uses VLM-derived semantic priors to guide multi-source urban LULC mapping validated across six continents.

6. Increasing respiration weakens the carbon sink over two decades in a temperate deciduous forest
   - Journal: Global Change Biology Communications (Early Access, posted 2026-08-04)
   - DOI: 10.1002/gcb4.70041
   - A 20-year eddy covariance record shows rising ecosystem respiration is eroding net carbon uptake in a US temperate forest.
   - Why it matters: Long-term flux evidence that a warming-driven respiration increase can outpace photosynthetic gains, weakening forest carbon sinks.

7. Modeling carbon budgets in conventional and diverse crop rotations with cover crops using eddy covariance data
   - Journal: Agricultural and Forest Meteorology (Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.agrformet.2026.111255

   - Why it matters: Field-measured evidence for how cover-cropping and rotation diversity shift cropland carbon balance.
