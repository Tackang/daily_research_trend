📚 [2026-08-17] Weekly Journal Digest (past 7 days Early Access)

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ Generating an annual 30 m rice cover product for monsoon Asia (2018–2023) using harmonized Landsat and Sentinel-2 data and the NASA-IBM geospatial foundation model
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115256
   - Fine-tunes the NASA-IBM Prithvi geospatial foundation model on auto-generated rice labels from harmonized Landsat/Sentinel-2 data to map rice across Monsoon Asia.
   - Why it matters: direct evidence that Landsat-based foundation models scale to operational crop-type mapping at continental scale.

2. AgriFM: A multi-source temporal remote sensing foundation model for agriculture mapping
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115234
   - Pretrains a Video Swin Transformer on MODIS/Landsat-8-9/Sentinel-2 time series (25M+ samples) for crop and land cover mapping.
   - Why it matters: shows multi-sensor temporal fusion outperforming single-sensor foundation models for agriculture.

3. Modeling carbon budgets in conventional and diverse crop rotations with cover crops using eddy covariance data
   - Journal: Agricultural and Forest Meteorology (Early Access, published 2026-08-15)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.agrformet.2026.111255
   - Six-year eddy-covariance dataset compares net ecosystem carbon budgets of a conventional corn-soybean rotation vs. a diversified rotation with cover crops in Ontario.
   - Why it matters: field-validated carbon accounting baseline useful for calibrating satellite-derived cropland carbon flux estimates.

4. Taller trees experienced less crown damage during a severe hurricane in a tropical forest
   - Journal: Global Change Biology (Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1111/gcb.70709
   - Uses airborne LiDAR-derived canopy structure to link tree height to hurricane-induced crown damage and biomass loss in a tropical forest.
   - Why it matters: refines disturbance-driven biomass loss models needed for satellite-based carbon stock monitoring in storm-exposed forests.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
5. Accelerating scientific discovery with Co-Scientist
   - Journal: Nature
   - DOI: 10.1038/s41586-026-10644-y
   - A Gemini-based multi-agent AI system for structured scientific reasoning that proposes and refines novel hypotheses across biomedical domains.
   - Why it matters: benchmark case for agentic AI systems doing autonomous hypothesis generation, a pattern transferable to Earth science discovery workflows.

6. A multi-agent system for automating scientific discovery
   - Journal: Nature
   - DOI: 10.1038/s41586-026-10652-y
   - "Robin" couples literature-search and data-analysis agents to generate hypotheses, propose experiments, and interpret results, applied here to dry AMD drug discovery.
   - Why it matters: end-to-end automated hypothesis-experiment-interpretation loop, a template for automating remote sensing model iteration and validation.
