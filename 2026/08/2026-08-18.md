🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks for Spatially Independent Multispectral Land Classification `arXiv:2608.13769`
   - Evaluates KAN architectures on Landsat 8 multispectral imagery for 5-class land classification, training on Edmonton and testing on Calgary for spatial generalization.
   - Why it matters: matches random-forest accuracy with far fewer parameters and better interpretability, directly relevant to lightweight Landsat classification backbones.

2. From Crown Candidates to Neighborhood Screening: Optical GeoAI for Urban-Canopy Assessment `arXiv:2608.13856`
   - Combines DeepForest crown detection, NDVI thresholding, and Segment Anything on 0.6m NAIP imagery to map urban tree canopy in Davis, CA, validated against LiDAR.
   - Why it matters: shows optical-only GeoAI pipelines can approximate LiDAR-grade canopy structure products at much lower cost.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
3. RubricForge: Reward-Free Rubric Induction for Judging LLM Agents `arXiv:2608.13564`
   - Evolves judging rubrics via reflective evolution against labeled trajectories to align rubric-based scoring with true environment reward.
   - Why it matters: better proxy judges reduce reliance on costly ground-truth reward signals when evaluating agent pipelines.

4. Second Thought: Training-Free Parallel Reasoning While Acting `arXiv:2608.13667`
   - Lets agents run auxiliary reasoning branches concurrently with the main action loop, cutting sequential decoding latency up to 43%.
   - Why it matters: inference-time speedups like this make long-horizon agentic workflows (e.g., automated literature review) more practical.

5. SocialRL: Small Models for Strategic Multi-Domain Negotiation `arXiv:2608.13787`
   - Trains a unified 4B-parameter model via RL across six negotiation domains, matching GPT-4.1-level average utility.
   - Why it matters: shows small specialized RL agents can rival frontier LLMs on structured multi-agent interaction tasks.

6. HELIX: Model-Harness Co-Evolution for Recursive Self-Improvement `arXiv:2608.13951`
   - Proposes an auditable framework where an agent's model and its execution harness co-evolve together, with explicit trajectory logging for interventions.
   - Why it matters: auditability is the missing piece for trusting self-improving research/agent systems in production.

7. Rethinking AI Evaluation: From Superhuman Autonomy to Human-AI Teams `arXiv:2608.13577`
   - Position paper arguing evaluation should measure human-AI team outcomes rather than isolated autonomous agent performance.
   - Why it matters: reframes how "AI for science" progress should be benchmarked—collaboration quality over solo capability.
