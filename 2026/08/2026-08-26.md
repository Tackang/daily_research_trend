🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ Interpretable Landsat-to-Hyperspectral Dual Super-Resolution Without Large Matrix Inversion `arXiv:2608.22790`
   - Converts global Landsat-8/9 multispectral imagery into AVIRIS-level hyperspectral data via a customized interpretable ADMM-Net, jointly handling spatial (30m→15m) and spectral (7→172 band) super-resolution.
   - Why it matters: Directly extends Landsat's usable spectral resolution without dense-matrix inversion — relevant to Landsat foundation-model input pipelines.

2. The Spatial Anatomy of Urban Wildfire Vulnerability: A Spatially Validated GeoAI Framework `arXiv:2608.22293`
   - Links 12,081 CAL FIRE damage inspections from the 2025 Palisades Fire to Sentinel-2 vegetation moisture, Landsat surface temperature, LANDFIRE fuels, and building/terrain data via spatially cross-validated ML.
   - Why it matters: Demonstrates multi-sensor (Landsat + Sentinel-2) fusion for structure-loss prediction with rigorous spatial block validation, not just random splits.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
3. EarthVerse: Benchmarking Scientific Agents Across Dynamic Earth Systems and Natural Hazards `arXiv:2608.23525`
   - 405 reproducible tasks over 199 documented hazard events testing whether agents can select compatible evidence, execute transparent calculations, and preserve provenance across heterogeneous Earth-system data.
   - Why it matters: Best system hits 84.65% answer-unit accuracy but only 34.81% strict end-to-end reliability — exposes how far Earth-science agents are from trustworthy autonomous analysis.

4. Toward Effective and Reliable LLM Agents via Dynamic Ontology `arXiv:2608.22974`
   - Proposes a dynamic ontology mechanism that lets LLM agents adapt their conceptual structure of tools/tasks during execution instead of relying on a fixed schema.
   - Why it matters: Addresses a core reliability gap (rigid tool/task representations) shared by scientific and geospatial agent pipelines alike.

5. SkillAlchemy: Open-World Agent Skill Creation `arXiv:2608.23417`
   - Framework for agents to autonomously synthesize new reusable skills in open-world settings rather than relying on a fixed predefined skill library.
   - Why it matters: A building block for self-extending research agents that need new capabilities on the fly during scientific discovery workflows.

6. From Inertia to Objectivity: Improving Deep Research Agents with Noise Isolation `arXiv:2608.23045`
   - Identifies "inertia" bias in deep research agents (over-anchoring on early evidence) and introduces noise isolation to improve objectivity of multi-step research reports.
   - Why it matters: Directly targets a failure mode relevant to any agent (including this briefing pipeline) that synthesizes literature into summaries.
