🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Multi-Year Geospatial Reasoning using Interannually-Consistent Historical Predictions as a Free Input Modality `arXiv:2608.05979`
   - Feeds prior-year crop-type predictions as time-ordered tokens into a classifier trained on ~5.4M Copernicus Land Monitoring Service pixels across Europe.
   - Why it matters: +1.6pp F1 overall (olives +4.6, fruits +3.7) shows temporal context is a cheap, reusable input for LULC-style classifiers.

2. Radio-FM: A Foundation Model for Radio Signal Representation Learning and Its Applications `arXiv:2608.05793`
   - Dual-channel (I/Q) foundation model pretrained on 15 radio-signal datasets spanning modulation, radar, and comms tasks.
   - Why it matters: SOTA on 13/15 downstream benchmarks with strong few-shot transfer — a template for signal-domain FMs relevant to SAR/RF sensing pipelines.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
3. OPERA: Operator-residual feedback for reliable autonomous optical experiments with language-model agents `arXiv:2608.05990`
   - LLM-agent framework for autonomous physics experiments that uses physically-interpretable residuals instead of raw scores to judge outcomes.
   - Why it matters: cut false "improvement" decisions from ~30% to ~1%, validated on 3 real optical instruments — a rare hardware-grounded agent result.

4. Project2Task: Graph-Guided Project-Level Planning for Autonomous Research `arXiv:2608.05225`
   - Converts research project briefs into dependency-graph "innovation atoms" with detailed task contracts for agent execution.
   - Why it matters: raised downstream research-task accuracy from 0.536 to 0.759 — directly targets automated scientific discovery.

5. OrchestraBench: Evaluating Multi-Agent Orchestration Failure Modes, Recovery, and Decomposition Quality `arXiv:2608.05263`
   - Benchmark with reproducible failure injection across enterprise multi-agent workflows.
   - Why it matters: shows cascade failures worsen with pipeline depth, and trusted-state signals beat self-detection — useful for anyone building agent pipelines.

6. SkillHEX: Improving Agent Skills via Hypothesis-Driven Autonomous Exploration and Exploitation `arXiv:2608.05628`
   - Combines hypothesis-driven self-verification with evidence-guided tree search for LLM-agent skill improvement under budget constraints.
   - Why it matters: 55.9%/57.9% pass rates on SkillsBench, a step toward self-improving research agents.

7. SkillTrace: Multi-Trace Provenance Auditing for LLM-Agent Skill Reuse `arXiv:2608.05204`
   - Detects provenance of reused skills across LLM-agent ecosystems via expression/implementation/operational traces.
   - Why it matters: 0.938 AUROC on 36,446 skills — a governance layer as agent skill-sharing scales.
