🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ HeatCast: A Benchmark for Neighborhood-Scale LST Forecasting across 124 U.S. Cities `arXiv:2608.07640`
   - Landsat-based benchmark (30m, 2013–2025) for monthly land surface temperature forecasting across 124 U.S. cities.
   - Earthformer beats CNN+LSTM; non-temperature channels alone nearly match full history — useful signal for Landsat FM pretraining choices.
   - Why it matters: Directly relevant to Landsat foundation model benchmarking and downstream urban heat applications.

2. Deep Learning based Detection of Fishing Vessels and Fishing Monitoring using Nightlight Images `arXiv:2608.09360`
   - Dual-branch YOLO11 on SDGSAT-1 nightlight imagery detects >31,000 fishing vessels along India's west coast (2022-23).
   - ~77% of detected vessels operated without AIS, revealing "dark fleet" activity via nighttime EO.
   - Why it matters: Shows nightlight satellite data extending maritime monitoring beyond typical optical/SAR pipelines.

3. Integrating spectral and morphological plant features with decision-tree models for early-season cotton biomass and nitrogen status estimation from multi-year UAV data `arXiv:2608.07801`
   - Multi-year UAV multispectral data + random forest/XGBoost estimates cotton biomass and nitrogen status (R²≈0.84-0.88).
   - Nitrogen nutrition index reliably flags deficient plots across stress levels.
   - Why it matters: Practical biomass estimation approach transferable to satellite-scale carbon/biomass monitoring workflows.

4. Real-time physics inversion for retrieval of sub-pixel wildfire temperatures from VSWIR imaging spectroscopy `arXiv:2608.07580`
   - Full-physics forward model retrieves sub-pixel fire temperature from AVIRIS-3/EMIT-class VSWIR spectra in near real time (RMSE 41.8K).
   - Validated over 168 overflights in the 2025 FireSense campaign.
   - Why it matters: Real-time hyperspectral inversion pushes wildfire EO toward operational, flight-cadence deployment.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
5. Adaptive Symmetry Discovery for Dynamical System Identification `arXiv:2608.08091`
   - Automatically discovers unknown symmetry groups from trajectory data, then exploits them to cut data needs for system ID.
   - Matches efficiency of methods that assume symmetries are known in advance, grounded in group representation theory.
   - Why it matters: A concrete step toward automated scientific discovery of physical structure from raw observations.

6. DoGMA: A Central-Dogma-Guided Foundation Model for Multi-Omics Alignment and Multi-Task Learning in Oncology `arXiv:2608.08148`
   - Transformer-MoE foundation model bakes central-dogma information flow into directed attention for multi-omics data.
   - Strong results on representation learning, survival prediction, and metastasis forecasting in cancer.
   - Why it matters: Domain-specific inductive biases in foundation model design — a pattern transferable to Earth-system FMs.

7. Towards Researcher Agents for Knowledge-Graph Question Answering `arXiv:2608.07700`
   - Self-improving "researcher agent" edits its own prompts/code to convert NL questions into SPARQL over DBpedia.
   - Finds predicate selection, not syntax, is the main bottleneck; flags benchmark scoring flaws from property ambiguity.
   - Why it matters: Self-improving agent loops are a building block for autonomous research pipelines.

8. Agent-MD: Selective LLM Intervention with Event-Driven Escalation for Stateful GCMC–MD Campaigns `arXiv:2608.07637`
   - LLM reasoning invoked only at setup and on anomalies in long molecular-dynamics campaigns; rule-based agent runs routine cycles.
   - Ran 120 simulation cycles autonomously, correctly flagging clay-water desorption anomalies when triggered.
   - Why it matters: Cost-efficient "selective LLM" pattern for AI agents overseeing long scientific simulation runs.

💡 One-line comment: Today's trend is agents doing less — selective, event-driven LLM intervention (Agent-MD, researcher agents) paired with physics/symmetry-aware inductive biases (DoGMA, symmetry discovery) is replacing brute-force always-on reasoning.
