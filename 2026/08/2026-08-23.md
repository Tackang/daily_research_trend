2. To Remove or Not to Remove Clouds: Comparative Analysis and Fusion of Raw SAR and Synthetic NDWI for Overcast Water Segmentation `arXiv:2608.17398`
   - Compares cloud-removal preprocessing vs. direct SAR/synthetic-NDWI fusion for water-body segmentation under persistent cloud cover.
   - Why it matters: practical guidance for optical-SAR fusion in cloud-prone regions.

   - Why it matters: pushes RS segmentation toward instruction-following, multimodal reasoning.
