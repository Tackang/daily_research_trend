📡 [2026-08-04] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Locally Consistent Transductive Information Maximization for Few-Shot RS Scene Classification `arXiv:2607.29192`
   - Introduces LC-TIM, extending transductive few-shot inference with local consistency regularization on RS foundation model embeddings; releases the first open benchmark across 10 datasets and 2 vision-language RS models.
   - Why it matters: Shows transductive methods beat zero-shot RS-VLM baselines, especially in low-shot regimes.

2. Think with Extra-Image: A Farmland Segmentation Agent `arXiv:2607.28186`
   - FarmSeeker is an LLM agent that detects ambiguous farm boundaries and dynamically queries extra spatio-temporal imagery; ships GSFS-Bench, a global-scale farmland segmentation benchmark.
   - Why it matters: Agentic loop for actively fetching auxiliary satellite data is a template for other LULC tasks.

3. Forecasting Land Art Under Climate Scenarios `arXiv:2607.28489`
   - Uses 1,744 Landsat and Sentinel-2 images (1984-2025) of Spiral Jetty plus IPCC scenarios and diffusion synthesis to forecast landscape appearance under climate change.
   - Why it matters: Novel long-horizon Landsat+Sentinel-2 time series application beyond typical classification/detection.

4. Poverty Mapping: Data, Models and Applications `arXiv:2607.29457`
   - Review of satellite imagery, mobile-phone, and social-media data fusion methods for fine-grained poverty estimation.
   - Why it matters: Good reference for socio-economic downstream tasks built on EO foundation model embeddings.

5. SatEdit: Mask-Conditioned Image Editing via VLM-Guided Segment Annotation `arXiv:2607.29367`
   - Uses VLMs to auto-annotate segments for controllable satellite image editing, reaching CLIP score 0.6322 on object-level edits.
   - Why it matters: Editable satellite imagery is useful for synthetic data augmentation and change-scenario simulation.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
6. Scaling Scientific Discovery Environments for Turn-Level Agentic RL `arXiv:2607.28990`
   - SciDisco trains discovery agents via process-verifiable, turn-level RL environments where LLMs interact with execution backends on real scientific data.
   - Why it matters: A scalable RL recipe for hypothesis-driven agentic science, generalizable beyond its benchmark domain.

7. SciToolAgent-Evo: Ontology-Aware Self-Evolving Agent for Open-World Scientific Tool Acquisition `arXiv:2607.28692`
   - Agent dynamically discovers and integrates new scientific tools using an evolving ontology rather than a fixed toolset.
   - Why it matters: Addresses the closed-toolset limitation common in current science agents.

8. Can AI Evaluate AI Scientists? `arXiv:2607.28631`
   - Benchmarks autonomous research-generation systems via multi-model review across originality, rigor, clarity, and significance.
   - Why it matters: Establishes a meta-evaluation layer needed as AI-Scientist-style pipelines proliferate.

💡 One-line comment: Today's crop leans toward agents that actively fetch/verify their own data (farmland, science tools) rather than passively consuming fixed datasets — a shift from static benchmarks to closed-loop, self-directed systems.
