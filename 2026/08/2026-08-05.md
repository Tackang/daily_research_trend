📡 [2026-08-05] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Global-Scale Self-Supervised Spatiotemporal Learning for NDVI Time-Series Reconstruction ⭐ `arXiv:2608.02322`
   - GloSSR uses self-supervised learning with artificial cloud-degradation augmentation to reconstruct vegetation index time-series from corrupted satellite data at global scale.
   - Why it matters: directly relevant to Landsat/foundation-model pretraining for continuous vegetation monitoring.

2. ISRS-DETR: Detection-Guided Click Propagation for Remote Sensing Interactive Segmentation `arXiv:2608.02468`
   - Reduces annotation cost by propagating user clicks to same-class objects via object-level evidence in RS imagery.
   - Why it matters: cuts labeling cost for large-scale RS segmentation datasets.

3. From Forest to Future Capital: Tracking Land Cover Change in Ibu Kota Nusantara (IKN) 2021-2026 with PlanetScope Imagery `arXiv:2608.01230`
   - Quantifies vegetation loss and urban expansion during Indonesia's new capital construction using high-res PlanetScope time-series.
   - Why it matters: real-world LULC change case study showing rapid land conversion dynamics.

4. Mapping Melliferous Tree Species in Kenya via One-Class Classification with Hyperspectral Domain Adaptation `arXiv:2608.02045`
   - HyUDA-One detects individual tree species from hyperspectral imagery using unsupervised domain adaptation for beekeeping resource mapping.
   - Why it matters: shows domain-adaptation value for fine-grained species-level mapping with limited labels.

5. USP-Mamba: Unmixing-Derived Spectral and Structural Prompting for Hyperspectral Super-Resolution `arXiv:2608.02401`
   - Combines spectral unmixing priors with Mamba state-space modeling to upsample hyperspectral imagery while preserving spectral fidelity.
   - Why it matters: efficient alternative to transformer-based HSI super-resolution.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
6. Abduction Without a Body? Representational Grounding and the Abduction Loop for Scientific Hypothesis Generation `arXiv:2608.02505`
   - Proposes a mechanistic framework where multimodal models generate scientific hypotheses via representational transformation instead of embodied interaction.
   - Why it matters: reframes automated hypothesis generation without requiring physical/simulated embodiment.

7. KC-Agent: A Dual-Process Cognitive Architecture for Efficient ML Model Improvement `arXiv:2608.02351`
   - Combines fast pattern recognition with deliberate reasoning to handle production data drift and improve deployed ML models.
   - Why it matters: practical agentic loop for continuous model maintenance under distribution shift.

8. A Taxonomy of Cognitive Capability Gaps in Generative and Agentic AI `arXiv:2608.02553`
   - Survey organizing limitations of agentic AI across persistent state, goal-directed autonomy, self-monitoring, and learning, proposing an adaptive architecture.
   - Why it matters: useful reference map for diagnosing where current agent systems fail.

9. Cooperative Coevolution for Resource-Constrained Agentic LLM Post-Training `arXiv:2608.02391`
   - Decomposes parameter space into subspaces for evolution-based post-training, recovering 92% of gradient-based gains at much lower memory cost.
   - Why it matters: cheaper path to fine-tune agentic LLMs without full backprop infrastructure.

💡 One-line comment: Self-supervised time-series reconstruction and domain-adaptation-driven fine-grained mapping are converging on the same goal — squeezing more signal out of imperfect, cloud/label-scarce satellite data.
