🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Mapping Woody Vegetation from Multi-Source Imagery and Prediction Fusion for Enhanced Data Efficiency and Accuracy `arXiv:2608.26471`
   - Fuses multi-source Sentinel-2 imagery via composition normalization, prediction fusion, and cross-source label transfer for woody vegetation mapping in New South Wales.
   - Why it matters: multi-source fusion cuts error 28-76% over single-source imagery, a reusable recipe for boosting data efficiency in any satellite-based CNN pipeline.

2. Learning Woody Clearing With Loss Alignment for Zero-Shot Regrowth and Woody Segmentation `arXiv:2608.26489`
   - Introduces a loss-scaling coefficient to align model objectives with end-user precision/recall needs, plus synthetic before/after augmentation for zero-shot regrowth detection.
   - Why it matters: task-aligned loss tuning + synthetic temporal augmentation is directly transferable to LULC change detection with scarce labeled bitemporal data.

3. DP-JMRNet: A Deep Unfolding Network for Differential Phase Preservation in Sparse Bitemporal SAR Reconstruction `arXiv:2608.26605`
   - Deep-unfolding network jointly reconstructs two SAR epochs from masked observations, preserving differential phase via an exchange-equivariant module and coherence-aware gating; validated on real Sentinel-1 data.
   - Why it matters: improves InSAR-style deformation/subsidence monitoring from sparse SAR acquisitions, relevant to any pipeline pairing optical foundation models with SAR change signals.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
4. Agentic AI for Operating Scientific Instruments for Nanoscale Characterization `arXiv:2608.26198`
   - Three-LLM-agent system (command generation, image-quality evaluation, artifact correction) runs an atomic force microscope end-to-end, matching expert operators in image quality and tuning time.
   - Why it matters: concrete evidence that agentic AI can safely close the loop on real physical scientific instruments, not just simulated workflows.

5. The Artificial Experimentalist: Discovery and Control of Self-Organizing Phenomena with Autotelic Reinforcement Learning `arXiv:2608.26116`
   - Goal-conditioned RL agent (CARL) autonomously discovers and steers stable "soliton" patterns in Lenia cellular automata via minimal local interventions.
   - Why it matters: a template for autotelic agents that both discover and control emergent structure in complex systems — transferable to automated discovery in physical/biological simulators.

6. LLM Agents for Time-Series: A Survey `arXiv:2608.26226`
   - Organizes LLM-based time-series agents by task (forecasting/reasoning, augmentation, anomaly detection, decision support) rather than architecture, with datasets and open challenges.
   - Why it matters: useful map for anyone building LLM-agent tooling around temporal Earth-observation or sensor data streams.

7. Don't Overthink, Don't Underthink: Toward Adaptive Reasoning in Agentic AI `arXiv:2608.26442`
   - Identifies over-reasoning (wasted compute) and under-reasoning (incomplete solutions) as failure modes in fixed-budget agentic LLMs, evaluated on MATH-500 and GAIA.
   - Why it matters: adaptive reasoning-budget allocation is a prerequisite for cost-effective multi-agent scientific-discovery pipelines at scale.

💡 One-line comment: remote sensing today is all about squeezing more signal from imperfect data (multi-source fusion, sparse SAR phase reconstruction), while the AI-for-science side shows agents graduating from benchmarks to controlling real instruments and physical simulators.
