2. SAR2Agri: Learning SAR Intensity Representations for Agricultural Monitoring `arXiv:2608.11142`
   - First self-supervised pretraining pipeline using only SAR intensity (no optical grounding), with masking + curriculum learning to capture phenology; hits 84.9% IoU on SICKLE crop mapping, beating optical baselines by 15.3pt and SAR baselines by 2.2pt.
   - Why it matters: shows SAR-only foundation pretraining can beat optical-grounded approaches for agricultural monitoring.

   - Why it matters: +2.5-2.7 mIoU over SOTA on a 7-dataset/6-continent benchmark, with strong zero-shot cross-region generalization.

4. Warping Earth Observations for Better Ice Labeling in the Marginal Ice Zone `arXiv:2608.11883`
   - Aligns Sentinel-1 radar and MODIS thermal/visible scenes via mutual-information warping to fix pixel misregistration from fast-drifting sea ice, using a new 2,088-point expert-labeled dataset.
   - Why it matters: solves a core multimodal EO alignment problem that limits dense supervision in any fast-changing environment, not just sea ice.
