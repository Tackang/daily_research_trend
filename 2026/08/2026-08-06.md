🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Standalone DINOv3 for Training-Free Open-Vocabulary Semantic Segmentation in Remote Sensing (DinoSplat-OV) `arXiv:2608.03023`
   - Adapts DINOv3 for training-free open-vocab segmentation of RS imagery, using Text-aware Laplacian Propagation and Gaussian Splatting Upsampling to handle dense, multi-scale scenes.
   - Validated on UDD5, DOTA, LoveDA with competitive results vs. trained methods.
   - Why it matters: Zero-training adaptation of general vision foundation models to RS is a cheap path to scalable segmentation without labeled data.

2. Adaptive Sampling for Automated Post-Disaster Rapid Damage Assessment via Level-Set Cost-Aware Bayesian Optimization `arXiv:2608.02868`
   - Uses cost-aware Bayesian optimization with level-set estimation to guide UAV/data collectors toward the most informative regions after disasters.
   - Cuts uncertainty while minimizing operational survey cost across geographic zones.
   - Why it matters: Smarter sampling strategy could plug into satellite/UAV damage pipelines for faster, cheaper post-disaster mapping.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
3. Verifier-Guided Model Discovery for Physical Dynamical Systems with Pretrained Symbolic Transformers `arXiv:2608.02662`
   - Builds a verifier-guided workflow around ODEFormer, using dynamical/physical-admissibility criteria to select equations from a multi-trajectory candidate pool.
   - Enables transfer to new physical systems without system-specific equation knowledge.
   - Why it matters: Interpretable symbolic surrogates guided by verifiers is a template for trustworthy scientific discovery automation.

4. Deep Divide-and-Reduce in Symbolic Regression `arXiv:2608.02628`
   - Generalizes expression decomposition/reduction for symbolic regression, avoiding brute-force sub-structure search used in prior methods like AI Feynman.
   - Broadens applicability to more complex equations with theoretical guarantees.
   - Why it matters: More robust automated equation discovery underpins AI-for-science pipelines beyond narrow physics benchmarks.

5. Scaling an Autoregressive Transformer for Single-Cell Generation `arXiv:2608.02961`
   - Causal transformer + quantized VAE tokenizer trained to generate single-cell gene expression vectors; derives a two-exponent scaling law and compute-optimal frontier.
   - First joint scaling-law study for a single-cell foundation model.
   - Why it matters: Establishes scaling recipes for biological foundation models, mirroring the LLM scaling playbook for science domains.

6. AgentPanel: Toward a New Paradigm for Human-AI Collaboration in Exploring Scientific Questions `arXiv:2608.03283`
   - Multi-agent forum framework where AI agents and humans jointly generate, critique, and refine scientific research ideas.
   - Aims to structure collaborative ideation rather than single-agent automation.
   - Why it matters: Signals a shift from "AI does science alone" toward structured human-AI co-discovery workflows.

💡 One-line comment: Today's trend is AI-for-science tooling (symbolic discovery, scaling laws, human-AI collaboration) outweighing pure RS work, with foundation-model adaptation (not retraining) as the common thread in the one strong RS paper.
