🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. LoDA: A Level of Detection Aware Method and Multimodal Sensing Benchmark for Object Level Change Detection `arXiv:2608.05356`
   - Introduces an object-level LiDAR/GNSS/IMU change-detection benchmark (Subiaco, 2023 vs. 2025) with fused bi-temporal city maps and five-class change labels.
   - Proposes detection-limit-aware registration plus geometry-driven object proxies to assign change labels with confidence.
   - Why it matters: pushes change detection from pixel/scene level to object-level with uncertainty-aware labels, a gap in most satellite-based LULC pipelines.

2. Iterative Hybrid Discrete-Continuous Viewpoint Planning for UAV Photogrammetry `arXiv:2608.05718`
   - Plans UAV camera viewpoints using photogrammetric heuristics (frontality, distance, parallax, multi-view coverage) with iterative discrete-continuous refinement.
   - Improves 3D reconstruction accuracy/completeness over existing UAV path-planning baselines on synthetic scenes.
   - Why it matters: better UAV acquisition planning directly improves the quality of training data feeding into RS foundation models.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
3. CASCADE: Agentic Prediction of Transcriptional Effects from Regulatory Networks `arXiv:2608.05359`
   - Agentic framework (exposed via MCP) predicts downstream transcriptional effects of gene perturbation from precomputed ARACNe regulatory networks.
   - Validated against real TCGA tumor data using copy-number amplification as a dosage proxy, showing 72-90% directional concordance across three cancer types.
   - Why it matters: a concrete example of agentic tool-use validated against real-world biological data rather than synthetic benchmarks.

4. Small Foundation Models of Human Cognition and Behaviour `arXiv:2608.05224`
   - Trains 14 models (135M-14B params, 4 architectures) on Psych-101 (10.7M trial-level choices, 160 experiments) as cognitive proxies.
   - In-distribution, 0.6-1B params match a 70B baseline; out-of-distribution, larger models scale much better on novel task structure.
   - Why it matters: shows scale mostly buys generalization, not in-distribution fit — a pattern likely relevant to RS foundation model scaling too.

5. DoctorAgents: Agentic AutoML Pipeline Refinement for Small Clinical Temporal Data `arXiv:2608.05375`
   - Agentic framework that iteratively refines AutoML pipelines for scarce, heterogeneous clinical temporal data, adding explicit reasoning/memory over brute-force search.
   - Targets a gap in existing AutoML systems that lack reasoning for small, temporally complex datasets.
   - Why it matters: reasoning-augmented AutoML agents are a template for automating pipeline design in other data-scarce scientific domains.

6. Innovation-Residual Auditing of Autonomous Analysis Agents `arXiv:2608.05490`
   - Audits fully autonomous data-analysis agents (cohort selection, joins, model fitting) without labeled mistakes, flagging operations that deviate from sound-analysis predictions.
   - Shows scoring against immediate-predecessor steps vs. full reconstructed analysis changes error localization and how a single mistake propagates into flags.
   - Why it matters: as agents take over more of the scientific pipeline, this kind of unsupervised error-auditing becomes essential for trust.
