🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Prior-matched evaluation of operational Earth-observation classifiers `arXiv:2607.07146`
   - Proposes a "three-number reporting" method (balanced-test, operational-prior, post-deployment) to expose the true precision gap of rare-event EO classifiers, demonstrated on Sentinel-1 internal-wave detection.
   - Tuned classifier reaches 0.927 precision at real-world event rates (~1/20 scenes) vs. just 0.192 for a naively balanced-data model.
   - Why it matters: rare-event EO systems (fires, spills, waves) routinely overstate real deployment performance — this gives an honest reporting standard.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
2. NEXUS: A Lightweight Foundation Model for Collider Physics with Multi-Domain Adaptation `arXiv:2607.27501`
   - A ~3M-parameter self-supervised autoencoder pretrained on LHC particle-track data, transferred to gravitational-wave, flood-forecasting, and neural-activity tasks.
   - Beats similarly-sized transformers on small labeled datasets while staying cheap enough for real-time/edge use.
   - Why it matters: small efficient foundation models can generalize across wildly different scientific domains, geoscience included.

3. Albilich: Steerable Proof-State Orchestration for LLM-Based Mathematical Research with CAS Integration `arXiv:2607.27705`
   - Open-source system combining long-horizon LLM reasoning, computer algebra systems, literature retrieval, and persistent context management for math research.
   - Solved all 10 RealMath benchmark problems and found a group-theory counterexample; CAS integration cut token use by 32%.
   - Why it matters: a template for tool-augmented "AI scientist" agents beyond pure LLM reasoning.

4. SkillMentor: LLM Agent Self-Evolution via Learning Blind-Spot Diagnosis `arXiv:2607.27360`
   - Trains a reinforcement-learned "Mentor" policy that diagnoses an agent's own recurring failure patterns and converts them into reusable corrective skills.
   - Achieves ~44.2% average gains on AppWorld/BFCLv3 without weight updates or human-labeled examples.
   - Why it matters: self-improving agents that don't need retraining or human supervision are key for scalable AI-for-science pipelines.

5. World Action Planner: Generalizable Decision-Making with Action-Conditioned World Models `arXiv:2607.27599`
   - Combines a VLM planner with an action-conditioned world model, iteratively refining plans via search over imagined rollouts instead of end-to-end imitation.
   - Outperforms state-of-the-art VLA/world-action-model policies on compositional and zero-shot manipulation tasks.
   - Why it matters: model-based planning + VLM reasoning could generalize to scientific instrument/robotic-lab automation.

6. RadHarmony: Radiological Data Handling in the Era of Agentic AI `arXiv:2607.27235`
   - Open-source library unifying 24 public radiology datasets into one schema, with an AI-agent skill that automates data integration end to end.
   - Demonstrated by pretraining RadHarmony-ViT across heterogeneous chest X-ray datasets with zero dataset-specific code.
   - Why it matters: agentic data-harmonization pipelines are exactly what's missing for scaling foundation models across messy scientific archives (relevant beyond radiology, e.g. EO data lakes).
