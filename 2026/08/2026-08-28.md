🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Synergising Local Geo-Environmental Characteristics with Spatial Context for Enhancing Landslide Susceptibility Mapping `arXiv:2608.24956`
   - Proposes a Local-Geo and Spatial Context Fusion (LGSCF) strategy that fuses per-pixel geo-environmental features with spatial context via feature-wise modulation, plugged into CNN backbones and tested across 2,644 km² in Taiwan.
   - Why it matters: addresses the pixel-vs-patch tradeoff in hazard mapping, relevant to any Landsat/Sentinel-based CNN pipeline needing better local-context fusion.

2. Semi-Supervised Adaptation of Vision-Language Models for Image Classification `arXiv:2608.25485`
   - Introduces Self-Evolutionary CLIP (SE-CLIP): warm-up on a few annotated seeds, then recursive class-balanced mining of high-confidence unlabeled satellite scenes; beats prior semi-supervised methods on UCM/NWPU.
   - Why it matters: label-efficient CLIP adaptation recipe directly transferable to scaling up satellite scene classification with minimal annotation.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
3. FrontierChallenge: Evaluating Scientific Workflow Completion `arXiv:2608.24979`
   - Cross-domain benchmark of 300 end-to-end scientific workflows (97 released) spanning chemistry, materials, life science, and environment; best agent+model configs complete only 20.6% of tasks.
   - Why it matters: sobering baseline showing current frontier agents are far from reliably automating full scientific workflows, not just isolated subtasks.

4. BixBench3: Benchmarking AI Agents on Research-Study-Scale Computational Biology Tasks `arXiv:2608.25286`
   - 20 real studies, 17 assay types, 138 graded artifacts; agents go from raw data to results, with top model (GPT 5.6 Sol) scoring only 0.48 average.
   - Why it matters: a rare "study-scale" agent benchmark with real cost/time accounting (avg. 6.8h, $43/run) — useful yardstick for AI-for-science agent maturity.

5. PhysElite: How Far Are LLMs from Solving Olympiad-Level Physics Problems? `arXiv:2608.25097`
   - Bilingual multimodal benchmark of 11,586 Olympiad-tier physics problems with diagrams and full step-by-step solutions.
   - Why it matters: fills the high-difficulty, vision-grounded gap in physics reasoning evals, useful signal for multimodal scientific-reasoning foundation models.

6. Federation Is Nearly Free, Reasoning Is Not: Tradeoffs for AI Co-Scientists in Protein Characterization Workflows `arXiv:2608.25215`
   - Compares federation topologies, RL vs. LLM-based policies, and models on protein function characterization; model choice dominates (Claude Opus 92-94% accuracy), federation overhead is negligible.
   - Why it matters: practical guidance for building multi-agent AI co-scientist pipelines — spend budget on the base model, not on coordination architecture.

7. Retrieve, Match, Escalate: Accurate and Scalable Product Linking with VLM-Distilled Cross-Encoders and Agentic VLMs `arXiv:2608.25037`
   - Three-stage cascade (retrieval → cross-encoder → agentic VLM escalation) for entity resolution, trained via VLM-consensus labels with no human annotation; raises coverage from 68% to 77% at 98% precision.
   - Why it matters: a general difficulty-aware cascade pattern applicable beyond retail to large-scale multimodal matching tasks, including RS scene/change matching.
