📡 [2026-04-16] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. A Multi-Agent Feedback System for Detecting and Describing News Events in Satellite Imagery `arXiv:2604.12772`
   - SkyScraper combines news article geocoding with multi-agent AI to locate and caption satellite image sequences, finding 5× more events than traditional geocoding. Releases a 5,000-sequence multi-temporal dataset.
   - Why it matters: LLM-driven agents can automate labor-intensive satellite image search and labeling at scale.

2. HuiYanEarth-SAR: A Foundation Model for High-Fidelity and Low-Cost Global Remote Sensing Imagery Generation `arXiv:2604.11444`
   - A SAR foundation model that integrates geographic coordinates with electromagnetic scattering physics to synthesize realistic SAR imagery at any global location.
   - Why it matters: Expands SAR research from analysis to generation, enabling scalable synthetic data pipelines for downstream tasks.

3. GTPBD-MM: A Global Terraced Parcel and Boundary Dataset with Multi-Modality `arXiv:2604.12315`
   - Combines high-res optical imagery, text descriptions, and DEM data for terraced agricultural parcel extraction; proposes ETTerra baseline leveraging all three modalities.
   - Why it matters: Multimodal fusion (vision + text + terrain) meaningfully outperforms vision-only approaches for challenging LULC mapping.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
4. The Long-Horizon Task Mirage? Diagnosing Where and Why Agentic Systems Break `arXiv:2604.11969`
   - Introduces HORIZON benchmark with 3,100+ trajectories across four domains, using trajectory-grounded LLM-as-a-Judge to systematically attribute failure modes in LLM agent execution.
   - Why it matters: Rigorous failure diagnosis is essential for building reliable agents in scientific workflows.

5. GAM: Hierarchical Graph-based Agentic Memory for LLM Agents `arXiv:2604.12285`
   - Decouples memory encoding from consolidation using event graphs and topic networks, allowing consistent long-term knowledge retention in LLM agents.
   - Why it matters: Structured hierarchical memory is a prerequisite for multi-step autonomous scientific discovery agents.

6. GCA Framework: Gulf-Grounded Dataset and Agentic Pipeline for Climate Decision Support `arXiv:2604.12306`
   - Pairs ~200K climate Q&A entries with remote-sensing inputs and tool-augmented LLMs for region-specific climate advisory; domain fine-tuning substantially outperforms general-purpose baselines.
   - Why it matters: Shows how domain-grounded agentic pipelines integrating RS data can deliver actionable climate science.

7. Long-Horizon Plan Execution in Large Tool Spaces `arXiv:2604.12126`
   - Entropy-Guided Branching algorithm optimizes exploration-exploitation trade-off for tool-augmented agents navigating massive API libraries.
   - Why it matters: A key step toward agents autonomously executing multi-step experiments across diverse scientific tool ecosystems.

💡 One-line comment: Multi-agent architectures are tightening their grip on both ends — from automated satellite event discovery to principled long-horizon planning in large tool spaces.
