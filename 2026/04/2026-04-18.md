📡 [2026-04-18] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ MapSR: Prompt-Driven Land Cover Map Super-Resolution via Vision Foundation Models `arXiv:2604.14582`
   - Frozen vision foundation models with prompt-driven metric inference and graph-based refinement upsample coarse land-cover labels to HR maps — no HR annotations required.
   - Achieves 59.64% mIoU on Chesapeake Bay dataset; cuts trainable parameters by 4 orders of magnitude and training time from hours to minutes.
   - Why it matters: Near-zero-label land cover mapping with foundation models could fundamentally transform global LULC monitoring pipelines.

2. WILD-SAM: Phase-Aware Expert Adaptation of SAM for Landslide Detection in Wrapped InSAR Interferograms `arXiv:2604.14540`
   - Phase-Aware Mixture-of-Experts adapter and Wavelet-Guided Subband Enhancement adapt SAM to handle wrapped phase ambiguity in InSAR data for slow-moving landslide detection.
   - Achieves SOTA on ISSLIDE and ISSLIDE+ benchmarks in both target completeness and contour fidelity.
   - Why it matters: SAR-based geohazard monitoring gains a scalable, foundation-model-powered solution.

3. SatBLIP: Context Understanding and Feature Identification from Satellite Imagery with Vision-Language Learning `arXiv:2604.14373`
   - Fine-tunes BLIP on GPT-4o-generated structured captions of satellite tiles (roof, road, greenery) to predict county-level Social Vulnerability Index.
   - SHAP analysis identifies which visual features are most predictive of rural vulnerability.
   - Why it matters: VLMs can extract meaningful socioeconomic signals from overhead imagery at county scale.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
4. AIBuildAI: An AI Agent for Automatically Building AI Models `arXiv:2604.14455`
   - Hierarchical multi-agent system (manager + designer + coder + tuner sub-agents) automates the full ML development lifecycle from modeling strategy to optimization.
   - Achieves 63.1% medal rate on MLE-Bench across visual, text, time-series, and tabular tasks — outperforming existing AutoML methods.
   - Why it matters: End-to-end autonomous AI model development with minimal human effort is becoming practically viable.

5. Mind DeepResearch Technical Report `arXiv:2604.14518`
   - Planning + DeepSearch + Report agents trained via SFT → RL → preference alignment reach 52.5 on DeepResearch Bench with ~30B parameters; deployed commercially at Li Auto.
   - Introduces MindDR Bench with 500 real-world research queries evaluated by multi-dimensional rubric.
   - Why it matters: Open-weight multi-agent research systems are approaching frontier research performance at modest scale.

6. El Agente Forjador: Task-Driven Agent Generation for Quantum Simulation `arXiv:2604.14609`
   - Multi-agent framework autonomously forges, validates, and reuses computational tools across quantum simulation tasks; tools from stronger agents boost weaker-agent accuracy.
   - Cross-domain tool transfer enables hybrid scientific problem-solving and lowers compute costs.
   - Why it matters: Agentic tool creation is emerging as a scalable, cost-efficient strategy for scientific computing.

7. CAMO: Automated Causal Discovery from Micro Behaviors to Macro Emergence in LLM Agent Simulations `arXiv:2604.14691`
   - Converts theoretical assumptions into measurable variables from simulation data and identifies minimal causal structures explaining emergent macro phenomena; counterfactual testing refines hypotheses.
   - Validated across four emergent social phenomenon settings.
   - Why it matters: Automated causal reasoning over agent simulations accelerates social science discovery without manual analysis.

💡 One-line comment: Prompt-driven zero-label workflows for land cover and agentic tool-forging for science both signal the same shift — foundation models are replacing task-specific training everywhere.
