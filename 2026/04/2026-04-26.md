🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ Structure-Semantic Decoupled Modulation of Global Geospatial Embeddings for High-Resolution Remote Sensing Mapping `arXiv:2604.19591`
   - Decouples geospatial FM embeddings into two pathways: structural (macroscopic spatial constraints) and semantic (context-feature alignment), specifically to bridge the semantic-spatial gap when plugging global FMs into high-res vision pipelines.
   - Achieves SOTA performance over cross-modal fusion baselines on land cover mapping benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: Shows how to extract maximum value from a pretrained geospatial FM (e.g., Landsat FM) for fine-grained downstream mapping without retraining.

2. DiffuSAM: Diffusion Guided Zero-Shot Object Grounding for Remote Sensing Imagery `arXiv:2604.18201`
   - Merges generative diffusion-based bounding box localization with SAM-family segmentation (RemoteSAM, SAM3) into a training-free zero-shot grounding pipeline for satellite imagery.
   - Demonstrates 14%+ improvement in Acc@0.5 over existing SOTA across complex multi-object scenes.
   - Why it matters: Zero-shot, training-free RS object grounding accelerates rapid deployment across heterogeneous sensors and new domains.

3. A Deep U-Net Framework for Flood Hazard Mapping Using Hydraulic Simulations of the Wupper Catchment `arXiv:2604.21028`
   - Replaces computationally expensive 2D hydraulic simulations with a U-Net surrogate model trained to predict maximum water level grids.
   - Validated on the Wupper catchment (Germany); systematic study of patch strategy and data management for spatial prediction.
   - Why it matters: DL surrogates for physics-based models are enabling real-time operational Earth hazard forecasting at scale.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

4. Multi-Agent Empowerment and Emergence of Complex Behavior in Groups `arXiv:2604.21155`
   - Formalizes empowerment — an information-theoretic intrinsic motivation — for multi-agent systems and derives an efficient computation scheme.
   - Shows spontaneous group organization in coupled mechanical agent pairs and a Vicsek flock model without any explicit reward engineering.
   - Why it matters: Intrinsically motivated multi-agent emergence provides a principled path to scalable self-organizing AI systems.

5. BioMiner: A Multi-modal System for Automated Mining of Protein-Ligand Bioactivity Data from Literature `arXiv:2604.21508`
   - Separates bioactivity semantic interpretation (LLM reasoning) from ligand structure construction (chemical-structure-grounded visual reasoning) to extract structured data from papers at scale.
   - Introduces BioVista (16k+ curated entries); delivers 5.59× speedup and 5.75% accuracy gain over manual workflows.
   - Why it matters: Blueprint for automated scientific literature pipelines that could generalize far beyond drug discovery to any data-rich research domain.

6. The Last Harness You'll Ever Build `arXiv:2604.21003`
   - Two-level meta-learning loop: a Harness Evolution Loop adapts AI agents to new task domains, while a Meta-Evolution Loop optimizes the evolution process itself — zero manual harness engineering.
   - Tested across diverse task types, showing rapid domain adaptation with no human-authored scaffolding.
   - Why it matters: Fully automated agent deployment infrastructure removes a key bottleneck in applying AI agents to novel scientific workflows.

💡 One-line comment: Geospatial FMs are learning to decouple what they know from how they apply it — while agent infrastructure quietly automates away the last bits of manual scaffolding.
