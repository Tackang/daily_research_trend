🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ OceanMAE: A Foundation Model for Ocean Remote Sensing `arXiv:2604.08171`
   - MAE pretrained on Sentinel-2 multispectral data augmented with ocean-specific physical descriptors (bathymetry, turbidity) for self-supervised marine representation learning.
   - Outperforms baselines on marine debris detection, pollutant identification, and seafloor depth estimation benchmarks; code and weights publicly released.
   - Why it matters: Domain-augmented MAE pretraining with auxiliary physical descriptors is a directly replicable recipe for Landsat foundation model design.

2. CloudMamba: Uncertainty-Guided Dual-Scale Mamba Network for Cloud Detection `arXiv:2604.06844`
   - Two-stage pipeline: an uncertainty module quantifies thin-cloud ambiguities, then a CNN-Mamba hybrid refines boundaries at dual scale with linear complexity.
   - Achieves state-of-the-art performance on public cloud detection benchmarks with high efficiency.
   - Why it matters: Reliable automated cloud masking is a prerequisite for Landsat time-series analysis; Mamba hybrid architectures show strong accuracy-speed trade-offs.

3. ProtoFlow: Class-Incremental Remote Sensing Segmentation via Low-Curvature Prototype Flow `arXiv:2604.03212`
   - Models class prototypes as smooth, low-curvature trajectories with enforced inter-class separation to mitigate catastrophic forgetting in LULC segmentation.
   - Gains 1.5–2.0 mIoU over baselines on class- and domain-incremental benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: Continual LULC segmentation without full retraining is critical for multi-year land cover monitoring workflows.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

4. SPAMoE: Spectrum-Aware Hybrid Operator Framework for Full-Waveform Inversion `arXiv:2604.07421`
   - Applies a spectral decomposition mixture-of-experts architecture to geophysical seismic inversion, routing frequency bands to specialized expert neural operators.
   - Achieves 54.1% error reduction vs. existing benchmarks on subsurface velocity reconstruction.
   - Why it matters: Spectral MoE routing is a transferable design principle for multi-frequency RS and geophysical signals.

5. Towards Knowledgeable Deep Research: Framework and Benchmark `arXiv:2604.07720`
   - Multi-agent KDR system combines structured databases and unstructured text with a vision-language Structured Knowledge Analyzer for comprehensive scientific report generation.
   - Spans 9 domains with 41 expert questions; outperforms Gemini's deep research system on vision-enriched metrics.
   - Why it matters: Multi-source deep research agents that natively ingest tables and figures are becoming viable tools for scientific literature synthesis.

6. SciDC: Scientific Knowledge-driven Decoding Constraints Improving LLM Reliability `arXiv:2604.06603`
   - Converts domain-specific scientific rules into multi-layered hard constraints applied during LLM decoding to suppress hallucination.
   - ~12% average accuracy improvement across industrial formulation, clinical diagnosis, and retrosynthesis tasks.
   - Why it matters: Hard domain constraints in decoding make LLM-based scientific assistants more trustworthy for hypothesis generation pipelines.

7. The Implicit Curriculum Hypothesis `arXiv:2604.08510`
   - Skill emergence order during LLM pretraining is highly consistent across model families and data mixtures (ρ = .81); composite tasks reliably emerge after component skills.
   - Emergence trajectories are predictable from model internals with R² = .68–.84 across 410M–13B parameter models.
   - Why it matters: Predictable emergent skill ordering informs curriculum design for domain-specific scientific and RS foundation model pretraining.

💡 One-line comment: Domain-specialized MAE pretraining with physical descriptors (OceanMAE) and spectral MoE routing (SPAMoE) signal that physics-aware inductive biases are the next key ingredient for RS and geoscience foundation models.
