🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Spatiotemporal Interpolation of GEDI Biomass with Calibrated Uncertainty `arXiv:2604.03874`

2. RL-Driven Sustainable Land-Use Allocation for the Lake Malawi Basin `arXiv:2604.03768`
   - Uses Sentinel-2 9-class LULC maps with a Proximal Policy Optimization agent to optimize ecosystem services while enforcing spatial coherence constraints.
   - Why it matters: Bridges satellite-derived LULC and RL-based decision-making for actionable land management policy.

3. PollutionNet: Vision Transformer for NO₂/SO₂ Assessment via Satellite-Ground Fusion `arXiv:2604.03311`
   - Fuses Sentinel-5P TROPOMI satellite data with ground-level stations using a Vision Transformer, reducing error by 14% over CNN/RNN baselines.
   - Why it matters: Demonstrates ViT superiority for multi-source satellite data fusion in atmospheric science.

4. Embedding-Only Uplink for Onboard Remote Sensing Retrieval Under Distribution Shift `arXiv:2604.03301`
   - Tests OlmoEarth foundation model embeddings on Sentinel-2 for onboard KNN retrieval under temporal, cloud cover, and geographic distribution shifts.
   - Why it matters: Validates foundation model robustness for edge-deployment satellite triage without full image uplink.
