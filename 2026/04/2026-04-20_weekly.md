📚 [2026-04-20] Weekly Journal Digest (past 7 days Early Access)

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ Generating an Annual 30 m Rice Cover Product for Monsoon Asia (2018–2023) Using Harmonized Landsat and Sentinel-2 Data and the NASA-IBM Geospatial Foundation Model
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Online First)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115256
   - Fine-tunes the NASA-IBM Prithvi foundation model on HLS (Harmonized Landsat-8/9 + Sentinel-2) time series to produce the first continental-scale, annually consistent 30 m rice map across Monsoon Asia (2018–2023); training labels are automatically aggregated from existing regional products.
   - Demonstrates that a Landsat/Sentinel-2 geospatial foundation model can be directly adapted for large-scale, high-frequency crop monitoring without scene-by-scene annotation.
   - Why it matters: A direct proof-of-concept that HLS-pretrained foundation models transfer to food-security applications at continental scale.

2. Global and Boreal Estimates of Woody Aboveground Biomass for 2020
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Online First)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115406
   - Fuses GEDI LiDAR footprint data with Landsat and Sentinel-2 spectral-temporal metrics to generate 30 m global and boreal woody aboveground biomass maps for 2020; uncertainty layers are co-delivered for IPCC reporting.
   - Provides the highest-resolution spatially explicit biomass benchmark yet released, anchored to field plots and ICESat-2 cross-calibration.
   - Why it matters: Directly enables carbon stock accounting and supports MRV (Measurement, Reporting, Verification) under REDD+ frameworks.

3. Tracking Land Surface Deformation in Lowland Permafrost Regions Across the Arctic Exploiting the First Decade of Copernicus Sentinel-1
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Online First)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115409
   - Exploits the full 2014–2024 Sentinel-1 archive to produce circumpolar time series of seasonal and long-term surface subsidence across Arctic lowland tundra using MT-InSAR; detects accelerating thaw in previously stable zones.
   - Offers the first decade-scale, pan-Arctic InSAR deformation record, revealing non-linear acceleration in multiple regions after 2021.
   - Why it matters: Quantifies permafrost carbon vulnerability at landscape scale, critical input to Earth system models.

4. Making Footprints Move: Temporal Disaggregation of Building Footprint Data Using Sentinel-2 Imagery and Bayesian Deep Learning
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Online First)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115413
   - Assigns construction dates to static building footprints by training a Bayesian U-Net on Sentinel-2 time series, producing probabilistic temporal metadata and uncertainty maps for national-scale datasets.
   - Converts timeless footprint inventories into spatio-temporal urban growth records without manual annotation.
   - Why it matters: Unlocks retrospective urban expansion analysis for risk assessment and population modelling from existing cadastral data.

5. Assessing English Peatland Dynamics Using MT-InSAR
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Online First)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115414
   - Applies multi-temporal InSAR over English peatlands to quantify seasonal and inter-annual surface height changes as proxies for water-table fluctuation and carbon flux; distinguishes drained degraded bogs from rewetted sites.
   - First national-scale MT-InSAR peatland assessment in the UK, resolving sub-centimetre seasonal dynamics across heterogeneous land management.
   - Why it matters: Peatlands store ~30% of global soil carbon; satellite-derived dynamics are essential for national GHG inventories.

6. ⭐ A Framework for Integrating Spatiotemporal Deep Learning Methods with Landsat for Annual Land Cover and Impervious Surface Mapping (LCAMS)
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Online First)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115347
   - Introduces LCAMS, a prototype spatiotemporal deep learning pipeline coupling per-year Landsat spectral-temporal metrics with temporal consistency enforcement to generate annual NLCD-aligned land cover and impervious surface maps at 30 m from 1985 onward.
   - Establishes the first operationally scalable Landsat-native spatiotemporal learning architecture intended as the backbone of the next-generation Annual NLCD.
   - Why it matters: Directly relevant to Landsat time-series foundation model development; sets the benchmark architecture for large-scale land-cover pretraining datasets.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

7. Mask-CDKD: A Source-Free and Label-Free Cross-Domain Knowledge Distillation Framework from SAM for Satellite Onboard VHR Land-Cover Mapping
   - Journal: ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (Online First)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2026.03.035
   - Distills a pretrained SAM teacher into a lightweight student model using masked image modeling and a Multi-scale Mixture-of-Adapters module, entirely without target-domain labels; evaluated on LuoJiaCDKD-100K (100 K global VHR images) and achieves real-time inference on satellite hardware.
   - Demonstrates that foundation-model priors can be compressed for onboard deployment, removing the cloud-offload bottleneck for time-sensitive applications.
   - Why it matters: Enables immediate AI inference on-orbit, a key step toward autonomous Earth observation satellites.
