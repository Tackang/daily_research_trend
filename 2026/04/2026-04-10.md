📡 [2026-04-10] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Near Real-Time Monitoring of Global Land-Ocean Cover Dynamics `arXiv:2604.05384`
   - Integrates remote sensing and reanalysis data to track global forest cover and Arctic sea ice at 5-day intervals (2018–2025); finds global forest coverage (~60%) dangerously close to the 54% lower safe planetary boundary.
   - Why it matters: First operational near-real-time global LULC system with embedded climate safety thresholds—critical for early-warning policy tools.

2. ⭐ HighFM: A Foundation Model for High-Frequency Earth Observation Data `arXiv:2604.04306`
   - Adapts SatMAE masked autoencoding on >2 TB of Meteosat SEVIRI geostationary imagery; achieves strong performance on cloud masking and active fire detection with fine-grained temporal encodings.
   - Why it matters: Shows temporally dense low-resolution geostationary data can power competitive EO foundation models—complementary design space to Landsat FM development.

3. ⭐ LLaRS: Unified Foundation Model for All-in-One Multi-Modal RS Restoration `arXiv:2604.05629`
   - Single model handles cloud removal, dehazing, denoising, and super-resolution across heterogeneous RS sensors using optimal transport for band alignment and MoE layers; introduces LLaRS1M, a million-scale multi-task RS dataset.
   - Why it matters: Language-prompted unified RS foundation model outperforms task-specific approaches—strong benchmark for multi-sensor pretraining strategies.

4. Prior-Guided Multimodal Change Detection from Optical-SAR Images `arXiv:2604.05527`
   - STSF-Net jointly models modality-specific and spatio-temporal features, with semantic priors from pretrained FMs guiding adaptive fusion; introduces Delta-SN6, first open VHR fully polarimetric optical-SAR multiclass change dataset.
   - Why it matters: New benchmark + FM-as-prior strategy pushes multimodal change detection toward production-ready cross-sensor pipelines.

5. CloudMamba: Uncertainty-Guided Dual-Scale Mamba for Cloud Detection `arXiv:2604.06844`
   - Two-stage framework identifies uncertain thin-cloud regions and refines them separately using a CNN+Mamba hybrid; linear complexity while capturing large-scale structure and fine boundary details.
   - Why it matters: Tackles persistent thin-cloud contamination in satellite time-series with an efficient, uncertainty-aware architecture.

6. Canopy Tree Height Estimation via Quantile Regression with Calibrated Uncertainty `arXiv:2604.06988`
   - Applies quantile regression to satellite-based tree height models with minimal architectural changes; shows uncertainty correlates with terrain complexity and vegetation diversity.
   - Why it matters: Provides statistically rigorous uncertainty estimates for biomass/carbon mapping pipelines directly from satellite data.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
7. FermiLink: A Unified Agent Framework for Multidomain Autonomous Scientific Simulations `arXiv:2604.03460`
   - Open-source multi-agent system supporting ~50 scientific software packages across 9 domains; four-layer progressive disclosure mechanism reproduced 56.1% of published figures in 132 real-world tasks.
   - Why it matters: Demonstrates scalable agentic orchestration of domain-specific simulators—key infrastructure for AI-driven scientific discovery loops.

8. MTA-Agent: An Open Recipe for Multimodal Deep Search Agents `arXiv:2604.06376`
   - Automatically selects tools and parameters to retrieve and validate evidence from visual and textual sources in a multi-step reasoning pipeline; fully open-source blueprint.
   - Why it matters: Practical, reproducible framework for building general-purpose multimodal research agents that combine web search with visual grounding.

💡 One-line comment: EO foundation models are diversifying beyond HR optical sensors—geostationary, multi-sensor, and language-prompted RS FMs all emerged this week, signaling the field's push toward all-condition, all-sensor earth monitoring.
