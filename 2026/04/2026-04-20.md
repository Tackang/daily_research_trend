🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Magnitude Is All You Need? Rethinking Phase in Quantum Encoding of Complex SAR Data `arXiv:2604.14229`
   - Benchmarks five quantum encoding strategies on complex-valued SAR data (MSTAR); magnitude-only hybrid models reach 99.57% accuracy on 3-class tasks, but phase becomes critical (+21.65%) in purely quantum architectures.
   - Shows that optimal encoding depends on model architecture, not data properties — a key design guide for quantum ML applied to SAR.
   - Why it matters: Establishes practical encoding rules for satellite SAR data in the NISQ era, laying groundwork for quantum-accelerated earth observation.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
2. DR³-Eval: Towards Realistic and Reproducible Deep Research Evaluation `arXiv:2604.14683`
   - Proposes a reproducible benchmark for deep research agents requiring planning, retrieval, multimedia analysis, and report generation across a controlled noisy corpus.
   - Evaluates five dimensions (retrieval, factual accuracy, source attribution, task compliance, analytical depth) and validates against human assessments.
   - Why it matters: Rigorous evaluation of AI research agents is the bottleneck for trustworthy AI-for-science adoption.

3. AgentGA: Evolving Code Solutions in Agent-Seed Space `arXiv:2604.14655`
   - Applies genetic algorithms at the agent-seed level (task prompts + parent archives) rather than mutating code directly; each generation spawns a fresh autonomous run.
   - Achieves 74.52% Exceeds-Human on Weco-Kaggle Lite vs. 54.15% for AIDE — offspring receiving parent archives consistently outperform cold-start runs.
   - Why it matters: Evolutionary search over agent initialization is a new paradigm for automated scientific exploration beyond prompt engineering.

4. SGA-MCTS: Decoupling Planning from Execution via Training-Free Atomic Experience Retrieval `arXiv:2604.14712`
   - Extracts State-Goal-Action atoms from offline MCTS trajectories and retrieves them at inference time using hybrid symbolic-semantic search — no fine-tuning needed.
   - Frozen open-weights models match GPT-5-level performance, achieving "System 2 depth at System 1 speeds."
   - Why it matters: Training-free multi-step reasoning unlocks powerful planning for scientific pipelines without model updates.

5. Layered Mutability: Continuity and Governance in Persistent Self-Modifying Agents `arXiv:2604.14717`
   - Identifies five mutation layers in persistent agents (pretraining → weight-level) and shows reverting an agent's self-description fails to restore original behavior after memory accumulation (hysteresis ratio: 0.68).
   - Introduces drift and hysteresis metrics to detect unauthorized behavioral trajectories from locally-reasonable but cumulatively misaligned updates.
   - Why it matters: Behavioral drift in long-running AI agents is an underappreciated safety risk for scientific automation pipelines.

6. Simulating Human Cognition: Heartbeat-Driven Autonomous Thinking Activity Scheduling for LLM-based AI Systems `arXiv:2604.14178`
   - Periodic "heartbeat" signals coordinate modular cognitive functions (planning, critique, memory, imagination) in LLM agents via meta-learning from historical interaction data.
   - New thinking modules can be added plug-and-play without retraining the base scheduler.
   - Why it matters: Modular, adaptive cognitive scheduling makes AI scientific assistants more extensible and robust in open-ended tasks.

7. Credo: Declarative Control of LLM Pipelines via Beliefs and Policies `arXiv:2604.14401`
   - Represents agent semantic state as structured beliefs; declarative policies automatically govern model selection, retrieval strategy, and corrective re-execution without touching pipeline code.
   - A database-backed control plane enables auditable, adaptive execution across dynamic agentic workflows.
   - Why it matters: Declarative pipeline governance improves reproducibility and auditability — essential for scientific LLM workflows.
