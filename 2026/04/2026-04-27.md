🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. UHR-DETR: Efficient End-to-End Small Object Detection for Ultra-High-Resolution Remote Sensing Imagery `arXiv:2604.21435`
   - Proposes a transformer detector with Coverage-Maximizing Sparse Encoder and Global-Local Decoupled Decoder designed for UHR satellite scenes.
   - Achieves +2.8% mAP and 10× inference speedup over sliding-window baselines on the STAR benchmark.
   - Why it matters: Solves the compute bottleneck for dense small-object detection in high-resolution satellite imagery.

2. The First Challenge on Remote Sensing Infrared Image Super-Resolution at NTIRE 2026 `arXiv:2604.21312`
   - Benchmarks 13 teams on 4× thermal infrared super-resolution from satellite-acquired imagery; 115 registered participants.
   - Establishes standard evaluation protocols and datasets for remote sensing thermal enhancement research.
   - Why it matters: Defines the baseline for IR super-resolution, critical for land surface temperature and heat-based analysis.

3. SyMTRS: Benchmark Multi-Task Synthetic Dataset for Depth, Domain Adaptation and Super-Resolution in Aerial Imagery `arXiv:2604.21801`
   - Generates 2048×2048 aerial RGB images with pixel-perfect depth maps, night-time domain variants, and ×2/4/8 low-resolution pairs.
   - Enables unified benchmarking of depth estimation, domain adaptation, and super-resolution under consistent ground truth.
   - Why it matters: First aerial dataset to jointly cover geometry, robustness, and resolution — reduces fragmentation in RS multi-task research.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
4. From Research Question to Scientific Workflow: Leveraging Agentic AI for Science Automation `arXiv:2604.21910`
   - Three-layer agentic architecture (semantic → deterministic → knowledge) converts natural language research questions into reproducible workflow DAGs.
   - Tested on the 1000 Genomes workflow: 83% intent accuracy (vs. 44% baseline) and 92% reduction in data transfer overhead.
   - Why it matters: Directly bridges the gap from research intent to executable scientific pipelines — a key step toward autonomous science.

5. From Data to Theory: Autonomous LLM Agents for Materials Science `arXiv:2604.19789`
   - LLM agent autonomously selects equation forms, writes and runs code, and validates discovered theories against raw experimental data.
   - Recovers Hall-Petch and Paris law equations; proposes novel strain-dependent HOMO-LUMO relationships; GPT-5 performs best.
   - Why it matters: Proof-of-concept for end-to-end autonomous theory generation — human validation still essential but loop is closing.

6. Machine Learning Supports Existence of Previously Unrecognized Transient Astronomical Phenomena in Historical Observatory Images `arXiv:2604.18799`
   - ML classifier (AUC 0.81) trained on 250 expert-labeled pre-Sputnik image pairs distinguishes real transients from plate defects at scale.
   - Applying to 107k+ candidates reveals significant clustering during nuclear testing windows (p<0.0001), suggesting an unknown transient population.
   - Why it matters: Demonstrates ML-driven discovery in historical scientific archives — a template for mining underexplored observational data.
