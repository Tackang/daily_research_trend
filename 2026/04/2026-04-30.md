🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. Agentic AI for Remote Sensing: Technical Challenges and Research Directions `arXiv:2604.24919`
   - EO workflows involve georeferenced, multi-modal, temporally structured data where operations like reprojection/resampling propagate errors through pipelines — fundamentally different from generic agentic AI.
   - Proposes structured geospatial state, tool-aware reasoning, and verifier-guided execution as core design principles, with calls for EO-specific benchmarks and trajectory-level evaluation.
   - Why it matters: First systematic framework for adapting agentic AI to operational EO pipelines — a blueprint for future RS foundation model deployment.

2. Edge-Cloud Collaborative Reconstruction via Structure-Aware Latent Diffusion for Downstream RS Perception `arXiv:2604.25319`
   - SALD separates satellite imagery into compressed low-frequency content + lightweight structural cues at the edge; a cloud-side diffusion model reconstructs detail using structural priors without hallucination.
   - Outperforms compression baselines on MSCM and UCMerced datasets for both scene classification and small-target detection under severe bandwidth constraints.
   - Why it matters: Practical satellite-to-ground pipeline that preserves semantic structure where it counts most — downstream task performance.

3. Deep Radiometric Normalization for Cross-Sensor Orbital Imagery `arXiv:2604.25208`
   - A cGAN (U-Net generator + PatchGAN discriminator) harmonizes multi-mission lunar imagery (Chandrayaan-2 TMC + SELENE), achieving tonal uniformity and reduced seam artifacts.
   - Learning-based cross-sensor normalization outperforms traditional histogram methods for seamless planetary mosaicking.
   - Why it matters: Cross-sensor radiometric harmonization is directly analogous to Landsat + Sentinel multi-mission fusion — techniques transfer.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

4. AutoResearchBench: Benchmarking AI Agents on Complex Scientific Literature Discovery `arXiv:2604.25256`
   - Defines two task types: Deep Research (trace a target paper through multi-step probing) and Wide Research (collect all papers meeting open-ended conditions); best models score only 9.39% and 9.31% IoU.
   - Many models fall below 5%, revealing major gaps between perceived and actual AI research agent capability.
   - Why it matters: Honest, rigorous audit of where LLM agents fail at scientific synthesis — essential reading before deploying research automation.

5. Plausible but Wrong: A Case Study on Agentic Failures in Astrophysical Workflows `arXiv:2604.25345`
   - CMBAgent evaluated across 18 astrophysical tasks; primary failure mode is silent incorrect computation — syntactically valid code producing physically inconsistent but plausible-looking results.
   - Domain-specific context improves one-shot performance ~6×, but complex reasoning tasks remain unsolved with no obvious failure signals.
   - Why it matters: AI scientific agents fail silently in domain workflows — a warning for anyone using LLM agents in Earth science pipelines.

6. Recursive Multi-Agent Systems `arXiv:2604.25917`
   - RecursiveMAS casts multi-agent collaboration as latent-space recursion via a RecursiveLink module enabling cross-agent state transfer, trained with an inner-outer loop algorithm.
   - Achieves +8.3% accuracy across 9 benchmarks (math, science, medicine, code) with 1.2–2.4× speedup and 34–75% token reduction vs. baselines.
   - Why it matters: Latent-space agent coordination is a more efficient alternative to token-heavy debate frameworks — scales better for scientific tasks.

7. OxyGent: Making Multi-Agent Systems Modular, Observable, and Evolvable `arXiv:2604.25602`
   - Oxy abstraction unifies agents, tools, LLMs, and reasoning flows as swappable components; permission-driven dynamic planning generates runtime execution graphs for full observability.
   - OxyBank module enables automated data management and continuous improvement in production MAS deployments.
   - Why it matters: Open-source production-grade MAS framework with explicit observability — a practical template for EO multi-agent pipelines.

💡 One-line comment: Agentic AI is entering domain science in force, but today's papers collectively warn that silent errors — not obvious crashes — are the real threat to reliable deployment.
