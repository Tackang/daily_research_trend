📡 [2026-09-30] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. The Earth in One Gaze: Training-Free Active Focus for UHR Remote Sensing Understanding `arXiv:2609.31747`
   - GazeEarth lets a frozen multimodal LLM pick question-relevant regions, then warps the full ultra-high-resolution scene so those regions are enlarged and the periphery is compressed.
   - Gains 4.6–9.4 points on three benchmarks with at most two model calls and no training.
   - Why it matters: Shows large RS foundation models can be improved at inference time, with no retraining.

2. Enhancing Foundation Models for Imbalanced SAR Ship Classification via Targeted Oversampling `arXiv:2609.31657`
   - Applies ADASYN and SVM-SMOTE in embedding space to DOFA and SAR-JEPA on OpenSARShip.
   - Macro-F1 improves by 4–6 points.
   - Why it matters: A cheap fix for class imbalance when adapting EO foundation models downstream.

3. Cross-Dataset Transfer and Unknown-Class Detection in Imbalanced SAR Ship Classification `arXiv:2609.31658`
   - Tests six pretrained models across two SAR ship datasets. In-domain balanced accuracy reaches 73.4%, but cross-dataset transfer is near chance for some models.
   - Task-specific uncertainty scores detect unknown classes better than MC-dropout variance.
   - Why it matters: Shows domain shift still limits pretrained EO models, which matters for cross-sensor generalization.

4. Stacked Intelligent Metasurface-Diffractive Deep Neural Networks for Onboard Terrain Classification from SAR Level-0 Raw Data `arXiv:2609.34333`
   - A physical wave-domain network classifies terrain directly from raw SAR data onboard the satellite, at about 90% accuracy on binary tasks.
   - Reduces the need for downlink bandwidth and ground-side compute.
   - Why it matters: Points to in-orbit inference for future EO systems.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
5. CP-Agent: Crystal Plasticity Simulation Workflows `arXiv:2609.31790`
   - LLM agents autonomously run crystal plasticity simulation workflows.
   - They recover the experimentally observed weakened, split basal texture.
   - Why it matters: A concrete case of agents running real scientific simulations end to end.

6. BioDyad: Synchronize Biomedical Discovery `arXiv:2609.31939`
   - Couples discovery and engineering agent hierarchies and scores highest on a 76-task biomedical benchmark under a two-hour budget.
   - Why it matters: Suggests splitting hypothesis generation from execution helps scientific-discovery agents.

7. Symbolic Guidance for LLM Agents in Distributed Multiagent Coordination `arXiv:2609.31963`
   - Compares levels of guidance, from open-ended language to fully prescribed algorithms.
   - Intermediate levels consistently beat both extremes.
   - Why it matters: Gives design guidance on how much autonomy to give multi-agent systems.

8. GameBoyWorlds: A Testbed for Self-Improvement in Embodied Video Games `arXiv:2609.32093`
   - A benchmark of 500 interactive tasks for self-improving agents.
   - Frontier models complete fewer than 50% of the tasks without demonstrations.
   - Why it matters: Shows how far current agents are from autonomous self-improvement.

💡 One-line comment: SAR and foundation-model adaptation dominated Earth observation today, and no new Landsat or optical foundation-model papers appeared, so none get ⭐. Agent-for-science work is moving from demos to end-to-end workflows.
