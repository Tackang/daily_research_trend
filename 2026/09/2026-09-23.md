📡 [2026-09-23] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. SatOV: Restoring Spatial Priors for Training-Free Open-Vocabulary Segmentation in Remote Sensing Imagery `arXiv:2609.22834`
   - Training-free framework fuses CLIP attention across layers and uses RGB-guided upsampling to restore spatial detail for open-vocabulary segmentation.
   - Why it matters: Enables zero-shot class-agnostic mapping on DOTA/LoveDA-style imagery without costly retraining.

2. Hapi: A Multivariable Land-Surface Transformer for Medium-Range Hydrological Forecasting at Continental Scale `arXiv:2609.22702`
   - U-Net Swin Transformer forecasts discharge, runoff, snow water equivalent, and soil wetness 24-72h ahead across the contiguous US, beating physics-based and AI baselines.
   - Why it matters: Shows land-surface transformers can outperform operational hydrology models at continental scale in near real time.

3. MarsRecon: Self-Supervised and Multimodal Surface Representations for Mars `arXiv:2609.22379`
   - Masked-autoencoder pretraining on unlabeled HiRISE orbital imagery, aligned with text and geographic coordinates for retrieval.
   - Why it matters: Demonstrates the self-supervised EO foundation-model recipe transferring to planetary surface analysis with scarce labels.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
4. AgentRouter: Heterogeneous Model Routing for Cost-Optimal Multi-Step Agentic Workflows `arXiv:2609.22951`
   - A lightweight 12M-parameter classifier routes each agent trajectory step to one of four model tiers, cutting cost 72% while retaining 97.3% of frontier quality.
   - Why it matters: Practical recipe for making multi-agent pipelines affordable at scale without sacrificing much accuracy.

5. PINNForge: Execution-Grounded Evolutionary Design of Physics-Informed Neural Networks for PDE Solving via LLMs `arXiv:2609.23023`
   - LLM-driven evolutionary search generates, executes, and refines PINN configurations using training feedback; best on 24/25 PDE benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: Automates a notoriously fiddly PINN design process, directly relevant to AI-for-science automation.

6. OptiSkill: A Hierarchical and Evolving SkillBank for LLM-Based Optimization Modeling `arXiv:2609.22987`
   - Maintains a validated, hierarchical "SkillBank" of reusable OR formulation knowledge that LLMs draw on to convert NL problems into math programs.
   - Why it matters: Reusable-skill approach could generalize to other scientific modeling agents beyond optimization.

7. FireWorldBench: Benchmarking Complex Physical World Intelligence through Coupled-Field Fire Dynamics `arXiv:2609.23064`
   - 520-scenario, 9,074-QA benchmark testing whether MLLMs/agents can infer physical state, causal mechanisms, and intervention outcomes in fire dynamics.
   - Why it matters: Probes whether AI agents actually understand physical world dynamics, a prerequisite for trustworthy scientific-discovery agents.
