📚 [2026-09-28] Weekly Journal Digest (past 7 days Early Access)

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ Generating an Annual 30 m Rice Cover Product for Monsoon Asia (2018–2023) Using Harmonized Landsat and Sentinel-2 Data and the NASA-IBM Geospatial Foundation Model
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Vol 335)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115256
   - Fine-tunes the NASA-IBM Prithvi geospatial foundation model on harmonized Landsat/Sentinel-2 to map 30 m rice cover across Monsoon Asia, 79–92% accuracy across climate zones.
   - Why it matters: direct evidence a Landsat-based foundation model generalizes to a continental crop-mapping task with limited labels.

2. Temporal Attention Multi-Resolution Fusion of Satellite Image Time-Series, Applied to Landsat-8/9 and Sentinel-2: All Bands, Any Time, at Best Spatial Resolution
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Vol 334)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2025.115159
   - A temporal-attention fusion network reconstructs all Landsat/Sentinel-2 bands at the finest resolution for arbitrary dates from irregular time series.
   - Why it matters: gives dense, gap-free Landsat+Sentinel-2 cubes that could serve as better pretraining input for EO foundation models.

3. Tracking Forest Carbon and Growth in Large-Scale Passive Restoration Using Multi-Temporal Airborne LiDAR
   - Journal: ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (Vol 231)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2026.07.013
   - Repeat airborne LiDAR (2012 vs 2018) over 69 km² of passively restored forest land in Brazil quantifies 6-year height and carbon gain at 30 m resolution.
   - Why it matters: a rare bias-corrected, high-resolution benchmark for validating satellite-based carbon-stock and biomass-change products.

4. SARMelt: MGLANet-Driven High-Resolution Surface Melt Mapping of Circum-Antarctic Ice Shelves from Sentinel-1 Imagery During 2015–2023
   - Journal: ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (Vol 240)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2026.07.012
   - A multiscale global-local attention network maps circum-Antarctic ice-shelf surface melt from Sentinel-1 SAR at 40 m, IoU 90.8%.
   - Why it matters: demonstrates attention-based architectures scaling to continent-wide SAR time series, transferable to other land-cover/change tasks.

5. Congo Basin Carbon Cycle Responses to Global Change
   - Journal: Global Change Biology (Vol 32, Issue 1)
   - DOI: 10.1111/gcb.70688
   - Synthesizes remote sensing and in-situ data to assess how deforestation, climate, and CO2 drivers are shifting the Central African carbon cycle.
   - Why it matters: flags where higher-resolution satellite carbon monitoring is most needed — a gap Landsat-scale products could fill.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
6. Reimagining Research Papers as Interactive and Reliable AI Agents
   - Journal: Nature
   - DOI: 10.1038/s41586-026-11044-y
   - Introduces "Paper2Agent," which converts a scientific manuscript's text, figures, and data into an interactive AI agent that can be queried and can interact with other paper-agents.
   - Why it matters: an early concrete instance of turning static literature into queryable, composable multi-agent scientific knowledge.

7. Capable Language Models Can Outgrow the Benefits of Collaboration
   - Journal: Nature Machine Intelligence
   - DOI: 10.1038/s42256-026-01268-y
   - Across 260 configurations (6 benchmarks, 5 architectures, 3 LLM families), finds a capability-saturation threshold beyond which adding more agents stops helping, predicting outcomes with 94% accuracy on SWE-bench/Terminal-Bench.
   - Why it matters: practical guidance on when multi-agent scaling is worth the compute versus a single strong model.

📊 This week's journal trend: Landsat/Sentinel-2 foundation-model fine-tuning is moving from generic benchmarks into operational products (rice mapping, dense reconstruction), while AI-agent research is pivoting from "more agents is better" to knowing the limits of collaboration.
