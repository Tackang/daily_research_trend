2. Modular Deep Learning Mechanisms for Auditable Next-Day Wildfire Spread Prediction `arXiv:2609.17763`
   - Adds wind/slope-based attention, retrieval-augmented correction, and fire-conditioned gating to 5 backbones on the Next Day Wildfire Spread benchmark (best F1 0.4216).
   - Why it matters: finds interpretable attention doesn't guarantee physically accurate fire-spread predictions, a caution for auditable EO forecasting models.
