📡 [2026-09-26] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ Recoverable Geographic Location Information in Earth-Observation Embeddings `arXiv:2609.29151`
   - Tests whether Tessera v1, v1.1 and AlphaEarth embeddings encode geographic coordinates, using European solar farm data.
   - All three models leak recoverable location information, and AlphaEarth shows the strongest distance correlation.
   - Why it matters: Location leakage can inflate downstream scores, so geographic-split evaluation is needed for any Landsat foundation model.

2. GeoNLI - A Natural Language Interpreter for Satellite Imagery `arXiv:2609.28741`
   - Ensembles EarthMind with RemoteSAM variants by majority voting for captioning, VQA and visual grounding.
   - Reports 82% on captioning, 83.32% on VQA and 64.94% on grounding.
   - Why it matters: A single pipeline covering several satellite-imagery language tasks is a practical baseline.

3. Efficient Continuous DEM Reconstruction under Limited Target-Resolution Supervision `arXiv:2609.29864`
   - SCOPE predicts latent coefficient fields with reusable local Fourier residuals to learn continuous terrain from coarse DEMs.
   - Reduces error by about 12% at unseen scales at low compute cost.
   - Why it matters: Multi-scale terrain is useful for biomass and land-cover work where high-resolution DEMs are scarce.

4. Evaluating Cross-region Generalization for Wavelet-Diffusion Precipitation Downscaling `arXiv:2609.28749`
   - Trains on Oklahoma MRMS radar data and tests on six US regions.
   - Stays competitive elsewhere, with gains concentrated in high-intensity precipitation.
   - Why it matters: It gives a template for testing geographic transfer of generative models.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
5. Generative Atmospheric Super-Resolution from Heterogeneous In Situ Observations `arXiv:2609.29027`
   - Conditions a pretrained diffusion model on radiosonde, aircraft and surface data through composable observation interfaces.
   - Cuts error by 9.24% versus radiosonde-only input, without retraining.
   - Why it matters: A frozen generative prior can absorb new sensor types without retraining.

6. BaseCamp: An Agentic AI Framework for Automating DNA Sequencing Data Pipelines `arXiv:2609.28557`
   - Six specialized agents built on locally run fine-tuned LLMs automate decisions from QC to reporting.
   - Keeps established bioinformatics tools and human oversight.
   - Why it matters: It shows a template for wrapping agents around existing scientific tools while staying reproducible.

7. Driving Epidemic Models with AI Agents: the Epydemix Agent Framework `arXiv:2609.28692`
   - Adds a natural-language agent interface to epidemic modeling software.
   - Across 50 sessions, it cut turns, tokens and cost on most tasks versus direct Python use.
   - Why it matters: Agent-friendly interfaces to scientific software can be measured in cost.

8. PFArena: Benchmarking Language Models for Protein Modification `arXiv:2609.28921`
   - Benchmarks protein language models, LLMs and LLM agents on protein engineering tasks.
   - Protein-specific models do better at open-ended mutation generation, and general LLMs do better at ranking when fitness data is available.
   - Why it matters: Which model type works best depends on how much experimental evidence is available.
