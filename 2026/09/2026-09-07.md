🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. STARS-GS: Structure-Aware Regularized Gaussian Splatting for Large-Scale Aerial Surface Reconstruction `arXiv:2609.03447`
   - Introduces structure-aware partitioning and neighborhood-aware geometric constraints for 3D Gaussian Splatting on large-scale aerial photogrammetry.
   - Beats prior Gaussian-based methods by ~9.1% F1 on large-scale aerial surface reconstruction benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: shows 3DGS maturing toward production-grade geospatial mapping/urban modeling from aerial imagery.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
2. AutoGraphForge: Towards Automated Graph Theory Discovery `arXiv:2609.03478`
   - Iterative pipeline that proposes, filters, and tests graph-theory conjectures against ~348K graphs, then formalizes survivors as Lean 4 proofs using DeepSeek-Prover-V2-671B and OProver-32B.
   - Surfaced 6,522 surviving conjectures with machine-verified proofs.
   - Why it matters: concrete end-to-end pipeline for automated scientific discovery, from hypothesis generation to formal verification.

3. Fresh Memory, Stale Plans: Dependency-Scoped Validation for Distributed LLM-Agent Memory `arXiv:2609.03340`
   - Points out that "fresh state" doesn't guarantee a multi-agent plan is still valid; proposes PlanFence, which ties plans to the specific records they depend on and validates only those before acting.
   - In 30 workflows with post-plan revisions, freshness-only baselines failed every task while PlanFence completed all of them.
   - Why it matters: a practical fix for a real failure mode in distributed multi-agent systems as they scale.

4. Speculative Macro Commit for Faster Tool-Using Agents `arXiv:2609.03236`
   - Pairs a large authoritative model with a fast speculative model that pre-executes multi-step tool-call patterns, committing them when they match the main model's decisions.
   - Cuts latency 10–19% on τ²-Bench Telecom and wall time up to 44.9% on AppWorld vs. sequential execution, with minor accuracy trade-offs.
   - Why it matters: speculative decoding-style tricks are moving from token generation to whole agent action sequences.

5. Xiaomi-TabLDM: A Tabular Foundation Model Technical Report `arXiv:2609.03880`
   - Foundation model for tabular classification/regression via in-context learning (no fine-tuning), pretrained on synthetic data from structural causal models with a sparse MoE architecture.
   - Ranks #1 on OpenML-CTR23 regression while using 82% less training time than the top prior tabular FM.
   - Why it matters: tabular data foundation models are catching up to vision/language FMs in generality and efficiency.

6. GPS-Bench: A Governance Policy Benchmark for Automating Policy Analysis `arXiv:2609.03553`
   - Benchmark for LLM-based simulation of policy outcomes, grounding actor personas in real legislative/lobbying/regulatory records and tracing downstream effects, not just pass/fail.
   - Finds fine-tuning on grounded records gives the strongest impact prediction; multi-agent decomposition adds interpretability of mechanism rather than raw accuracy.
   - Why it matters: a rare rigorous testbed for agentic systems applied to real-world institutional/scientific policy reasoning.
