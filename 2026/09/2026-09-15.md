📡 [2026-09-15] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Earth-Agent-Pro: Towards Real-World Full-Chain Earth Observation with Agents `arXiv:2609.12533`
   - LLM-agent framework that plans and executes full EO workflows (RGB, spectral, remote sensing products) using expert-authored skills and a workflow-tracking memory.
   - Why it matters: pushes EO from single-model inference toward agentic, end-to-end scientific pipelines.

2. When Ground-Truth Fidelity Matters: UAS Multispectral Detection of Wheat Streak Mosaic Virus `arXiv:2609.12169`
   - Orchestrated UAS multispectral imagery pipeline with vision transformers for early crop disease detection.
   - Why it matters: shows label-quality bottlenecks in operational agri-remote-sensing, relevant to any field-scale monitoring task.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
3. Occamy-1.0: Open Pareto-frontier 35B Intelligence for Co-work `arXiv:2609.11977`
   - Open 35B-parameter model tuned for co-work agentic tasks spanning info gathering, tool use, coding, and file manipulation.
   - Why it matters: another open model closing the gap on proprietary agentic coding assistants.

4. BlueLM-GUI: A Real-Device-Centric Flywheel for Self-Improving Mobile GUI Agents `arXiv:2609.12394`
   - Three-stage pretrain/fine-tune/RL recipe trained on real mobile devices for self-improving GUI agents.
   - Why it matters: real-device training loops may generalize better than simulator-only GUI agent training.

5. AIM: A Privacy-Aware Interoperable Memory Framework for Multi-Agent Multi-User LLM Systems `arXiv:2609.12320`
   - Memory architecture letting multi-agent, multi-user LLM systems persist private and shared state safely.
   - Why it matters: memory/privacy design is a growing blocker for deploying multi-agent systems at scale.

6. Language Is an Insufficient Substrate for Quantitative Reasoning `arXiv:2609.12105`
   - Argues token-based LLMs are structurally weak at quantitative reasoning and proposes "Large Quantitative Models" as a distinct model class.
   - Why it matters: challenges the assumption that scaling language models alone solves numerical/scientific reasoning.

7. Mined from Scientific Literature: Process Schemas for Atomic Layer Deposition and Etching `arXiv:2609.12139`
   - Uses a literature-mining "schema-miner" to extract structured process knowledge for materials science automation.
   - Why it matters: concrete step toward LLM-driven scientific discovery pipelines in materials/process engineering.
