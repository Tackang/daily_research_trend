🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. PDA++: Field-Aligned Planning and Scene-Adaptive Insertion in Remote Sensing `arXiv:2609.18329`
   - Environment-aware object insertion for synthetic data augmentation, aligning inserted objects to field geometry across optical/SAR imagery.
   - Why it matters: Improves few-shot recognition without costly new labeled data collection.

2. Understanding Dynamic Scenes at Gigapixel Scale: Wide-Area Spatio-Temporal Perception from UAVs `arXiv:2609.18210`
   - Introduces HARD, a 12768×9564 ultra-high-res airborne dataset for detection, tracking, and VQA over wide dynamic scenes.
   - Why it matters: Pushes RS perception benchmarks toward gigapixel, wide-area realism beyond satellite tiles.

3. ⭐ Multimodal Foundation Models Adaptation based on Domain-Aware Relaxed Orthogonal Subspace for Remote Sensing `arXiv:2609.13654`
   - DROS reformulates LoRA-style fine-tuning as data-conditioned subspace learning for adapting large RS foundation models.
   - Why it matters: Directly relevant to efficient Landsat/Sentinel foundation model adaptation strategies.

4. Selective Tool Use for Agentic Change Visual Question Answering in Remote Sensing `arXiv:2609.14523`
   - A VLM agent decides whether to answer directly or invoke a deterministic change-analysis tool for bi-temporal imagery questions.
   - Why it matters: Blends agentic reasoning with reliable change-detection tooling for LULC monitoring.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
5. Position: It is Time to Virtualize Foundation Models with a Self-evolving Operating System Layer `arXiv:2609.19203`
   - Proposes an FM Operating System (FMOS) layer orchestrating memory, model selection, and policy across foundation model interactions.
   - Why it matters: Frames a systems-level abstraction for managing many foundation models at scale.

6. Rethinking Multi-Agent Collaboration: When More Is Less `arXiv:2609.19759`
   - SAIGE shows multi-agent gains are bounded by task structure, not universal, challenging "more agents = better" assumptions.
   - Why it matters: Useful guidance for designing scientific-discovery agent pipelines efficiently.

7. An Architecture for Long-Horizon Agents: Levels, Ticks and Cascaded Intelligence `arXiv:2609.19523`
   - Hierarchical architecture letting agents run continually across day-spanning context windows without forgetting.
   - Why it matters: Addresses long-horizon memory, a bottleneck for autonomous research agents.

8. AutoData: Agentic Search for Pre-training Data Selection `arXiv:2609.19754`
   - An agent iteratively discovers and refines data-selection algorithms for pretraining using validation feedback loops.
   - Why it matters: Automates a key foundation-model pipeline step via agentic search.

💡 One-line comment: Today's crop leans heavily on making agents and foundation models more adaptable and self-managing — efficient fine-tuning (RS) and long-horizon orchestration (AI) are the shared thread.
