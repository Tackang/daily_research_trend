🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Integrating global canopy height models with satellite data for improved forest inventory in Ukraine
   - Journal: Agricultural and Forest Meteorology (Vol. 388, 15 Sept 2026)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.agrformet.2026.111265
   - Benchmarks six global canopy-height models (incl. Landsat/Sentinel-derived) against 2,600+ Ukrainian NFI plots, reaching LiDAR-comparable accuracy (R²=0.77 for volume) where field access is blocked by war.
   - Why it matters: shows satellite-only structural inventories can substitute for ground NFI in conflict/inaccessible regions.

2. ⭐ AgriFM: A multi-source temporal remote sensing foundation model for agriculture mapping
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Vol. 334, 2026)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115234
   - Video-Swin-Transformer-based foundation model pretrained on 25M+ MODIS/Landsat-8-9/Sentinel-2 samples, unifying spatial and phenological temporal modeling for crop mapping.
   - Why it matters: directly relevant to Landsat-centric foundation model design — synchronized spatiotemporal downsampling is a transferable architecture idea.
