🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ Temporal Sensitivity Analysis of Tessera Embeddings `arXiv:2608.27175`
   - Studies how the Tessera EO foundation model's embeddings degrade when computed from shorter observation windows instead of a full year for LULC mapping.
   - Finds temporal coverage needs vary by task, enabling near-real-time mapping with partial-year data.
   - Why it matters: Directly informs temporal-window design for Landsat-style foundation model pretraining.

2. Learning the Target Priors Before Image Translation `arXiv:2608.28517`
   - Proposes LTP-BIT, decoupling target-domain prior learning from cross-modal conversion using large unpaired imagery before fine-tuning.
   - Achieves strong cross-modal (e.g., SAR-to-optical) translation results with minimal task-specific parameters.
   - Why it matters: Cheap adaptation strategy for multi-sensor harmonization (Landsat/Sentinel-like fusion).

3. Mapping Woody Vegetation from Multi-Source Imagery and Prediction Fusion `arXiv:2608.26471`
   - Fuses predictions across multiple imagery sources to improve tree-cover mapping robustness to varying image quality.
   - Cuts error up to 76% while needing far less annotated training data.
   - Why it matters: Data-efficient approach relevant to biomass/carbon-stock mapping pipelines.

4. CRISP: Calibration-Aware Visual State Space Duality for RS Segmentation `arXiv:2608.23746`
   - Introduces a Duality Calibration Operator to fix boundary-smoothing artifacts in Mamba-style state-space segmentation models.
   - Adds an Orthogonal Multi-Prototype head to preserve fine detail at ~30M parameters, competitive on RS benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: Lightweight architecture improvement for LULC segmentation at scale.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
5. PhenoIntel: Lifecycle-Aligned Multi-Agent App for Plant Phenotype Analysis `arXiv:2608.27999`
   - Nine specialized agents split plant phenotyping into verified stages with calibrated uncertainty and full provenance.
   - Runs in-browser on standard hardware, addressing reliability gaps in conversational phenotyping tools.
   - Why it matters: Template for trustworthy multi-agent scientific-discovery pipelines.

6. Efficient Auto-Interpretability of AI Models in Biology `arXiv:2608.27754`
   - Three-stage pipeline interprets sparse autoencoder latents via cross-seed stability and coherence validation, generating testable biological hypotheses.
   - Shows efficiency gains applied to protein structure prediction models.
   - Why it matters: Scalable interpretability method for foundation models used in scientific discovery.

7. Automated Analysis Framework for Multilingual Climate-Health Literature `arXiv:2608.27998`
   - Multi-agent LLM system automates cross-lingual scientific literature review, tested on 32,000+ Chinese-English climate-health papers.
   - Achieves 0.92 F1 and extracts ~2,000 city-literature associations at scale.
   - Why it matters: Demonstrates multi-agent literature mining as a viable tool for climate-adjacent research synthesis.
