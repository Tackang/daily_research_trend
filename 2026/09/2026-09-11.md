📡 [2026-09-11] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Infra-Bench CLS: A Global, Open-Source Benchmark for Critical Infrastructure Classification with Earth Observation Foundation Models `arXiv:2609.09482`
   - New benchmark testing EO foundation models on 18,756 Sentinel-1 SAR + Sentinel-2 multispectral facility images across 7 continents, 13 infrastructure classes.
   - Top foundation model hit 57.9% macro F1 vs 39.2% for a ResNet-18 baseline.
   - Why it matters: shows current geospatial FMs still struggle at facility scale, a gap relevant to any Landsat/Sentinel FM benchmarking work.

2. MethaneFuse: Learning from Multi-Sensor Satellite Observations for Methane Plume Detection `arXiv:2609.09762`
   - Fuses Sentinel-2, Landsat 8/9, EMIT, and Sentinel-5P under partial sensor availability using a new MethaneUnion dataset (8,981 plume cases).
   - Reaches 84.87 F1 at 480m resolution, +5.65 F1 over baseline.
   - Why it matters: demonstrates practical cross-sensor fusion including Landsat, useful pattern for multi-source pretraining pipelines.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
3. OpenDiscoveryTrace: Process Traces for Evaluating AI Scientist Workflows `arXiv:2609.09203`
   - Public dataset of 558 AI-scientist agent trajectories with 9-field step traces (thoughts, tool calls, errors, confidence) across drug discovery, materials science, genomics.
   - Covers GPT-5.4, Claude Opus 4.6, Gemini 3.1 Pro plus open-weight models.
   - Why it matters: moves AI-for-science evaluation beyond final outputs to auditable reasoning process.

4. An Autonomous GeoAI Agent for Arctic Eco-Navigation `arXiv:2609.09374`
   - Human-in-the-loop multi-agent GeoAI system unifying operational, ecological, and community criteria for Arctic ship routing via skyline-based decision support.
   - Accepted at ACM SIGSPATIAL 2026.
   - Why it matters: concrete example of multi-agent systems applied to real-world geospatial decision-making, not just benchmarks.

5. CityPlanner: A Sandbox Agent for Executable Urban Planning `arXiv:2609.09578`
   - Multi-agent framework combining LLM planning with executable feedback for spatial optimization in urban design tasks.
   - Why it matters: another data point on agentic systems closing the loop between LLM reasoning and executable spatial simulation.

6. Do Agents Know When They Succeed? Calibrating Agent Confidence `arXiv:2609.09448`
   - Studies whether LLM agents' internal representations can predict task success, proposing calibration methods for agentic confidence.
   - Why it matters: confidence calibration is a prerequisite for trusting autonomous agents in scientific or geospatial pipelines.

7. Decision-Focused Active Learning for Critical-Materials Recovery `arXiv:2609.09413`
   - Active learning framework that selects experiments based on downstream decision impact rather than pure model uncertainty, applied to materials science.
   - Why it matters: decision-focused active learning could transfer well to sample-efficient remote sensing label collection.

💡 One-line comment: today's crop leans hard into "agents that check their own work" — confidence calibration, process-trace auditing, and executable feedback loops all point at making autonomous AI scientists/GeoAI agents verifiable, not just capable.
