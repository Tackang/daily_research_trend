🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ Geospatial Embeddings Detect Old-Growth Forests but Buffered Spatial Validation Narrows Their Advantage over Sentinel Features `arXiv:2609.28194`
   - Maps old-growth forest over 211,893 ha in Romania's Southern Carpathians using geospatial foundation model embeddings and Sentinel features.
   - Under buffered spatial validation, the embeddings' lead over plain Sentinel features shrinks to near parity.
   - Why it matters: Spatial-autocorrelation leakage inflates foundation model gains, so evaluate mine with buffered splits.

2. AgroBench: A Reproducible Multimodal Benchmark for Weakly Supervised Crop Yield Learning `arXiv:2609.26809`
   - Turns USDA county yield statistics into pixel-level labels, paired with Sentinel-1/2, climate and terrain data.
   - Covers 788,654 crop pixels, over 13M observations, five crops and eight seasons.
   - Why it matters: A ready downstream benchmark for testing geospatial foundation models on weak labels.

3. Temporally Ordered Region-Token Mamba with Logit-Space Diffusion for Remote Sensing Change Detection `arXiv:2609.27149`
   - BMD-CD combines a state-space (Mamba) backbone with diffusion refinement on bitemporal imagery.
   - Reaches F1 of 93.7/96.0/97.8/99.0% on four standard benchmarks at low compute.
   - Why it matters: Efficient change detection is a natural downstream task for LULC-change work.

4. A Comparative Assessment of Global Building and Settlement Datasets `arXiv:2609.28154`
   - Compares seven global products, including Overture Maps, across 135 study areas; Overture is best on vectors (F1 0.786).
   - Accuracy rises with building density, and temporal alignment adds up to 0.060 F1 in fast-growing regions.
   - Why it matters: Shows which built-up layers are reliable enough to use as labels or masks.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
5. Large Knowledge Model: From Papers to a Scientific Reasoning Landscape `arXiv:2609.27297`
   - Converts the literature into reasoning graphs across Question, Workflow and Evidence landscapes.
   - Lets researchers and agents retrieve work by scientific intent and plan investigations.
   - Why it matters: Infrastructure for literature-grounded AI-for-science agents.

6. WhatWorkedBench: Benchmarking Experimental Understanding in AI Agents `arXiv:2609.27490`
   - Tests whether research agents can predict effects of component changes after budgeted experiments.
   - Gaussian-process fitting and code-equivalence encoding improve how well agents recover effects.
   - Why it matters: Measures understanding of experiments, not just final scores, which is central to automated discovery.

7. PR-Smoother: Simulator-Preserving Non-Gaussian Smoothing for Data Assimilation `arXiv:2609.26890`
   - Learns only future-conditioned corrections around a prescribed simulator rollout.
   - Scales from 4 to 16,384 dimensions, jointly inferring state, parameters and sensor bias.
   - Why it matters: A route to combining learned models with physical simulators and observations in Earth-system work.
