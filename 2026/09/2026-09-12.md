🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Optimizing GEDI Simulator Configuration for European Temperate Forests `arXiv:2609.11440`
   - Calibrates NASA GEDI waveform simulator against ~9,500 airborne LiDAR pairs over French forests, cutting mean absolute bias from 1.28m to 0.40m in leaf-off conditions.
   - Why it matters: Better GEDI calibration directly improves biomass/carbon stock estimates used in global carbon monitoring.

2. Toward Interpretable Multimodal Fusion: Heat Conduction Modeling for Hyperspectral and LiDAR Joint Classification `arXiv:2609.11040`
   - Proposes M2Heat, using physics-inspired heat conduction dynamics plus frequency embeddings to fuse hyperspectral and LiDAR for land-cover classification.
   - Why it matters: Physics-grounded fusion offers interpretability that pure deep nets lack, useful for trustworthy LULC mapping.

3. Evaluation of Vision-Language Models Across Diverse Coastal Environments `arXiv:2609.10855`
   - Benchmarks seven modern VLMs on 1,000+ images spanning three Hawaiian coastal regions.
   - Why it matters: Shows how far general-purpose VLMs generalize to niche geospatial/coastal monitoring tasks without fine-tuning.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
4. Autonomous Chemical Mechanistic Discovery through Agentic Reasoning and Validation `arXiv:2609.11147`
   - ARCHE combines a reasoning LLM, a domain computational-chemistry model, and a tool registry to autonomously propose and validate reaction mechanisms.
   - Why it matters: Concrete case study of agentic AI doing real hypothesis-generation-and-validation science, not just literature summarization.

5. Defining AI Agents: A Compendium of Criteria, Metrics, and Benchmarks `arXiv:2609.11018`
   - Surveys agent definitions across five axes (interaction, learning, autonomy, goals, temporal coherence) and releases a public "Agent Compendium" of evaluation methods.
   - Why it matters: A reference point for standardizing what counts as an "agent" amid inconsistent claims in the field.

6. Mr.LHDR: A Benchmark for Multimodal Real-World Long-Horizon Deep Research Agents `arXiv:2609.11318`
   - New benchmark requiring ~12 chained inferences over mixed image/PDF/table/video evidence; best system only hits 43.1% accuracy.
   - Why it matters: Exposes how far current "deep research" agents are from reliable multi-hop, multimodal evidence synthesis.

7. The Agent Incident Registry: Toward Preventing Repeated AI Agent Failures `arXiv:2609.11030`
   - Proposes a structured registry cataloging real-world AI agent failure modes to enable systematic post-mortems.
   - Why it matters: An "incident database" mindset (borrowed from safety engineering) could speed up agent reliability research.
