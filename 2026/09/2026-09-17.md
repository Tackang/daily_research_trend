🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ From Foundation Embeddings to Cropland Maps: Label Efficiency, Temporal Transferability and Independent Human Validation `arXiv:2609.17138`
   - Frozen AlphaEarth foundation embeddings map Maine cropland at 93.7% overall accuracy with only 60k labeled pixels, matching full-dataset performance.
   - Why it matters: shows frozen geospatial embeddings can rival fine-tuned models — directly relevant to Landsat FM label-efficiency questions.

2. Multisource Remote Sensing and Geospatial Analysis of Vineyard Wildfire Impacts and Resilience: The 2019 Kincade Fire `arXiv:2609.16168`
   - Combines Sentinel-2, OpenET, gridMET and terrain data across 4,581 vineyard fields to assess wildfire resilience and recovery through 2021.
   - Why it matters: demonstrates multi-source fusion for post-disaster land resilience analysis, a template for carbon/biomass recovery studies.

3. A Sentinel-2 Benchmark Dataset for Deep-Learning Active-Fire Segmentation Across 25 California Wildfires `arXiv:2609.16199`
   - Releases 2,148 labeled Sentinel-2 image-mask pairs (841 with active fire) plus a ResNet-34 U-Net baseline for rare-class fire segmentation.
   - Why it matters: new open benchmark lowers the barrier for training/testing satellite-based fire detection models.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
4. Metacognitive Steering: Learning the Structure of Scientific Judgment `arXiv:2609.16245`
   - Recovers judgment patterns from scientist interaction traces to steer a trillion-parameter model's reasoning without weight updates; deployed as autonomous system "Columbus-1."
   - Why it matters: activation-level steering for research judgment could generalize to guiding domain-specific (e.g., geospatial) discovery agents.

5. ScienceBuddy: Recursive-in-Recursive Self-Improvement for Interactive Scientific Agents `arXiv:2609.17523`
   - Dual-loop framework where an inner loop refines the agent's operational scaffolding while an outer loop retrains the model, using researcher feedback as training signal.
   - Why it matters: concrete architecture for self-improving scientific research agents, a core "AI for Science" direction.

6. The AI-Enabled Scientific Frontier `arXiv:2609.16258`
   - Large-scale study of 2,507 head-to-head comparisons shows AI now beats scientific computing methods in over half of cases since 2020, though often at higher compute cost.
   - Why it matters: empirical evidence for where AI genuinely outperforms traditional scientific methods versus where it's overhyped.

💡 One-line comment: Frozen foundation embeddings keep proving "good enough" for real-world mapping, while the AI-for-science frontier is shifting from raw benchmarks to steering and self-improving research agents.
