📡 [2026-09-10] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ AstraMoE-SR: Trajectory-Guided Diffusion for Blind Satellite Jitter Deblurring and Super-Resolution `arXiv:2609.07012`
   - Estimates platform-motion trajectories (not blur kernels) via conditional diffusion to fix pushbroom satellite jitter/blur and upscale resolution without auxiliary sensors.
   - Why it matters: Directly useful as a preprocessing step for noisy Landsat/pushbroom imagery feeding foundation models.

2. AAMBERS-UAV: Acquisition-Aware Multimodal Backbone Evaluation and Ranking for UAV Weedy Rice Segmentation `arXiv:2609.05762`
   - Shows standard image-level train/test splits leak acquisition info, flipping which modality (RGB vs RGB+MS) looks best in UAV crop segmentation.
   - Why it matters: A cautionary benchmark-design lesson relevant to any multispectral/UAV LULC evaluation.

3. Map the Possibilities: Spatial Belief Fields for Language-Goal Aerial Navigation `arXiv:2609.05841`
   - SBFNav lets aerial agents track a probability distribution over target locations (not one waypoint), improving language-guided navigation on CityNav (25.9%→32.3% success).
   - Why it matters: Uncertainty-aware spatial reasoning could transfer to change-detection/monitoring agents over large scenes.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
4. The convergent laboratory: when AI reasoning, autonomous experiments, HPC and quantum computing reshape chemistry `arXiv:2609.05643`
   - A TPC26 perspective piece arguing AI reasoning, self-driving labs, HPC, and quantum computing are jointly reaching a tipping point in materials/chemistry discovery.
   - Why it matters: Frames where "AI for science" automation is headed beyond single-tool demos.

5. EdgeMem: LLM-Free Agent Memory Construction and Retrieval via Evidence-Preserving Multi-Anchor Hypergraph `arXiv:2609.05553`
   - Stores raw conversation evidence in a hypergraph instead of LLM-summarized memory, beating 7 reproduced systems on LoCoMo (61.0 vs 58.7) with zero LLM calls for storage/retrieval.
   - Why it matters: Cheaper, more faithful long-horizon memory for research/literature agents.

6. MOAE: Multi-Objective Agent Evolution with Pareto-Preserving Search `arXiv:2609.05992`
   - Evolves LLM agent rollouts via Pareto-preserving search across competing objectives instead of collapsing them into one score, tested on TravelPlanner/AgentDojo.
   - Why it matters: Weight-free multi-objective tuning could help balance accuracy vs. cost in scientific-discovery agents.

7. Explaining AI Agents Through Execution Traces `arXiv:2609.06063`
   - Post-hoc framework turns agent execution traces into trace-faithful natural-language explanations, flagging unsupported claims/evidence gaps.
   - Why it matters: Practical transparency tool for auditing autonomous research/discovery agents.

💡 One-line comment: Today's crop leans toward agent infrastructure (memory, evaluation, explainability) more than new Earth-observation foundation models — the RS side is dominated by data-quality/benchmark-hygiene fixes rather than new architectures.
