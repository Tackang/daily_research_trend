🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Plant Growth Estimation with a Camera-Based Vegetation Index Mapping System for Agricultural Ground Vehicles `arXiv:2609.03872`
   - Ground-vehicle-mounted multispectral camera + RTK-GNSS builds real-time NDVI field maps, using perspective projection to exclude soil/background noise.
   - Why it matters: Cheap in-field alternative to satellite NDVI for validating remote vegetation indices at plot scale.

2. Neural-Network Maxent: Learned Nonlinearity for Desert Locust Distribution Modelling `arXiv:2609.03603`
   - Replaces Maxent's fixed feature dictionary with a GRU trained on 50-day satellite/climate time series, improving AUC 0.792→0.862 with a 7-day forecast lag.
   - Why it matters: Shows how satellite-driven species distribution models can add real forecasting lead time for ecological/pest early-warning.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
3. Causal Foundation Models `arXiv:2609.03003`
   - Introduces pretrained networks that estimate causal quantities (e.g., treatment effects) on new datasets via in-context learning, no retraining needed.
   - Why it matters: Could collapse the custom estimator-per-problem workflow into one general-purpose causal model.

4. SimSkill: A Lifelong Learning AI Agent for Autonomous Mastery of Traffic Simulation `arXiv:2609.03753`
   - Self-evolving agent builds procedural/semantic memory and a reusable skill library without touching the base LLM, improving verified task completion up to 25pp.
   - Why it matters: Concrete evidence that external memory, not model fine-tuning, can drive long-horizon agent skill accumulation.

5. Value-Preserving Architectures for Agentic AI Systems `arXiv:2609.03920`
   - Proposes federated, pluralist, and guard-agent topologies for multi-agent LLM systems to encode privacy, diversity, and fairness constraints.
   - Why it matters: Architecture-level (not prompt-level) approach to trustworthy multi-agent deployment.

6. Dude: A Dual-Detection Multi-Agent System for Paper-Code Discrepancy Detection `arXiv:2609.03416`
   - Multi-agent framework aligns paper/code granularity to catch inconsistencies bidirectionally, boosting recall/F1 by up to ~20%.
   - Why it matters: Direct tool for scientific reproducibility auditing — relevant to "AI for Science" quality control.
