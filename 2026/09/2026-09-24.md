🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ Annual Earth-observation embeddings encode wildfire disturbance and support simplified burned area mapping `arXiv:2609.25734`
   - Uses annual Tessera and AlphaEarth EO embeddings to map burned areas without paired pre/post-fire imagery. Recovers 97% of burned area in California and transfers to European fires with minimal retraining.
   - Why it matters: Shows pretrained EO embeddings can replace bespoke change-detection pipelines, which is relevant to Landsat foundation models.
2. Hi-OPD: Hierarchy-Aware Open-Prompt Detection for Remote Sensing Images `arXiv:2609.25597`
   - Handles inconsistent annotation granularity across sources with 153 atomic categories. Reaches 79.7/72.3 AP50 on DIOR/DOTA-v2.0.
   - Why it matters: Merging heterogeneous label taxonomies is a core obstacle for large-scale RS pretraining.
3. Agentic Building-Aware Satellite Gaussian Splatting for Auditable Urban DSM Reconstruction `arXiv:2609.25578`
   - Uses semantic building masks as priors in neural rendering of satellite DSMs. Building-region MAE drops from 0.844 m to 0.806 m, with auditable decision records.
   - Why it matters: Adds traceability to agent-driven geospatial 3D reconstruction.
4. MIND the Gap: A Geographic Implicit Neural Representation with Adjustable Spatial Scale `arXiv:2609.25492`
   - Learns geographic embeddings with adjustable spatial granularity via Matryoshka distillation. Achieves state of the art on the 52-dataset CoordBench.
   - Why it matters: Scale-flexible location encoders can be paired with EO backbones for multi-resolution tasks.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
5. ArticleMiner: Ontology-Guided Knowledge Graph Construction from Scientific Publications `arXiv:2609.25337`
   - Extracts quantitative data from tables and supplements of papers using domain-specific modules. Evaluated on chemistry, materials, ML, and mineral geochemistry benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: Automates literature-to-data extraction, a key step in scientific discovery pipelines.
6. Lean Pool: An AI-Maintained Archive of Formalized Mathematics `arXiv:2609.25199`
   - Describes a formalized-math repository grown and maintained by AI agents. Content is stored in machine-verifiable form.
   - Why it matters: Verifiable, agent-curated knowledge bases are a template for trustworthy science automation.
7. The AI Neuroscientist: An Interactive Agentic Interface for Neuroimaging Analysis `arXiv:2609.25254`
   - Connects LLMs to neuroimaging tools for natural-language exploration, QC, and analysis of fNIRS data. Removes the need for scripted pipelines.
   - Why it matters: Domain-tool agents lower the barrier to data analysis, and the same pattern applies to EO workflows.
8. Making Agents More Consistent: Skills Should Form Habits for Repeat Tasks `arXiv:2609.25299`
   - Agents mine execution history for deterministic skill variants. Text-to-SQL repeats reach 100% consistency with 14-56% fewer tokens.
   - Why it matters: Repeatable agent behavior is a prerequisite for reliable research automation.
