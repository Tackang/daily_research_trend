📡 [2026-09-21] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Cross-Architecture Foundation-Model Distillation for Edge Flood Segmentation `arXiv:2609.20441`
   - Distills a 300M-param Prithvi-EO-2.0 geospatial foundation model (fine-tuned on Sen1Floods11) into a 0.7M-param EfficientViT-B0 student using unlabeled Sentinel-2 imagery.
   - Why it matters: ⭐ Direct blueprint for shrinking Landsat/Sentinel foundation models to edge-deployable sizes without losing much accuracy.

2. Learning Principal-Agent Contracts for Equitable Smallholder Carbon Farming under Moral Hazard and Adverse Selection `arXiv:2609.20404`
   - Models carbon-farming contract design for smallholder farmers in South Asia/sub-Saharan Africa, addressing why current soil-carbon programs fail to reach them.
   - Why it matters: Connects carbon-stock MRV data (often from satellite/biomass estimates) to the economic mechanisms needed to actually scale it.

3. Enhanced Agriculture-informed Neural Network by Domain Knowledge `arXiv:2609.19466`
   - Predicts N2O emissions from agricultural soils by embedding biochemical domain knowledge into a deep learning model to improve interpretability under sparse observations.
   - Why it matters: Shows how physics/domain priors can compensate for scarce ground-truth in land-management modeling, relevant to LULC/carbon pipelines.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
4. Can 4D Foundation Models Remember? `arXiv:2609.20819`
   - Introduces a benchmark testing whether 4D (video/scene reconstruction) foundation models actually retain memory of previously perceived dynamic environments, beyond pixel-level metrics.
   - Why it matters: Memory evaluation methodology could transfer to assessing temporal consistency in multi-temporal EO foundation models.

5. AgentPProf: Semantic Profiler for Long Horizon AI Agents `arXiv:2609.20301`
   - Proposes a profiling framework for long-running AI agents that pinpoints where failures, unsafe effects, and budget overruns occur across tool/resource use.
   - Why it matters: Practical tooling for debugging the kind of multi-day autonomous science agents this field is racing to build.

6. When AI Agents Commit: Cognitive Serializability Across Data, Evidence, Policy, and Authority `arXiv:2609.20261`
   - Formalizes when an autonomous agent's decision is "valid" as its underlying data, evidence, and policy inputs change mid-reasoning, borrowing from database transaction isolation theory.
   - Why it matters: Addresses a foundational correctness gap in agentic systems now used for scientific discovery automation.

7. SCGFM-ART: Amortized Relational Transport for Structure-Centric Graph Foundation Models `arXiv:2609.20419`
   - Proposes a structure-centric graph foundation model that aligns heterogeneous graph domains onto a shared representation space to improve cross-domain transfer.
   - Why it matters: Cross-domain generalization tricks here parallel challenges in building sensor-agnostic remote sensing foundation models.

💡 One-line comment: Today's crop is thin on satellite-native papers but strong on making foundation models smaller, more memory-aware, and more accountable — the infrastructure work that eventually feeds back into deployable Earth observation systems.
