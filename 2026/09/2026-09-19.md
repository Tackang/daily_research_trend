🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ Earth Surface Immune System for Rapid Monitoring of Unknown Anomalies `arXiv:2609.20662`
   - Proposes ESIA, a bio-inspired system detecting unknown Earth-surface anomalies as unobserved changes in time-series satellite imagery.
   - Why it matters: Combines multimodal foundation models with time-series satellite monitoring, directly relevant to Landsat-style change detection pipelines.

2. Task-Oriented Semantic Feature Transmission for Multi-Task Satellite Remote Sensing over Low-SNR Channels `arXiv:2609.20150`
   - Introduces a semantic communication scheme that compresses and transmits satellite features for multiple downstream tasks under noisy channels.
   - Why it matters: Tackles the bandwidth bottleneck of onboard satellite AI, key for real-time EO pipelines.

3. VPRef: A Cross-Domain Benchmark for Referring Remote Sensing Image Segmentation `arXiv:2609.16486`
   - Builds a cross-domain benchmark for language-referred object segmentation in satellite imagery.
   - Why it matters: Pushes vision-language grounding toward practical, query-driven EO analysis.

4. Nationally Consistent, Locally Incomplete: A Bayesian Remote-Sensing Audit of Rooftop Photovoltaic Registries `arXiv:2609.16294`
   - Uses Bayesian modeling on satellite imagery to audit gaps in national rooftop solar registries.
   - Why it matters: Shows remote sensing as a validation layer for policy-grade infrastructure datasets.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
5. ScientistTwo: Pioneering the Human Knowledge Frontier with Autonomous AI `arXiv:2609.19644`
   - Presents an autonomous AI system aimed at generating and validating novel scientific knowledge beyond existing human results.
   - Why it matters: A concrete step toward closed-loop, agentic scientific discovery automation.

6. Treadstone: A Social-Media-Inspired Platform for Multi-Agent Collaborative Data Analysis `arXiv:2609.19774`
   - Proposes a social-feed-style interaction platform where multiple AI agents collaboratively analyze data.
   - Why it matters: New coordination paradigm for multi-agent systems beyond simple pipelines.

7. DataCanvas-EDU: An Agentic Framework for Instructor-Guided Synthetic Data Generation `arXiv:2609.19617`
   - Agentic framework letting instructors guide LLM agents to generate synthetic training data.
   - Why it matters: Practical use case of agent orchestration for controllable dataset creation.

8. Not All AI Agents Are Equal: Characterizing Resource and Performance Dynamics `arXiv:2609.19947`
   - Empirically characterizes compute/resource trade-offs and performance variance across AI agent architectures.
   - Why it matters: Useful benchmark for choosing agent designs under real deployment constraints.
