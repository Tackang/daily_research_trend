🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ Emerging Flexible Designs for Geospatial Multimodal Foundation Models `arXiv:2606.12595`
   - Controlled apples-to-apples comparison of encoder-only, encoder-decoder, and masked-AE architectures on GEOBench, with emphasis on flexibility across varied spectral band configurations.
   - Why it matters: First fair FM architecture benchmark for EO — directly informs design choices for Landsat multi-band foundation models.

2. Continuous Biome Representations from Earth Observation Embeddings `arXiv:2606.11510`
   - Applies linear classifiers on Clay v1.5 satellite embeddings to generate continuous biome probability vectors across 6 Brazilian biomes; achieves AUC 0.618 vs. 0.570 for species occurrence over discrete categorical labels (1.3M embeddings, 10k+ forest inventory plots).
   - Why it matters: Satellite embeddings outperform categorical biome maps for fine-grained ecological modeling.

3. ZODS-RS: Zero-training Oriented Detection & Segmentation for Remote Sensing `arXiv:2606.10769`
   - Training-free closed-form pipeline combining DINOv3 dense features and SAM-style proposals for oriented bounding box detection and instance segmentation in aerial imagery; benchmarked on FAIR1M and xView.
   - Why it matters: Zero-shot RS detection removes task-specific retraining barriers, enabling rapid cross-sensor deployment.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

4. EurekAgent: Agent Environment Engineering is All You Need For Autonomous Scientific Discovery `arXiv:2606.13662`
   - Frames autonomous scientific discovery as environment engineering — permissions, artifact/Git management, budget awareness, and human-in-the-loop design — achieving new SOTA circle-packing results for under $11 in API costs.
   - Why it matters: Shifts the AI-for-science bottleneck from prescribing workflows to designing the agent environment.

5. Contemporary AI Lacks the Imagination to Diverge or Negate in Science `arXiv:2606.08251`
   - Largest scientist-in-the-loop evaluation to date: 6,749 scientists rated 25,139 LLM-generated ideas across biology, medicine, chemistry, and social sciences; finds LLMs produce homogeneous ideas and cannot spontaneously propose null hypotheses.
   - Why it matters: Quantifies the LLM scientific creativity ceiling against expert judgment at scale — sets a clear benchmark for next-gen AI scientist systems.

💡 One-line comment: EO foundation model design is entering rigorous comparative evaluation, while AI-for-science confronts a measurable creativity gap that better environment engineering may help close.
