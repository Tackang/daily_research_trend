🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. FAF-CD: Frequency-Aware Fusion for Change Detection under Imperfect Multimodal Remote Sensing `arXiv:2606.03114`
   - DINOv3-pretrained ConvNeXt + VMamba decoder with tri-branch Fourier/Haar-wavelet fusion for heterogeneous EO/SAR change detection. Strong results on BRIGHT, LEVIR-CD, WHU-CD with ~24 GFLOPs savings.
   - Why it matters: Robust multimodal CD without needing perfectly aligned or temporally consistent satellite pairs.

2. ML vs. DL for Transferable Satellite-Derived Bathymetry via Sentinel-2 `arXiv:2606.02764`
   - Benchmarks RF vs. ResNet/EfficientNet/ConvNeXt on Sentinel-2 depth extraction, training on Pratas Island and testing on Great Barrier Reef. DL generalizes better cross-region; multi-temporal fusion improves accuracy.
   - Why it matters: Practical guideline for cross-site Sentinel-2 coastal mapping under label scarcity.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
3. GAMBLe: Analytical Framework for AI-Driven Research Systems `arXiv:2606.02863`
   - Decomposes AI research pipelines into generator, assessor, discovery mechanism, and budget; validated over 760+ runs on NP-hard problems. Achieves 13–67% performance gains via optimal component selection.
   - Why it matters: First rigorous benchmarking framework for evaluating and designing AI scientific discovery systems.

4. Self-Revising Discovery Systems: A Categorical Framework for Agentic AI `arXiv:2606.01444`
   - Formalizes AI agents that revise their own conceptual frameworks during discovery using category theory, with typed skills and statistical validation gates. Demonstrated on protein mechanics and KG-based reasoning.
   - Why it matters: Gives AI science agents formal grounding for open-ended hypothesis revision—beyond fixed-task automation.

5. LEAP: Agentic LLMs for Formal Theorem Proving in Lean `arXiv:2606.03303`
   - Agentic system decomposes proofs into subgoals and iterates with Lean compiler feedback; solved all 12 Putnam 2025 problems and raised Lean-IMO-Bench solve rate from <10% to 70%.
   - Why it matters: Compiler-grounded agentic decomposition reaches competition-level formal mathematical reasoning.

6. CP-Agent: Multimodal Reasoning for Cellular Morphological Profiling `arXiv:2606.03435`
   - Agentic multimodal LLM integrates drug-perturbed cell images with metadata and tool use to generate mechanistic interpretations. Automates high-content imaging analysis for drug discovery.
   - Why it matters: Direct AI-for-science deployment—a replicable template for domain-specific agentic discovery agents.
