📡 [2026-06-11] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. CSI-Net: Content-Induced Spatial-Spectral Aggregation Network for Change Detection `arXiv:2606.10328`
   - Combines graph-convolution spatial reasoning with spectral difference modules, validated on LEVIR-CD, WHU-CD, and CLCD datasets.
   - Why it matters: Joint spatial-spectral fusion reduces false positives from unaltered regions in multitemporal satellite imagery.

2. Building Change Detection in Earthquake: Multi-Scale Interaction Network and TUE-CD Dataset `arXiv:2606.10329`
   - Introduces Turkey Earthquake CD dataset and MSI-Net with multi-scale offset calibration to handle misregistered short-interval post-disaster imagery.
   - Why it matters: First dedicated dataset + model for rapid post-earthquake building damage assessment via satellite CD.

3. Geometric Coastline Localization using Vision-Language Models `arXiv:2606.10468`
   - CoastlineVLM-7B predicts coastlines as polylines (not segmentation masks), cutting Hausdorff distance from 37.74m to 31.84m on NZ coastal imagery.
   - Why it matters: Geometry-first VLM approach reframes EO boundary detection beyond pixel-level classification.

4. Multi-Angular Reflectance Anisotropy Observed from UAV Multispectral Imagery `arXiv:2606.10350`
   - SfM-based workflow reveals up to 137% variability in IR-band reflectance across viewing angles in drone multispectral surveys.
   - Why it matters: BRDF effects at UAV scale must be corrected for reliable biophysical parameter retrieval.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

5. AutoPDE: Reliable Agentic PDE Solving via Explicitly Represented Solver Strategies `arXiv:2606.10752`
   - Three-stage agent (analyze → strategy → tune) maintains solver strategy as an inspectable artifact, achieving 54.5% on benchmarks (+14.2pp vs prior best).
   - Why it matters: Explicit intermediate representations make AI scientific solvers verifiable and self-correcting.

6. InquiTree: Evaluating AI Agents in the Scientific Inquiry Loop `arXiv:2606.09550`
   - Formalizes scientific inquiry as interactive Research Trees; finds agents develop "cognitive tunneling" with degraded critical judgment over long horizons.
   - Why it matters: Exposes a fundamental failure mode — Erosion of Marginal Capabilities — in LLM agents applied to open-ended discovery.

7. Moonshine: Autonomous Mathematical Research Agent for Conjecture Generation `arXiv:2606.10806`
   - Autonomously proposes the Neural Jacobian Conjecture (NJC) from exploring Jacobian conjecture structure and partially proves the N=n+1 case.
   - Why it matters: First AI agent to formulate a mathematically novel, formally-verifiable conjecture through autonomous reasoning.
