🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. SatSplatDiff: Geometry-Preserving Generative Refinement for High-Fidelity Satellite Gaussian Splatting `arXiv:2606.27223`
   - Shadow-guided diffusion refinement combined with monocular depth supervision improves 3D satellite reconstruction by 18% in geometric accuracy and up to 5× in resolution on IARPA2016/DFC2019 datasets.
   - Addresses facade hallucination artifacts caused by limited top-down viewpoints in satellite Gaussian Splatting.
   - Why it matters: Enables high-fidelity 3D urban mapping from satellite imagery without ground-truth facade supervision.

2. Does Aurora Encode Atmospheric Structure? Latent Regime Analysis and Attribution `arXiv:2606.26361`
   - Spatial PCA and layer-wise relevance propagation reveal that Aurora's latent space is organized by seasonal cycles; masking weather-relevant regions degrades forecasts significantly more than random masking.
   - Demonstrates that the model learns vertical atmospheric structure and meteorological patterns without explicit guidance.
   - Why it matters: First mechanistic interpretability study of a major earth-system foundation model — critical for climate extrapolation and community trust.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

3. Scientific Discovery as Meta-Optimization: A Combinatorial Optimization Case Study `arXiv:2606.26728`
   - Proposes jointly optimizing both the evaluation objective and the solution via LLM-generated objectives aggregated through correlation-weighted consensus voting.
   - Achieves 67× speedup and scaling improvement from N^2.51 to N^1.33 on 3-SAT problem instances.
   - Why it matters: Meta-optimizing the criterion itself — not just the answer — is a general and scalable path to automated algorithm discovery.

4. SKILL-DISCO: Distilling and Compiling Agent Traces into Reusable Procedural Skills `arXiv:2606.26669`
   - Extracts reusable parameterized finite-state-machine subgraphs from successful agent trajectories and compiles them into callable, verifiable procedural skills.
   - Improves success rates and reduces execution steps on ALFWorld and WebArena benchmarks across model scales.
   - Why it matters: Agents accumulate transferable task knowledge over time rather than reasoning from scratch on every run.

5. JERP: Joint Learning of Experiential Rules and Policies for LLM Agents `arXiv:2606.27136`
   - Maintains a dynamic rule pool retrieved at decision time, co-trained with policy gradient updates so rules stay synchronized with the evolving model.
   - Shows consistent performance gains on AlfWorld and WebShop interactive task environments.
   - Why it matters: Unifies symbolic interpretable rules with flexible learned policies in a single training loop for robust LLM agents.

6. When Does Combining Language Models Help? A Co-Failure Ceiling Across 67 Frontier Models `arXiv:2606.27288`
   - Shows that all ensemble methods (routing, voting, mixture-of-agents) are bounded by β — the co-failure rate where all models fail simultaneously; on open-ended math, observed β is ~2.5× what standard metrics predict.
   - Combining models rarely beats the single best model without reliable per-query routing signals.
   - Why it matters: Sets a hard empirical ceiling on multi-model ensembles and redirects attention to failure-diversity as the key metric for scaling AI systems.
