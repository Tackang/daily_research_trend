🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ Clustering Guided Domain-Specific Pretrained Foundation Model for VHR Arctic Remote Sensing `arXiv:2605.30467`
   - Builds an Arctic-focused RSFM via diversity-aware affinity-propagation clustering of 267TB VHSR imagery (~3M chips) and MAE+ViT self-supervised pretraining.
   - Why it matters: Validates that domain-specific data curation outperforms generic pretraining for specialized satellite imagery—directly applicable to Landsat foundation model design.

2. HADT: Heterogeneous Multi-Agent Differential Transformer for Autonomous EO Satellite Cluster `arXiv:2605.31023`
   - Proposes a multi-agent system enabling real-time autonomous resource scheduling across heterogeneous optical and SAR satellite constellations without ground-control intervention.
   - Why it matters: First multi-agent framework for heterogeneous EO satellite constellation management, pointing toward fully autonomous Earth observation infrastructure.

3. SCALMU: Synthetically-trained HSI-MSI Fusion via Adaptive Learned Multiplicative Updates `arXiv:2605.30973`
   - Unrolled NMF-based neural network for hyperspectral-multispectral image fusion, trained entirely on synthetic dead-leaves data without real paired samples.
   - Why it matters: Eliminates expensive labeled training data requirements for HS-MS fusion—applicable to Sentinel-2/DESIS or Landsat-8/PRISMA fusion workflows.

4. SubsurfaceGen: Procedural Generation of Field-Scale Earth Models and Seismic Data `arXiv:2605.30550`
   - GPU-accelerated synthesis of 3D subsurface velocity models and seismic wavefields to enable ML-based full waveform inversion at scale.
   - Why it matters: Large-scale synthetic earth model generation unlocks data-hungry deep learning for geophysical subsurface imaging.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

5. AutoSci: A Memory-Centric Agentic System for the Full Scientific Research Lifecycle `arXiv:2605.31468`
   - Integrates memory-augmented LLM agents across literature review, hypothesis generation, experiment orchestration, and manuscript writing in a unified pipeline.
   - Why it matters: One of the first end-to-end agentic systems covering the entire research lifecycle—benchmarks autonomous performance against human researcher workflows.

6. DisasterLex: Expert Concept-to-Schema Knowledge Graph for Geospatial Reasoning in Disaster Analytics `arXiv:2605.30541`
   - Knowledge-graph-mediated text-to-SQL framework for disaster databases, achieving 1.4–2.75× improvement over state-of-the-art baselines on geospatial schema reasoning.
   - Why it matters: Bridges natural-language queries and complex spatial databases—enabling rapid, structured geospatial analytics for disaster response.

💡 One-line comment: Domain-specific pretraining (polar imagery, subsurface earth) and end-to-end agentic science are today's twin themes—specialization and automation accelerating together.
