🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Advanced Flood Prediction with Physics-Guided Deep Learning `arXiv:2606.06524`
   - Fuses Sentinel-1 SAR, Sentinel-2 optical, and DEM in a UNet+FNO hybrid with shallow-water physics constraints; achieves IoU 0.82 / F1 0.90 for flood extent mapping.
   - Physics-guided loss simultaneously estimates water depth (RMSE 0.21 m) and flow velocity, going beyond extent to full hydrodynamic outputs.
   - Why it matters: Multi-modal physics-constrained DL sets a new benchmark for operationally reliable, physically coherent flood mapping.

2. Beyond Backscatter: InSAR Coherence from Detected SAR Images `arXiv:2606.07374`
   - ResU-Net trained on Sentinel-1 predicts InSAR coherence directly from amplitude-only images, eliminating the need for precise coregistration.
   - Generalizes across diverse geographies and temporal baselines; outputs are compatible with GEE analysis-ready data pipelines.
   - Why it matters: Unlocks large-scale coherence-based change monitoring without requiring full SLC image stacks.

3. RPC-GS: Gaussian Splatting with Native RPC Rendering for Satellite Imagery `arXiv:2606.06690`
   - First Gaussian Splatting framework natively supporting Rational Polynomial Camera (RPC) models for pushbroom satellite sensors.
   - Reduces mean altitude error by 29.6% and 63.8% over perspective and affine approximation baselines on DFC2019.
   - Why it matters: Accurate satellite 3D reconstruction that respects real-world sensor geometry without camera model approximation.
