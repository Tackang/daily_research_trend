📡 [2026-06-21] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. SARLO-80: Worldwide Slant SAR Language Optic Dataset 80cm `arXiv:2606.20523`
   - 119K SAR-optical-text triplets at 80cm VHR across 257 global sites; three caption lengths (SHORT/MID/LONG) per scene.
   - Why it matters: First large-scale paired VHR SAR + optical + language dataset enabling vision-language model training for RS.

2. Evaluating and Enhancing Negation Comprehension in Remote Sensing MLLMs `arXiv:2606.20177`
   - RS-Neg benchmark + NeFo test-time method reveals SOTA RS-MLLMs hallucinate and degrade severely on negation queries; ECCV 2026.
   - Why it matters: Exposes a critical reasoning gap in RS foundation models that goes beyond perception accuracy.

3. GeoDisaster: Benchmarking Orchestrated Agents for Operational Disaster Geo-Intelligence `arXiv:2606.17246`
   - 2,921 instances across 43 question types; 18-tool multi-agent system for flood monitoring, building damage, and deforestation tracking.
   - Why it matters: First benchmark unifying satellite geospatial data with multi-agent orchestration for disaster response workflows.

4. Training-Free Open-Vocabulary Visual Grounding for Remote Sensing Images and Videos `arXiv:2606.16124`
   - RSVG-ZeroOV leverages frozen VLMs + diffusion models for zero-shot RS visual grounding without task-specific training.
   - Why it matters: Removes the annotation barrier for novel objects in aerial/satellite imagery using off-the-shelf foundation models.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
5. Towards End-to-End Automation of AI Research `arXiv:2606.15497`
   - The AI Scientist autonomously generates ideas, runs experiments, writes manuscripts, and passes peer review end-to-end; published in Nature (2026).
   - Why it matters: First AI system completing the full research lifecycle — a milestone for automated scientific discovery at scale.

6. Advances in Scientific Machine Learning for Coupled Fluid Flow and Transport `arXiv:2606.19562`
   - Survey of PINNs, DMD, and β-VAEs for incompressible Navier-Stokes + scalar transport equations with HPC integration.
   - Why it matters: Comprehensive SciML roadmap for physics-constrained surrogate modeling of complex Earth system dynamics.
