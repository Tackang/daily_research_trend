📚 [2026-06-01] Weekly Journal Digest (past 7 days Early Access)

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation

1. ⭐ Spectral and Spatial Harmonization of Landsat ETM+, OLI, and Sentinel-2 MSI Using Time-Series Harmonic Analysis and Blur Kernel Estimation
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment, 339, 115384 (June 2026)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115384
   - Proposes a unified two-stage pipeline combining harmonic analysis to remove spectral offsets across Landsat ETM+/OLI/Sentinel-2 MSI, and blur kernel estimation to reconcile spatial resolution mismatches; validated over diverse biomes globally.
   - Why it matters: Cleaner cross-sensor harmonization directly improves the HLS training datasets that underpin Landsat foundation model pre-training.

2. ⭐ Harmonized Tasseled Cap Transformation Coefficients for Landsat 8 and 9 OLI Sensors Using Surface Reflectance from Near-Coincident Underfly Observations
   - Journal: Science of Remote Sensing, 13, 100353 (June 2026)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.srs.2025.100353
   - Derives empirically consistent TCT coefficients for brightness, greenness, and wetness using scenes co-observed by Landsat 8 and 9 within 30 minutes, substantially reducing inter-mission discontinuities in long-term monitoring.
   - Why it matters: Mission-consistent spectral indices are foundational for stable temporal feature representations in Landsat FM pre-training and ecosystem monitoring downstream tasks.

3. ⭐ Generating an Annual 30 m Rice Cover Product for Monsoon Asia (2018–2023) Using Harmonized Landsat and Sentinel-2 Data and the NASA-IBM Geospatial Foundation Model
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment, 335, 115256 (March 2026)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115256
   - Fine-tunes the NASA-IBM Prithvi foundation model on HLS time-series data; auto-labeling from existing regional products eliminates manual annotation and achieves F1 > 0.85 across six subregions of Monsoon Asia at 30 m annual resolution.
   - Why it matters: First peer-reviewed demonstration that an HLS-pretrained foundation model transfers to continental-scale crop mapping with minimal labels — a direct blueprint for Landsat FM deployment.

4. ⭐ A Framework for Integrating Spatiotemporal Deep Learning Methods with Landsat for Annual Land Cover and Impervious Surface Mapping
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment, 338, 115347 (March 2026)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115347
   - Introduces LCAMS, a two-stage (per-year spatial + multi-year temporal) deep learning architecture that powers the new Annual NLCD product; loosely coupled design allows independent model updates across CONUS using Landsat-8/9 time series.
   - Why it matters: Establishes the operational benchmark for Landsat-based annual LULC products — the primary downstream target for Landsat foundation model fine-tuning evaluation.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models

5. ⭐ AgriFM: A Multi-Source Temporal Remote Sensing Foundation Model for Agriculture Mapping
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment, 334, 115234 (March 2026)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115234
   - Pre-trains a Video Swin Transformer on 25M+ images from MODIS, Landsat-8/9, and Sentinel-2 with synchronized spatiotemporal downsampling, handling variable-length multi-sensor time series; achieves SOTA on crop mapping, field boundary delineation, and agricultural change detection.
   - Why it matters: First journal-published multi-source (Landsat + Sentinel + MODIS) temporal foundation model — expands the Landsat FM paradigm beyond single-sensor pretraining to heterogeneous fusion at global scale.

📊 This week's journal trend: RSE is consolidating the Landsat FM ecosystem — from upstream harmonization (HLS calibration, TCT coefficients) through FM fine-tuning (Prithvi for rice, AgriFM for crops) to operational LULC products (LCAMS/Annual NLCD) — signaling the field is shifting from pretraining novelty to deployment infrastructure.
