🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. FairRSFM: A Biome-Aware Benchmark and Debiasing Framework for Remote Sensing Foundation Models `arXiv:2610.05790`
   - Evaluates remote sensing foundation models across ecological regions and finds performance disparities.
   - Tests debiasing methods for more equitable satellite imagery analysis.
   - Why it matters: Biome-level bias is a check any Landsat foundation model should pass before global use.

2. T-JEPA: A Temporal Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture for Learning Better Remote Sensing Representations `arXiv:2610.05731`
   - Learns satellite image representations by predicting latent transitions across time.
   - Uses temporal structure in Earth observation sequences instead of single-date reconstruction.
   - Why it matters: A temporal pretext task suits Landsat's multi-decade archive.

3. Extending Dynamic World Surface Water Mapping to Sentinel-1 with AlphaEarth Embeddings `arXiv:2610.06704`
   - Extends optical-based water mapping to radar using weak supervision and spatial context embeddings.
   - Improves surface water detection performance.
   - Why it matters: Shows pretrained embeddings can carry optical labels over to SAR.

4. A Framework for Automated Multi-Source Satellite Data Analytics and LLM-Based Report Generation `arXiv:2610.05625`
   - Automates thermal feature extraction from Landsat data.
   - Uses language models to write interpretive temperature-mapping reports.
   - Why it matters: Links Landsat products to LLM-based reporting.

5. A Two-Stage Cascade for Near-Real-Time Forest Anomaly Detection from Sentinel-1 SAR Time Series `arXiv:2610.02763`
   - Combines statistical change detection with autoencoder confirmation on SAR time series.
   - Produces operational alerts for forest loss such as illegal logging.
   - Why it matters: Cloud-independent forest monitoring is useful for LULC change and carbon accounting.
