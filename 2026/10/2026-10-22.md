📡 [2026-10-22] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Channel Selective Interaction Fusion Network for Optical-SAR Object Detection `arXiv:2610.27757`
   - Selectively fuses optical and SAR channels to exploit inter-channel modality differences.
   - Reports roughly 3–4% gains over prior methods on remote sensing detection.
   - Why it matters: Channel-level fusion could carry over to multi-sensor pretraining.
2. Generic On-Board AI Processing Chain for Earth Observation `arXiv:2610.26340`
   - End-to-end pipeline from application development to embedded deployment, with orchestration via unikernels.
   - Demonstrated with quantized wildfire segmentation on satellite hardware.
   - Why it matters: Moves EO models toward onboard inference.
3. Uncertainty Bounds in Spectral Shallow Water Bathymetry `arXiv:2610.26352`
   - Compares profile-likelihood tests for satellite-derived water depth.
   - Finds the best test depends on sensor type and water conditions.
   - Why it matters: Gives principled error bars for derived EO products.
4. Long-wave Infrared Polarimetric Image Fusion for Vehicle Detection `arXiv:2610.27018`
   - Mamba-based fusion of LWIR intensity and polarization imagery.
   - Releases AIR-LWIR-Pol, 1,901 registered image pairs.
   - Why it matters: A new multimodal dataset, though airborne rather than satellite.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
5. Sage-A1.5: From Long-Horizon Execution to Scientific Exploration `arXiv:2610.28578`
   - A 35B mixture-of-experts agent adding subagent orchestration and self-verification.
   - Targets open-ended scientific exploration.
   - Why it matters: Shows where science-oriented agent models are heading.
6. OptScientist: Automated Discovery of Optimization Laws `arXiv:2610.28562`
   - An LLM-agent framework that infers compact symbolic laws from data.
   - Recovers ground-truth laws for over 80% of a 99-problem physics benchmark.
   - Why it matters: Concrete evidence that agents can do symbolic discovery.
7. MELD: Recursive Self-Improvement for Multi-Agent Scientific Discovery `arXiv:2610.24454`
   - Jointly adapts a team Coordinator and individual Scientist policies.
   - Best result on 20 of 22 MLGym and MLE-bench tasks.
   - Why it matters: Team-level coordination is becoming a trainable component.
8. Bayesian Model Discovery with Language Models `arXiv:2610.25747`
   - Pairs LLM-proposed model families with Bayesian fitting and information-gain experiment design.
   - Feeds the resulting beliefs back to the LLM.
   - Why it matters: Closes the hypothesis–experiment loop in a principled way.
