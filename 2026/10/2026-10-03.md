🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. FedMAD: Modulation-Aware Directional Aggregation for Federated Learning in Remote Sensing Image Classification `arXiv:2610.00693`
   - Personalized federated learning that separates shared representation parameters from client-specific modulation modules. Aggregation weights clients by update alignment, and it is tested on BigEarthNet-S2 and EuroSAT.
   - Why it matters: Handles heterogeneous, decentralized EO data, which is relevant to training across sensors and regions.

2. Decoding the Disaster: Multi-Task Geospatial Reasoning with VLMs and Crowdsourced Imagery for Disaster Mapping `arXiv:2610.00302`
   - GRDisaster is a VLM framework for geolocalization and damage assessment. It uses 26,340 cross-view crowdsourced images from disasters in 2018–2024.
   - Why it matters: It turns unstructured ground photos into geospatial intelligence that can be cross-checked against remote sensing. (Submitted Sept 28, shown in the Oct 2 listing.)

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
3. K-Dense BYOK: An Open-Source AI Research Assistant `arXiv:2610.00074`
   - A local research assistant that keeps hash-chained lab notebooks. They record the agent's actual actions rather than the model's claims.
   - Why it matters: Auditable provenance addresses reproducibility in agent-driven science.

4. Scientific Agents: Evaluating Profession-Specific System Prompts `arXiv:2610.00084`
   - Detailed profession-specific prompts raise token cost 2.2–4.5× on scientific tasks. Accuracy does not improve consistently.
   - Why it matters: Elaborate persona prompting may waste compute in science agents.

5. Rules to Tools: Executable Checks for LLM Agents in Scientific Computing `arXiv:2610.00313`
   - Executable constraint checks help agents repair scientific code more reliably than text descriptions of the rules.
   - Why it matters: Verifiable tools beat prose instructions for reliable scientific pipelines.

6. CompMat-Bench: Benchmarking AI Agents for Computational Materials Science `arXiv:2610.00636`
   - 94 tasks drawn from published materials research. Agents must prepare inputs and analyze outputs for expensive simulations.
   - Why it matters: It measures realistic end-to-end scientific workflows rather than toy problems.

7. Heavy-Tailed Memory Traces in Long-Horizon Language Agents `arXiv:2610.00010`
   - Agent memory concentrates on core information while prediction errors build up in rare states. The authors propose a rank-based memory controller to save tokens.
   - Why it matters: Better memory management supports long-running autonomous research agents.
