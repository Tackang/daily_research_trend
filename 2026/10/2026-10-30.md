📡 [2026-10-30] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Efficient Remote Sensing Image-Text Retrieval (fast-then-fine) `arXiv:2610.36756`
   - Interaction-free multi-granular recall, then text-guided reranking.
   - Why it matters: Faster retrieval over large satellite archives without losing accuracy.

2. MDF-Mamba for MODIS Spectral-Temporal Unmixing `arXiv:2610.36750`
   - A Mamba model that handles endmember variability. Estimates abundances from MODIS time series over Alberta and Saskatchewan.
   - Why it matters: Handling endmember variability is relevant to sub-pixel LULC mapping with moderate-resolution sensors, including Landsat.

3. Ground-to-Satellite Knowledge Distillation for Crop Classification `arXiv:2610.37021`
   - Ground photos supervise classifiers built on pretrained AlphaEarth and TESSERA embeddings. The photos are not needed at inference.
   - Why it matters: Cheap ground labels can improve EO foundation-model embeddings for downstream tasks.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
4. Why and How to Do Science When AI Is Better at It `arXiv:2610.36116`
   - Proposes a test for which research tasks can be delegated to AI. The test is based on keeping human understanding and direction of science.
   - Why it matters: Frames how far scientific discovery automation should go.

5. The Trinity: Recursive Self-Improvement for Multimodal Agents `arXiv:2610.36077`
   - Creator, Explorer, and Evolver roles build environments and evolve reusable capability modules.
   - Why it matters: A concrete multi-agent design for self-improving agents.

6. CogArena `arXiv:2610.36484`
   - A benchmark where multimodal agents run ten behavioral-science experiments in a browser. Finishing an experiment and reproducing its expected effects turn out to be different things.
   - Why it matters: Shows that finishing a task is not the same as getting a valid scientific result.

7. Self-Driving Microfluidics Lab: When Can a Design Surrogate Be Trusted? `arXiv:2610.36395`
   - An offline closed-loop framework for choosing which measurements to take next in an autonomous lab.
   - Why it matters: Addresses surrogate reliability in autonomous experimentation.

8. Latent ODEs for Discovering Lie Symmetries `arXiv:2610.35967`
   - Learns symmetries and conserved quantities of dynamical systems from time-series data, such as video.
   - Why it matters: Automated discovery of physical structure directly from data.

💡 One-line comment: Today's EO papers are about reusing pretrained embeddings and doing sub-pixel mapping. The AI-for-science papers are about when autonomous agents can be trusted.
