📡 [2026-10-21] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Spatio-Temporal Detection of Photovoltaic Systems on Industrial Building Rooftops Using Satellite Imagery and ML `arXiv:2610.23970`
   - Detects rooftop solar PV on industrial buildings from open Sentinel-2 imagery with a Random Forest.
   - Tracks installations over time.
   - Why it matters: Shows a cheap, open-data pipeline for monitoring energy infrastructure at scale.

2. Super-resolution as Spatial Downscaling: Coherence, Structure and Task Utility in Earth Observation `arXiv:2610.23972`
   - Evaluates satellite super-resolution on EO tiles using land-use classification and building-change detection.
   - Judges outputs by downstream task utility, not only image fidelity.
   - Why it matters: Visual sharpness may not mean better analysis, which matters for harmonizing Landsat/Sentinel resolutions.

3. LC-TSRNet: A Land-Cover-Guided Two-Stage Network for Annual MODIS Surface Reflectance Time Series Reconstruction `arXiv:2610.25120`
   - Reconstructs annual MODIS surface reflectance time series in two stages, guided by land-cover information.
   - Why it matters: Land-cover priors for gap-filling are relevant to LULC change work and to Landsat time-series pretraining.

4. SPIRIT: Sparse Physics-Informed Inverse Rendering for SAR Imaging `arXiv:2610.23914`
   - Reconstructs SAR imagery with a sparse, physics-informed inverse-rendering approach.
   - Why it matters: Physics-based priors may help cloud-independent EO where optical data is limited.

5. OrTeN: Neural Inverse Graphics for Joint Terrain and Line-scan Camera Trajectory Reconstruction `arXiv:2610.24075`
   - Jointly recovers terrain maps and camera trajectories from line-scan satellite imagery, for planetary digital terrain maps.
   - Why it matters: Neural inverse graphics is spreading from natural scenes into satellite photogrammetry.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
6. Physics-Aware Self-Supervised Pretraining on Multi-Domain Raman Spectra `arXiv:2610.23955`
   - Masked-autoencoder pretraining for Raman spectroscopy with physics-based peak objectives.
   - Why it matters: Physics-informed objectives in scientific foundation models parallel spectral-aware pretraining for multispectral EO.

7. CME-SpectrumBench: Can LLMs Analyze Condensed Matter Spectral Data? `arXiv:2610.24304`
   - Benchmark testing LLMs on interpreting condensed-matter spectral data.
   - Why it matters: Quantifies how far general LLMs are from useful scientific-data analysis.

8. Bootstrap Stochastic Interpolant Tree for Inference-time Search Under Sparse Reward `arXiv:2610.24185`
   - Inference-time search method evaluated on rare-species discovery and target-aligned molecule generation.
   - Why it matters: Test-time search is becoming a lever for discovery tasks where rewards are rare.
