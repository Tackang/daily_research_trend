🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. State-Space Unlearning for Non-Stationary Bias in Land Surface Forecasting `arXiv:2610.02248`
   - SSU-LSF removes learned bias from non-stationary events (irrigation changes, sensor recalibration) in Mamba-based land-surface forecasters. It cuts confounding by 0.77–0.86 on CropHarvest, NDVI-LST and ERA5 at 8.4× lower cost than retraining.
   - Why it matters: Long satellite time series contain sensor and management shifts, so this is a cheap way to keep EO forecasters from carrying them forward.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
2. The AI Theorist reveals excitonic structure in α-RuCl₃ `arXiv:2610.02417`
   - An autonomous AI system proposes physical models from unpublished optical spectra and checks them with first-principles calculations. It identified distinct excitonic states in a candidate Kitaev quantum spin liquid material.
   - Why it matters: It is a concrete case of an agent going from raw data to a physical model without a human in the loop.

3. Learning What to Investigate Next: Meta-Reasoning for Long-Horizon Research Agents (MIRA) `arXiv:2610.02525`
   - A meta-reasoner decides the next investigation or when to stop, and an inner executor carries it out. Compute allocation improves in theorem proving and neural architecture search.
   - Why it matters: Deciding what to try next is the bottleneck for automated research, and this makes that decision a learnable unit.

4. Counterfactual Predictions in Scientific Emulators Without Controlled Experiments (ReRoute) `arXiv:2610.02252`
   - ReRoute fixes an input of a pretrained backbone and reintroduces its variation through a known mechanistic pathway. Error drops 18.2–31.8% under severe CO₂ distribution shift in climate emulation.
   - Why it matters: It lets emulators answer "what-if" questions without costly new simulation runs.
