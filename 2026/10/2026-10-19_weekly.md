🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Integrating Landsat and ECOSTRESS for field-scale evapotranspiration mapping
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115738
   - Combines Landsat with ECOSTRESS thermal data to map evapotranspiration at field scale. (Summary from title; abstract not reviewed.)

2. Global mapping of center-pivot irrigated cropland with knowledge-guided data augmentation
   - Journal: Remote Sensing of Environment (Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.rse.2026.115736
   - Global-scale irrigated cropland mapping that uses knowledge-guided data augmentation. (Summary from title; abstract not reviewed.)
   - Why it matters: Shows how domain knowledge can stand in for scarce labels in global LULC mapping.

3. DynamicVis: Dynamic visual perception for efficient remote sensing foundation models
   - Journal: ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (Online)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2026.09.042
   - Targets efficient visual perception for remote sensing foundation models. (Summary from title; abstract not reviewed.)
   - Why it matters: Efficiency is a practical bottleneck for scaling RS foundation models to long time series like Landsat's.

4. GeoPhysAdapter: Scale-matched geophysical adaptation for cross-domain landslide mapping with vision foundation models
   - Journal: ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (Online)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2026.09.035
   - Adapts vision foundation models to landslide mapping across domains, with scale matching. (Summary from title; abstract not reviewed.)
   - Why it matters: A template for downstream adaptation of foundation models to new EO tasks.

5. Diagnosing drought–flood vulnerability of rice systems across growth stages using Sentinel-1/2 and CHIRPS time series
   - Journal: ISPRS Journal of Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (Online)
   - DOI: 10.1016/j.isprsjprs.2026.10.010
   - Uses Sentinel-1/2 and CHIRPS time series to assess rice vulnerability to drought and flood by growth stage. (Summary from title; abstract not reviewed.)
   - Why it matters: Stage-specific analysis shows what dense multi-sensor time series can give for agriculture.

6. A Coupled Spatiotemporal Fusion Method for Multisensor Reconstruction
   - Journal: IEEE TGRS (Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1109/tgrs.2026.3743198
   - Proposes a coupled spatiotemporal fusion method for multisensor reconstruction. (Summary from title; abstract not reviewed.)
   - Why it matters: Spatiotemporal fusion (e.g., Landsat with coarser sensors) is a common preprocessing step for Landsat-scale models.

7. High Accuracy Estimation of Soil Organic Carbon Density in China Driven by Ensemble Learning and HASM
   - Journal: IEEE TGRS (Early Access)
   - DOI: 10.1109/tgrs.2026.3742793
