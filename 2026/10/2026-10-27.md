📡 [2026-10-27] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ Reuse, Don't Retrain: Efficient, Modality-Versatile Geospatial Foundation Models through Feature Distillation `arXiv:2610.32566`
   - Distills existing geospatial foundation models into smaller ViTs.
   - Merges multispectral and high-res RGB experts into one backbone.
   - Why it matters: A cheap way to build compact, multi-sensor backbones, useful for Landsat foundation model work.

2. ⭐ OlmoEarth v1.3: Embeddings `arXiv:2610.32294`
   - Compresses multimodal, multitemporal EO data into 64/128-d vectors.
   - Releases global annual 10 m embeddings.
   - Why it matters: Ready-made embeddings are a baseline to compare Landsat-based representations against.

3. ⭐ Earth embeddings are decomposable records of the land surface `arXiv:2610.32617`
   - Decomposes two popular annual Earth embedding products.
   - Finds they encode fire, forest clearing and crop type, checked against reference data.
   - Why it matters: Shows what embeddings actually capture, which guides LULC and biomass downstream tasks.

4. LoCA: Enabling Multi-Modality in Geospatial Location Encoders `arXiv:2610.32894`
   - Trains Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 encoders jointly, then adds location and tabular adapters.
   - Evaluated on 17 geospatial tasks.
   - Why it matters: Modular multi-sensor fusion without full retraining.

5. DS-BiCA-Net: Pair-Preserving Cross-Temporal Modeling for Remote Sensing Change Captioning `arXiv:2610.32200`
   - Uses separate per-image streams, a state-space module, and bidirectional cross-attention.
   - Describes changes between bi-temporal images.
   - Why it matters: Moves change detection toward interpretable text output.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
6. Can Test-Time Memory Evolution Outperform Test-Time Training for Scientific Discovery? `arXiv:2610.32046`
   - Updates external memory instead of model weights on math discovery tasks.
   - Matches or beats test-time training at lower cost.
   - Why it matters: Cheaper adaptation for discovery agents.

7. Do AI-for-Science Agents Preserve Model Safety? `arXiv:2610.32045`
   - Wrapping an LLM in a science agent harness can weaken its safety behavior.
   - Why it matters: Science agents need their own safety evaluation.

8. PersonaScienceBench: Evaluating Scientific Agents with Diverse Simulated Researchers `arXiv:2610.32106`
   - Varies simulated researcher personas to test collaboration, not just task completion.
   - Why it matters: Measures how well science agents work with different users.

9. Phronix: The Capability-Centric Learning Paradigm for Agents `arXiv:2610.31789`
   - Scientific-computing agents build and reuse validated capabilities from their own runs.
   - Why it matters: Less rediscovery and lower token cost.

💡 One-line comment: Earth embeddings and distilled geospatial foundation models are the theme today, and science-agent work is turning to memory and safety.
