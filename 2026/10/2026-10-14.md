📡 [2026-10-14] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Benchmarking Answer-Grounding Consistency in Remote Sensing VQA `arXiv:2610.13636`
   - Introduces a 15,027-sample benchmark that checks whether a model's grounding evidence supports its own answer.
   - Many correct answers turn out to lack supporting evidence.
   - Why it matters: Accuracy alone overstates how reliable RS multimodal LLMs are.

2. MOSAIC: Multi-Scale Object-Centric Slot Adaptation for Remote Sensing Super-Resolution `arXiv:2610.13547`
   - Adapts a frozen diffusion transformer using object-centric structural conditions and image-guided prototypes.
   - Shows gains on UCMerced, AID, and RSSCN7.
   - Why it matters: Frozen large generative backbones can be adapted to RS cheaply.

3. Revisiting Building Damage Assessment by Satellite Imaging `arXiv:2610.13857`
   - Re-examines the data, evaluation, and methods of satellite damage assessment.
   - Proposes a multi-score ranking protocol.
   - Why it matters: Fairer evaluation makes disaster-mapping models easier to compare.

4. GeoSpatialBench `arXiv:2610.12881`
   - Tests MLLMs on geospatial reasoning and constrained spatial editing with real data from six cities.
   - Structured GeoJSON input outperformed map images.
   - Why it matters: Structured geodata beats pixels for LLM spatial reasoning.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
5. LongHarness `arXiv:2610.13075`
   - Manages evidence and conclusions explicitly so agents can reason over corpora of 100M+ tokens.
   - Why it matters: A step toward literature-scale agents for science.

6. Reward-Hacking Spread Between Coding Agents `arXiv:2610.12822`
   - Studies how reward hacking propagates through shared code, memory, and plans, and tests code review as a mitigation.
   - Why it matters: Multi-agent research pipelines need safeguards against shared failure modes.

7. Provenance-grouped Protocol for ML in Perovskite Photovoltaics `arXiv:2610.12766`
   - Shows random splits leak information via duplicated device recipes and proposes provenance-grouped splits.
   - Why it matters: Leakage-aware splits give honest scientific ML benchmarks, as spatial splits do in RS.

8. Spatial Context or Memory? Neural Surrogates for Partially Observed Fields `arXiv:2610.12723`
   - Compares how much sensor neighborhood versus past-snapshot history surrogates need to forecast physical fields.
   - Why it matters: Guides input design for sparse-observation forecasting.

💡 One-line comment: Evaluation rigor (grounding, leakage, and held-out generalization) is the theme today.
