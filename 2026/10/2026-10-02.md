🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. APERTURE: Training-Free Multiscale Concept Bottlenecks for Remote Sensing `arXiv:2609.38603`
   - Training-free concept bottleneck that pairs multiscale concept detection with multimodal LLMs for interpretable EO models. It beats the best training-free baselines by more than 10 macro-F1 points on the new SiFC dataset.
   - Why it matters: Interpretable, annotation-free concept analysis could be used to probe what EO foundation models, including Landsat-based ones, have actually learned.

2. Raw Imagery Impacting Your AI: Should You Care? `arXiv:2609.38265`
   - Tests YOLOv5s, YOLOX-S and NanoDet on high-resolution satellite imagery under varying noise, blur and resolution. Severe blur combined with noise causes the largest detection losses.
   - Why it matters: It gives practical guidance on sensor and AI trade-offs for onboard satellite processing.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
3. AIM: Agentic Idea Management for Automated Research `arXiv:2609.38445`
   - An autonomous framework that organizes discovered ideas and picks promising research directions using an agentic surrogate and acquisition mechanism. It matches baselines up to 3.1x faster.
   - Why it matters: It moves automated scientific discovery from running single experiments to managing a portfolio of ideas.

4. PathAnchor: Path-Structured Evidence for Scientific Agents `arXiv:2609.38766`
   - Organizes evidence into structured paths instead of independent passages. Accuracy reaches 82.6% and source recall rises from 61.3% to 82.9%.
   - Why it matters: Better-grounded citations make scientific agents more trustworthy.

5. CARAT: Do Materials LLMs Reason or Recite? `arXiv:2609.38340`
   - A benchmark with matched views and structural controls to separate reasoning from memorization. Materials LLMs often quote structural relations without using them to solve problems.
   - Why it matters: It is a caution against over-reading benchmark scores for domain-specific science models.

6. AREX-2: Advancing Self-Improving Agents through Long-Horizon Reflective Tasks `arXiv:2609.38288`
   - Trains an agent on long-horizon improvement trajectories from ML and programming tasks. It scores 81.8 on MLE-bench Lite and transfers to BrowseComp (84.0) and GAIA (92.2).
   - Why it matters: Iterative self-improvement is a core capability for autonomous research agents.

7. Component-Aware Feedback for Self-Evolving Programs `arXiv:2609.38639`
   - Tracks which program components changed during evolutionary search and logs the metric differences. It reaches baseline quality with one-third of the search budget.
   - Why it matters: Credit assignment at the component level makes LLM-guided program discovery cheaper.
