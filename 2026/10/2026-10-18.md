📡 [2026-10-18] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ Spectral-Spatial Interpretation `arXiv:2610.21114`
   - Attribution method for multispectral images that scores each spatial position and channel jointly.
   - Evaluated on HLS Burn Scar (Harmonized Landsat-Sentinel) and EuroSAT.
   - Why it matters: Band-level explanations can be applied to Landsat foundation models.

2. A large-scale climate-aware satellite image dataset for domain adaptive land-cover semantic segmentation `arXiv:2610.21238`
   - CASID: 980 images (5000×5000 px) from 30 regions across 4 climate zones.
   - Benchmarks segmentation and unsupervised domain adaptation methods.
   - Why it matters: It tests LULC generalization across climates.

3. AI-Driven Disaster Response and Displacement Monitoring `arXiv:2610.21482`
   - Survey of AI for humanitarian work: satellite damage assessment, displacement tracking, deforestation monitoring.
   - Why it matters: It shows where EO applications are heading, but it is a survey, not new methods.

4. KNOU-Flood: Advancing global streamflow forecasting with lead-aware probabilistic machine learning `arXiv:2610.20640`
   - Extends an LSTM recipe with a longer input window, GraphCast plus ECMWF HRES forecasts, and a probabilistic ensemble.
   - Why it matters: It is a probabilistic global flood forecasting baseline.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
5. IneqAgent `arXiv:2610.20681`
   - Combines LLM reasoning with symbolic computation to prove inequalities in Lean 4.
   - Solves 175 of 186 benchmark problems and produces some new Lean-verified results.
   - Why it matters: It is a concrete case of agents producing verified discoveries.

6. Kolab `arXiv:2610.21109`
   - Dataset of 115+ hours of professionals collaborating with agents on long-horizon workflows.
   - Frontier models overestimate how well the collaboration went.
   - Why it matters: Agent evaluation should include human-in-the-loop work.

7. SkillNEO `arXiv:2610.20926`
   - Non-elitist evolutionary optimization of agent skills.
   - Raises one model's average reward on 86 SkillsBench tasks from 49.1% to 66.4%.
   - Why it matters: Skill libraries can be optimized automatically.

8. Probe-and-Resume `arXiv:2610.21088`
   - Calls a critic only when an agent claims it is done, probing a forked environment.
   - Adds 8.4 points on Terminal-Bench 2.1.
   - Why it matters: Verifying at completion time is a cheap way to make agents more reliable.

💡 One-line comment: EO work is moving toward interpretability and cross-climate generalization, while agent work is moving toward verification.
