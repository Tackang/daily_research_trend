📡 [2026-10-20] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ Deep Learning for Forest Plantation Mapping in Godavari Districts `arXiv:2610.22415`
   - Pixel-level neural net on cloud-free Harmonized Landsat Sentinel-2 (HLS) data maps small plantations and beats a random forest baseline.
   - Why it matters: HLS is a practical Landsat input for biomass and land-cover work.
2. MajorTOM Elliot Pretrain `arXiv:2610.18624`
   - Multi-sensor, multi-temporal tile dataset with ablations on which sensors and targets help pretraining.
   - Why it matters: Shows what to include when building a Landsat-scale foundation model corpus.
3. FloodVLM-Pix `arXiv:2610.23377`
   - Flood benchmark and model that describes severity and segments flood pixels.
   - Why it matters: Links language and pixel-level EO outputs.
4. Domain-specific Language Alignment of Multimodal Embeddings `arXiv:2610.23110`
   - Aligns embeddings with domain language, so Earth embedding predictions (e.g. species distribution) can be explained.
   - Why it matters: Makes Earth embeddings more interpretable.
5. Bayesian Spatially Varying Model for Urban Heat Islands `arXiv:2610.21980`
   - Models Landsat land surface temperature in Bologna with uncertainty and local warming/cooling trends.
   - Why it matters: A Landsat-based product that reports uncertainty.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
6. Agent-built Earth Observation Models via Harnesses and Verifiers `arXiv:2610.18845`
   - Coding agents build and recursively improve EO models given a task harness and verifier.
   - Why it matters: Agents may take over EO model development.
7. Trust before Autonomy in Agentic Remote Sensing `arXiv:2610.18789`
   - Argues agent autonomy should match demonstrated reliability, and supports only assistive roles for now.
   - Why it matters: A caution for deploying EO agents in crisis response.
8. Null Controls Are Necessary but Not Sufficient `arXiv:2610.22445`
   - Acceptance tests for automated discovery can accept empty candidates; accepted explanations need independently verified consequences.
   - Why it matters: Sets a stricter bar for validating AI-driven discovery.
9. At the Limits of Computability `arXiv:2610.22603`
   - Gemini-based agentic pipeline finds and Lean-verifies Collatz-like accelerations for BB(6) Busy Beaver machines.
   - Why it matters: A concrete example of verified AI-driven discovery.

💡 One-line comment: Agentic EO workflows and Earth embeddings are rising, but trust and verification are the open questions.
