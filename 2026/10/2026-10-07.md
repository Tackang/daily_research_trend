🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Evaluating Zone-Guided Front Extraction for Glacier Calving-Front Delineation in SAR Imagery `arXiv:2610.04066`
   - Compares deep learning approaches for finding glacier calving fronts in SAR images.
   - Tests whether zone-level semantic supervision beats direct front prediction.
   - Why it matters: Shows whether auxiliary supervision gives more reliable cryosphere monitoring from SAR.

2. Diffusion Meets Unrolling: Compressive SAR Image Reconstruction `arXiv:2610.06107`
   - Combines a denoising diffusion prior with model-driven unrolled refinement.
   - Reconstructs SAR images from undersampled measurements.
   - Why it matters: Fewer measurements per SAR image means lower acquisition and downlink cost.

3. REACT: Physically and Chemically Consistent Reconstruction of Marine Active Tracers `arXiv:2610.03888`
   - Carbon-first framework that reconstructs sea surface pH.
   - Keeps carbonate equilibrium constraints satisfied during reconstruction.
   - Why it matters: Physics-consistent ocean carbon products matter for carbon budget work.

4. TIRMamba: Thermal Infrared Image Super-Resolution `arXiv:2610.05182`
   - Uses a network of about 0.9M parameters for thermal IR super-resolution.
   - Matches competitive methods with 29–40x fewer parameters.
   - Why it matters: A lightweight model could sharpen coarse thermal bands such as Landsat TIRS.
