🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. ⭐ Evaluating AlphaEarth Embeddings for Crop Classification `arXiv:2610.36857`
   - Compares AlphaEarth embeddings with traditional features for crop separability and temporal dynamics.
   - Class separation is better, but transfer from the US to India is weaker.
   - Why it matters: Shows where EO foundation embeddings break down across regions, a key test for a Landsat FM.
2. From Sentinel-2 to 1-m Imagery: ×10 Cross-Sensor Super-Resolution `arXiv:2610.34742`
   - A visual autoregressive model upsamples Sentinel-2 tenfold to match 1-m commercial imagery.
   - It reports the best scores on the test set.
   - Why it matters: Free 10 m data could approach commercial-grade detail.
3. Quantized Edge Inference of a Self-Supervised SAR-Optical Encoder `arXiv:2610.37056`
   - INT8 quantization cuts CPU latency and energy on commodity x86 hardware.
   - Accuracy is unchanged.
   - Why it matters: Makes large multimodal EO encoders practical to deploy.
4. Assessing BIOMASS Radiometric Stability using Permanent Scatterers `arXiv:2610.37400`
   - Uses stable radar scatterers to estimate gain drift in ESA's P-band BIOMASS SAR.
   - Case studies are in Nairobi and Córdoba.
   - Why it matters: Radiometric stability underpins the mission's forest biomass and carbon estimates.
