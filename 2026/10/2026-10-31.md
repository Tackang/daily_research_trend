📡 [2026-10-31] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. From Next Day Wildfire to Next Days Wildfire: Multi-Continent Active-Fire Spread Dataset `arXiv:2610.38086`
   - AFSeq: multimodal satellite time series of 1,194 fire events across 8 countries.
   - Adds UTAE-AR, an autoregressive model for multi-day spread forecasting.
   - Why it matters: Multi-day, multi-continent benchmarks move fire forecasting beyond single-region, next-day prediction.

2. Neighbourhood shortcuts in building damage assessment from VHR satellite imagery `arXiv:2610.38162`
   - Damage models often rely on neighbouring buildings' damage, not the building itself.
   - Proposes a building-evidence plug-in that reduces this reliance.
   - Why it matters: Benchmark scores may overstate real per-building accuracy.

3. UnMixMamba: Unmixing-Guided State Space Subpixel Mapping of Crops from MODIS Time Series `arXiv:2610.38507`
   - Maps crop types from 250 m MODIS time series to field-scale labels with a Mamba model.
   - Spectral unmixing guides the subpixel mapping.
   - Why it matters: Coarse, long time series become usable for fine-scale crop mapping, a mixed-pixel problem Landsat-scale work also faces.

4. Sparse tensor networks for individual tree diameter estimation in mixed forests `arXiv:2610.37784`
   - Estimates tree diameter from UAV laser scanning point clouds.
   - Aimed at forest inventory in mixed stands.
   - Why it matters: Tree-level ground truth could help calibrate satellite biomass and carbon products.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
5. SciGame `arXiv:2610.37919`
   - Turns papers into original and counterfactual environments.
   - Tests whether LLM agents can induce scientific laws.
   - Why it matters: Counterfactual settings separate real discovery from recalled knowledge.

6. VerifiedLab `arXiv:2610.37923`
   - Multi-agent harness that checks research records field by field.
   - Tested on perovskite solar cell data.
   - Why it matters: Verification is a bottleneck for trustworthy automated science.

7. OpenFrontiers `arXiv:2610.37739`
   - Corpus of open research problems plus a formally graded benchmark of recently resolved math problems.
   - Measures whether LLMs can rediscover results.
   - Why it matters: Gives a cleaner test of discovery ability than static benchmarks.

8. Are Seismic Foundation Models More Than Glorified Feature Extractors? `arXiv:2610.38003`
   - Tests whether seismic waveform foundation models learn more than generic features.
   - Argues the training target is the bottleneck.
   - Why it matters: A caution for geoscience foundation models, including EO ones, about pretraining objectives.

💡 One-line comment: Today's EO papers stress rigorous evaluation (shortcut diagnosis, fire benchmarks), and the AI-for-science papers focus on benchmarks for verifying and testing discovery agents.
