🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Transferability and operational reliability of a Prithvi crop classification foundation model across three continents `arXiv:2610.08810`
   - Tests a geospatial foundation model on 37 events in 12 countries. Mean accuracy falls from 0.65 (US) to 0.40 (Europe).
   - Why it matters: Phenological misalignment, not just spatial shift, limits transfer, so EO foundation models need season-aware training.

2. Anaximander: Interactively Running Geospatial Deep Learning Models on Any Compute Backend `arXiv:2610.09085`
   - Interface that unifies model source and compute location (local CPU to cloud) for satellite imagery in GIS. Supports SAM 3 for field delineation and crop monitoring.
   - Why it matters: Lowers the barrier to applying EO models operationally (ECCV 2026 TerraBytes II workshop).

3. Fast Whittle ML Parametric Spectrum Estimation for Weather-Radar Doppler Moments `arXiv:2610.09161`
   - Parametric spectrum estimation that reduces finite-sample bias for short coherent processing intervals.
   - Why it matters: Gives more accurate radar moments from short dwells, which fast-scanning weather radar needs.

4. Beyond the Doppler Dilemma: Weather Radar Doppler Velocity Spectrum Reconstruction from Sparse Aperiodic Sweeps `arXiv:2610.09172`
   - Fast Doppler counter-aliasing for short-dwell measurements, using aperiodic sampling.
   - Why it matters: Resolves the velocity-ambiguity trade-off in rapid-update radar.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
5. Can AI Agents Make Open-Ended Scientific Discovery? Evidence from Station `arXiv:2610.08923`
   - Agents work on open-ended science tasks with no results or web access. They rediscover 62.7% of paper criteria on average.
   - Why it matters: Gives a concrete benchmark for autonomous scientific discovery.

6. Agent Plasticity: Measuring Self-Improvement Through Experience `arXiv:2610.08902`
   - Measures how well agents turn experience into gains on held-out tasks, via reusable artifacts.
   - Why it matters: Separates real self-improvement from memorization, which matters for science agents.

7. Sequential Probabilistic Uncertainty Estimation for Parallel Multi-Agent Reasoning (SAUCE) `arXiv:2610.08901`
   - Training-free estimator that aggregates agreement signals across reasoning rounds.
   - Why it matters: Cheap confidence estimates make multi-agent outputs easier to trust.

8. Humanity's Sixth Sense: Benchmarking Intuitive Visual Reasoning in Multimodal Models `arXiv:2610.08966`
   - Frontier models reach 53.6% versus 93.1% for humans on implicit visual reasoning.
   - Why it matters: Shows a large remaining gap in multimodal perception.
