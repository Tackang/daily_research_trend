🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Data-Driven Vision-Language Models for Remote Sensing: A Survey `arXiv:2610.31304`
   - Argues RS vision is shifting from task-specific architectures to adapting general VLMs with domain data.
   - Reviews RS VLM datasets and gives a guide to dataset building, fine-tuning, and evaluation.
   - Why it matters: Dataset quality is now the main lever for RS foundation-model performance.

2. In-Orbit Computing for Remote Sensing: Task-Oriented Processing and Transmission `arXiv:2610.30693`
   - SSGC-RS adapts onboard processing depth to the task and to compute and link budgets. Its three modes are onboard inference, compressed semantic downlink, and split inference.
   - Land-cover recognition is the case study.
   - Why it matters: It shows a way past the raw-data downlink bottleneck for EO pipelines.

3. Accelerating Wildfire Hazard Mapping Across Canada with an AI Surrogate Modelling Framework `arXiv:2610.30170`
   - A multi-task deep surrogate approximates BurnP3+ simulations from fuel, terrain, ignition, and weather inputs.
   - Reports an 806x CPU speedup while preserving downstream hazard classes.
   - Why it matters: Surrogates make national-scale, frequent hazard mapping feasible.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
4. A Topology-Guided GeoAI Agentic Framework for Urban Flood Screening `arXiv:2610.30660`
   - A rule-based controller runs terrain analysis, and the LLM is limited to logged decision points with human checkpoints.
   - In a College Park case study it narrowed 278 depressions to 70 basins (61–83% agreement with flow routing in other areas).
   - Why it matters: It is a template for auditable geospatial agents.

5. Breaking the high-degree barrier in Lehmer's problem with a human-AI discovery loop `arXiv:2610.30277`
   - A human-AI loop with solvers and distributed search yields a five-parameter Generalized Boyd algorithm.
   - It improves six published Mahler-measure values for even degrees up to 2000.
   - Why it matters: A concrete case of AI-assisted discovery producing verifiable new results.

6. From Prose to Properties: Testing Research Claims with ARATest for Agent-Native Science `arXiv:2610.30495`
   - Turns prose claims into executable property tests. Of 243 claims it tested 54, and 8 were violated (2 confirmed as real errors).
   - It detected all 553 seeded faults with no false alarms on 19 valid controls.
   - Why it matters: It gives automated science a way to check its own claims.

7. RoleFlow: Self-Evolving Multi-Role Agentic Collaboration via Structural Adaptation `arXiv:2610.30429`
   - Multi-agent structure evolves through small edits and new roles, trained with SECPO counterfactual credit assignment.
   - Beats the strongest baseline by 4.7 points on average across 7 benchmarks.
   - Why it matters: Adaptive agent topologies are replacing hand-designed workflows.
