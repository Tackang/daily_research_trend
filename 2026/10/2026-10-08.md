🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. CETUS: How Far Do Representations Trained on Earth Transfer to Cassini SAR of Titan? `arXiv:2610.07576`
   - Compares DINOv2, DOFA and CROMA encoders against classical features for Titan terrain classification. Pretrained encoders win on macro F1, but fine-tuning helps DINOv2 and hurts DOFA.
   - Why it matters: It shows how well EO foundation models (DOFA, CROMA) transfer to a new sensor domain, which bears on the cross-sensor generalization of Landsat models.

2. Skillful Data-Driven Subseasonal Soil Moisture Forecasting `arXiv:2610.07060`
   - A dual-attention Vision Transformer forecasts European soil moisture and beats ECMWF operational baselines at all lead times. Residual learning and physical units, rather than standardized anomalies, are the key choices.
   - Why it matters: It shows that problem formulation matters as much as architecture for land-surface forecasting, though flash-drought onset is still unsolved.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
3. EPOCH: Reliable Discovery through Evidence-Governed Search `arXiv:2610.06986`
   - A research-agent framework with task contracts, typed memory, active falsification and independent replay. It scores 0.65 on AlgoTune against a 0.53 baseline.
   - Why it matters: It targets validating agent-made discoveries rather than only optimizing evaluator feedback, which is a key weakness of AI-scientist systems.

4. Principles that Guide, Actions that Inform: Agent Evolution via Knowledge Abstraction (SAGA) `arXiv:2610.06964`
   - LLM agents turn their experience into episodes, procedures and general principles, without any fine-tuning. This improves results on ScienceWorld and ALFWorld.
   - Why it matters: It offers a route to agents that keep improving at science tasks while using closed-source LLMs.

5. MemCo: Memory-Centric Collaboration for Generalizing LLM Agents to Unseen Environments `arXiv:2610.07376`
   - Agents keep local, environment-specific memory and a global memory of transferable workflows, routed by decision phase. This raises success rates and cuts wasteful exploration.
   - Why it matters: Multi-agent memory sharing that transfers to unseen environments is a prerequisite for general-purpose science agents.
