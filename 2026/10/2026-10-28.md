📡 [2026-10-28] Daily Research Briefing

🔬 Remote Sensing & Earth Observation
1. Accuracy and efficiency of compact foundation models for surface-water mapping during floods `arXiv:2610.34904`
   - Tests Prithvi-EO-2.0-Tiny on 446 hand-labeled Sentinel-2 flood chips. A fine-tuned U-Net scored slightly higher, but Prithvi ran faster on both GPU and CPU.
   - Why it matters: Small EO foundation models can approach task-specific accuracy at far lower compute, a useful reference for a Landsat foundation model.

2. Cross-Scale Sparse Attention for Referring Segmentation in Remote Sensing (CSSAM) `arXiv:2610.33551`
   - Segments remote sensing regions from natural-language descriptions using cross-scale fusion and sparse attention. Introduces the M³RRSIS-D dataset for multi-target, multi-hop references.
   - Why it matters: Language-driven querying of imagery is moving toward complex, multi-step references.

3. Component Pooling Trained Across Orientations for Few-Shot Remote Sensing Scene Classification (RoCoP) `arXiv:2610.33518`
   - A parameter-free pooling network groups object components and trains on randomly rotated views. It improves few-shot results on NWPU-RESISC45, AID and UC Merced.
   - Why it matters: Rotation robustness helps in label-scarce settings.

4. How Much Sensing is Enough? Landslide Detection from Sparse and Noisy 3D Observations `arXiv:2610.34869`
   - Builds a dataset from OpenTopography DEMs and NASA SMAP soil moisture, then runs a CNN + XGBoost pipeline. With 20% of observations it reaches 80% accuracy and 0.868 ROC-AUC.
   - Why it matters: It quantifies how little data hazard monitoring needs.

5. FastSAR: High-Throughput Open-Source SAR Image Formation on CPUs, GPUs and TPUs `arXiv:2610.33932`
   - Open-source SAR image formation library using factorized and exact backprojection, plus polar format. Forms the Panama image in 11.2 s on a 16-vCPU instance.
   - Why it matters: Fast open SAR processing lowers the barrier to large-scale SAR work.

🤖 AI & Foundation Models
6. LegoSR: A Unified Framework for Self-improving Agent-Guided Scientific Discovery `arXiv:2610.33465`
   - Unifies seven equation-discovery solvers under one representation. An agent selects solvers and adapts the search.
   - Why it matters: Agents orchestrating existing discovery tools is a practical path to automated science.

7. BenchBench-Cloning: Evaluating Molecular Cloning Design and Protocol Development `arXiv:2610.33355`
   - A 65-task benchmark on LLMs designing cloning constructs and writing executable lab protocols. The best system scores 30.6%.
   - Why it matters: It shows how far lab-automation agents still are from reliable use.

8. AdaPRA: Process Reward Agents with Adaptive Rubrics for Data Science `arXiv:2610.33245`
   - A persistent data-science agent harness with rubric-guided process supervision. It improves leaderboard percentiles on DSPredict.
   - Why it matters: Process-level rewards make long-horizon science agents more dependable.

💡 One-line comment: Compact EO foundation models and agent-driven science tools are both getting measured on efficiency and reliability, not just raw accuracy.
